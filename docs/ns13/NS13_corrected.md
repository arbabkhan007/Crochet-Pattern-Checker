# Christmas Ornament Bundle

> **US + UK equivalent  ·  Beginner  ·  15-40 min per piece, excluding blocking**  —  Design Code NS-13

Three quick decorations from leftover yarn: a stuffed round bauble, a flat-blocked five-point star, and a crisp six-arm snowflake. Allow 15-40 minutes per piece, plus overnight blocking and drying.

**FINISHED SIZE**  Bauble about 2.6 cm (1 in) across and 2.5 cm tall - 24 stitches round at the widest point. Star about 6 cm (2.4 in) point to point - 35 stitches round plus the five treble points. Snowflake about 6 cm (2.4 in) across - a 12-stitch ring plus six chain-and-treble arms. All three are arithmetic at 3.4 mm per stitch and 3.0 mm per round (3.5 mm hook, DK), not measurements of a finished sample: work one piece first and measure it.

## Materials

- Yarn: DK / light worsted (#3) cotton or acrylic - white, red, gold, green, silver, blue. The set is 201 worked stitches (144 bauble + 45 star + 12 snowflake) plus the chain loops and slip stitches, about 6 m of yarn in total, so scraps are enough: roughly 3 m for the bauble and 1.5 m each for the star and snowflake. Cotton gives the crispest snowflake; acrylic is fine too.

- Hook: 3.5 mm (US E/4). The finished sizes above assume this hook; a larger hook makes the same stitch counts larger, not wrong.

- Optional: about 1 g of fiber fill for the bauble - a small pinch is all a 2.6 cm ball will take; metallic yarn for the stripe in step 2 of Finishing.

- Notions: tapestry needle, scissors, pin board or mat for blocking the star and snowflake.

## Gauge & size

Gauge: about 29 sc and about 33 rounds to 10 cm (4 in), over single crochet worked in the round. That spacing - 3.4 mm per stitch and 3.0 mm per round - is the basis of the finished sizes: 2.6 cm across the bauble, 6 cm point to point for the star, 6 cm across the snowflake. Work tightly enough that the bauble holds its fill and the star blocks flat. The figures are calculated from the stitch counts rather than measured from a sample, so measure your first piece and change hook if you want the set larger or smaller.

## Abbreviations (US + UK)

MR - magic ring  ·  inc - increase (2 sc in one st) / UK: increase (2 dc in one st)

ch - chain  ·  sc2tog = dc2tog - work 2 stitches together as one (a decrease)

sl st - slip stitch  ·  FO - fasten off

sc = dc - single crochet / double crochet  ·  st(s) - stitch(es)

hdc = half treble - half double crochet / half treble crochet  ·  R - round

dc = tr - double crochet / treble crochet  ·  Sts column - stitch count at the end of the round, in brackets

tr = dtr - treble crochet / double treble crochet

How to read this dual pattern: every round appears twice side by side - the US-terms instruction in the left column, the exact UK equivalent in the right column. Stitch counts are identical for both terminologies. Prose tips use US terms.

## Construction & techniques

The bauble is worked in continuous rounds (no joins) with the marker in the first stitch. The star and snowflake are worked in JOINED rounds / off-the-ring motifs: each round closes with a slip stitch. Read each section from its own heading down - they are three small standalone patterns.

1. **The magic ring** - Pull the tail tight after round one; if the center gaps, work the tail through the first round once more before weaving.

2. **Fan points (star)** - Each point is a fan of 6 worked stitches into ONE center stitch, with a ch-2 tip: (sc, hdc, dc, ch 2, dc, hdc, sc). The ch-2 tip is a space and is not counted as a worked stitch - it resembles a picot but is not one, because the tip is never closed with a slip stitch. Per point you make 6 fan stitches plus 1 anchoring sl st, so the five-point round has 35 counted stitches (plus five ch-2 spaces). The symmetry comes from blocking, not tugging.

3. **Chain spaces (snowflake)** - The snowflake skeleton is 6 ch-5 loops worked into the 12-dc round, one loop per pair of spokes. The arms are worked into those chain spaces, never into individual chains.

4. **Blocking** - For cotton, wet or mist the piece, pin every point to shape and let it dry fully. Acrylic often does not hold a shape from pins alone; if the yarn label permits, steam-block cautiously with the steamer or iron held above the fabric, never touching it, and test a scrap first. Do not press or iron either piece.

## Instructions

### A. Round Christmas Bauble - stuffed ball

The sphere closes at the same 6-stitch crown it started from. Two-color option: change at R5.

| Rnd | US terms | UK equivalent | Sts | Note |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| R1 | 6 sc in MR | 6 dc in MR | (6) | - |

| R2 | inc in each st around | inc in each st around | (12) | - |

| R3 | [sc, inc] x 6 | [dc, inc] x 6 | (18) | - |

| R4 | [2 sc, inc] x 6 | [2 dc, inc] x 6 | (24) | - |

| R5 | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (24) | contrast color: change on the last pull-through of R4 |

| R6 | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (24) | last contrast round: change back on the last pull-through of the last stitch of R6 |

| R7 | [2 sc, sc2tog] x 6 | [2 dc, dc2tog] x 6 | (18) | - |

| R8 | [sc, sc2tog] x 6 | [dc, dc2tog] x 6 | (12) | start stuffing here |

| R9 | sc2tog x 6 | dc2tog x 6 | (6) | stuff firm, then close |

Finish: stuff through the R9 opening, a little at a time, until the sphere springs back when pressed instead of feeling rigid. A 2.6 cm ball holds only about 1 g, so add it in pinches; too much is what makes the shape lumpy. Then FO with a long tail, thread it through the front loops of the last 6 stitches, cinch the opening closed and weave in. (Never close first and stuff afterwards: a closed sphere cannot be filled.)

Hanging loop: join yarn at the crown, ch 18, sl st into the same joining place, FO and weave in. Do not hang the finished bauble where a child can reach the loop.

### B. Five-Point Star - flat, blocked

Each point uses exactly 2 of the 10 center stitches - one for the slip stitch that anchors it, one for the fan that fills it. Block before hanging.

| Rnd | US terms | UK equivalent | Sts | Note |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| R1 | 10 sc in MR, sl st to first sc to close | 10 dc in MR, sl st to first dc to close | (10) | center ring |

| R2 | [sl st in next st, (sc, hdc, dc, ch 2, dc, hdc, sc) in next st] x 5, sl st to first sl st, FO | [sl st in next st, (dc, half treble, tr, ch 2, tr, half treble, dc) in next st] x 5, sl st to first sl st, FO | (35) | 5 sl sts + 30 fan sts; ch-2 tips not counted |

Hanging loop: pick the top point, join yarn in its ch-2 tip, ch 18, sl st into the same ch-2 space.

The repeat uses all 10 center stitches exactly (2 per point). Each fan contains 6 worked stitches; with its anchoring sl st, each point contributes 7 counted stitches, for 35 total. The five ch-2 tip spaces are additional structure, not counted stitches.

### C. Six-Point Snowflake - open lace

Three short rounds. The ring is closed first so the arms hang on real chain spaces - keep the dc round snug, the ch-5 round loose.

| Rnd | US terms | UK equivalent | Sts | Note |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| R1 | ch 6, sl st in first ch to form a ring; ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in ring, sl st to top of ch-3 | ch 6, sl st in first ch to form a ring; ch 3 (counts as first tr), 11 tr in ring, sl st to top of ch-3 | (12) | 12 spokes |

| R2 | [ch 5, skip next st, sl st in next st] x 6, sl st to base of first ch-5 to close | [ch 5, skip next st, sl st in next st] x 6, sl st to base of first ch-5 to close | - | 6 ch-5 spaces |

| R3 | (sl st, ch 3, 3 tr, ch 3, sl st) in each of the 6 ch-5 spaces, sl st to first sl st, FO | (sl st, ch 3, 3 dtr, ch 3, sl st) in each of the 6 ch-5 spaces, sl st to first sl st, FO | (6 arms) | first sl st of each arm loose, last sl st snug |

Hanging loop: join yarn in the ch-3 tip of one arm, ch 18, sl st into the same tip.

Block flat using the blocking method above; let it cool if steamed and dry completely before hanging.

## Finishing & assembly

1. Work the three pieces in order: A bauble, B star, C snowflake.

2. Bauble only: stuff through the R9 opening until firm but springy, then thread the tail through the front loops of the last 6 stitches and cinch closed.

3. Optional metallic stripe (bauble): once the piece is closed and stuffed, work surface slip stitches in metallic yarn along the R6/R7 join. Insert the hook through the fabric just above an R6 stitch, pull up a loop of metallic yarn, then work one sl st into each R6 stitch around - 24 in all - so the stripe sits on the join. For the zigzag shown in the cover render, work a second line into R7, offset by 3 stitches. Do not try to work into the front loops of R6 once R7 is done: those loops are already used.

4. Block the star and snowflake flat and let them dry completely - overnight for cotton.

5. Add a hanging loop to each piece: ch 18, sl st into the same place, FO, weave in. Keep each loop no longer than its support needs, and never hang the ornaments where a child can reach a loop.

6. Weave in every remaining end in two directions - lace shows knots more than amigurumi does.

7. For gifting, thread each loop through a cardboard or felt backing card with the knot hidden underneath.

## Troubleshooting

- **Bauble looks like a lemon.** Too much stuffing added late. Stuff gradually from R8 and top up through the R9 opening before closing, then roll the sphere between your palms to shape it and keep the closing pinch tight.

- **Star will not lie flat.** It needs blocking, not more tugging. Wet-block cotton. For acrylic, pins alone may not set the shape; follow the yarn label and cautiously steam from above without touching or melting the fibers.

- **Snowflake arms lean one way, or the base bunches.** Both arm-end sl sts share the same ch-5 space, and tension is the whole trick. Work the first sl st of each arm loose enough that the base lies flat - if it is too tight, the arm twists out of the space - then work the last sl st snug so the arm stands upright.

## Care & storage

Wash only as the yarn label allows: cool water, a drop of mild soap, squeeze gently - never wring - then reshape and dry flat. Metallic thread can dull with washing, so spot-clean a striped bauble instead. Store the ornaments dry and flat or supported, without crushing the star or snowflake points. Reblock as described above, and never apply an iron directly to the fabric.
