# Benchmark consensus

Three files, three viewpoints. None of them is rewritten here.

- `docs/wrong_benchmarks.md` — original wrong patterns
- `docs/gemini_corrected.md` — Gemini Flash, unedited
- `docs/chatgpt_corrected.md` — ChatGPT, unedited

Consensus means the comparison below. It is not a fourth pattern. Where Gemini and ChatGPT disagree, both texts stay. Do not auto-merge them.

The checker is not a vote. It can confirm a round only when that round is written in its own dialect. It still cannot see every short-row, frill, or prose assembly claim in these notes.

## Agreed

These are the points both corrected viewpoints treat as real defects, with the same kind of repair.

| Pattern | Agreed defect | Agreed repair |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | 12 stitches jump to 24 | Insert an 18-stitch round, then return to 24 |
| Bear | Arm cinched into a sealed cap | Do not cinch. Flatten and sew |
| Bear | Legs and ears missing | Add both pieces before assembly |
| Bear | Safety eyes | Name them in the instructions |
| Wyvern | 5 stitches in every wing stitch | Reduce the edging. 70 from 14 is too full |
| Dragon | 3 leg stitches sewn to 4 torso stitches | Use equal counts |
| Dragon | Wing tab about twice the socket | Make the tab about the same width as the socket |
| Dragon | fpdc into a short sc row | Give the post a taller foundation |
| Dragon | Safety eyes never placed | Put the placement in the head section |
| Leviathan | Closed tentacle used as an open socket | Choose closed sewing or an open base. Not both |
| Leviathan | bpdc ridge facing the inside | Use a front-facing post, on a tall enough row |
| Leviathan | 5 arm stitches sewn to 3 body stitches | Use equal counts |
| Leviathan | 3.33 inch fin sewn flat to 1.69 inch body | Resize one side, or state that the fin is gathered |
| Chimera | 756 on 108 is not 21 times full | 756 / 108 = 7. Seven times full is still too dense |
| Chimera | Eyes on the free frill | Move the eyes to solid head fabric, before stuffing |
| Chimera | Closed tentacle and a live socket | Pick one construction |

## Disagreed

Do not pick a winner from this table. The two files are the record.

| Pattern | Gemini | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| Bear arms | Stop at 8 stitches and flatten to 4 | Decrease to 4, then flatten. Do not cinch |
| Bear ears | 6, then 9 | 6, then 12, then 18 |
| Bear legs | Stay at 12 | Decrease 12 to 9 |
| Bear eye spacing | 5 stitches | About 4 stitches |
| Wyvern join | Rewrite to 24, with 3 held stitches on each leg | Original 30 is valid once the chain bridge is worked |
| Wyvern wing | 35 stitches, 2.5 times full | 42 stitches, 3 times full, or 28 for a flatter edge |
| Dragon rejoin | 8 + 3 + 22 + 3 = 36 | Close all 34 positions, or mark 6 as skipped |
| Dragon round 15 | `(4 sc, dec)` 6 times to 30, from 36 | If 16 is required, start from 18 and end with 4 sc |
| Leviathan hub | Rebuild the join to 64, with skips and held stitches matched | The written clauses already make 48. Change 54 to 48 |
| Leviathan round 17 | `(6 sc, dec)` 8 times, 64 to 56 | `(6 sc, dec)` 6 times, 48 to 42 |
| Leviathan tentacle | Start with an open chain ring of 12 | Closed stuffed tentacle is allowed if it is only sewn on |
| Chimera round 15 | `(9 sc, dec)` 4 times, 44 to 40 | `(5 sc, dec)` 6 times, plus 2 sc, 44 to 38 |
| Chimera post | New hdc round, then fptr into that round | Keep the 38 count, and do not reach down two rounds if a short ridge is wanted |
| Chimera frill | Shell in every 3rd stitch, 150 stitches, about 1.6 times | 3-stitch group in every stitch, 324 stitches, 3 times |
| Chimera graft | 24 stitches to 24 stitches | 36 stitches to 36 stitches |
| Chimera head to torso | 24 to 24 | 26 to 26, or state a gather into 18 |
| Chimera socket | Skip 6 body stitches and hold 6 limb stitches | Only 3 body stitches were left, so match 3 to 3 unless the round is rewritten |

## What the repo should learn

- Keep all three files. Deleting a disagreement is not learning.
- A later consensus pattern is allowed only for a row in the agreed table.
- A row in the disagreed table stays unresolved until a person chooses one file, or writes a fourth file and says which source it follows.
- Patterns 3, 4, and 5 are still incomplete. Neither corrected file is a full pattern.
