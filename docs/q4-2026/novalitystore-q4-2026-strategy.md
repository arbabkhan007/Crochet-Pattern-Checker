# Step 1 — Shop Audit & Competitor Benchmark

## 1.1 The five structural problems, ranked by revenue impact

### 🔴 Problem 1 — Your spreadsheet prices are 5–10× the market. This is the single biggest leak.

Your Christmas Gift Tracker sells at **$11.99**. Here is the live competitive set for the identical product on Etsy right now:

| Ultimate Christmas Planner – Lifetime (610) | $6.00 |
| Christmas Gift Tracker Spreadsheet… (1.8k) | $3.99 |
| **Your Christmas Gift Tracker** | **$11.99** |

You are the most expensive option in the category by roughly **4×** against the volume leaders and **2×** against the most expensive credible competitor. Your product is genuinely *better* — 13 tabs and 2,500 formulas beats a 5-tab sheet — but Etsy search is sorted by relevance and buyers scan price first. A shopper comparing $0.99 to $11.99 does not open your listing to discover it has 13 tabs.

**This does not mean "cut to $0.99."** It means: (a) your $11.99 listing is fighting a fight it cannot win on its own, and (b) you are missing the entire $3–$7 mid-market, which is where the money actually is. See Step 4 for the ladder fix.

### 🔴 Problem 2 — 3.7★ from 3 reviews is a conversion killer at Q4 traffic levels

A 3.7 average is below the threshold where Etsy's star-seller-style trust signals operate, and it renders as an unflattering partial star in search results. With only 3 reviews the average is extremely volatile — **one more 3★ review drops you to 3.55; one 5★ lifts you to 4.03.**

At 11 sales in 4 months you are not generating the review velocity to outrun this. Fixing review *acquisition* is more urgent than any tag change, because it multiplies the conversion rate of every other fix you make.

### 🟠 Problem 3 — Both your hero listings are in the wrong Etsy category

- **Christmas Tree Skirt Pattern** (your highest-priced crochet item at $7.50) sits under **Craft Supplies & Tools**. It is a *pattern*. It should be under **Patterns & How To › Crochet Patterns**. As filed, it is invisible to buyers browsing the pattern taxonomy and is competing against yarn and hooks.
- **Christmas Gift Tracker** sits under **Paper & Party Supplies › Stationery**. That is a defensible but secondary home; your competitor set clusters in Templates/personal-finance.

Mis-categorisation removes you from category browse traffic entirely and weakens your relevance score for the browse-derived queries.

### 🟠 Problem 4 — A permanent 25%-off sale across all 36 listings has destroyed your price signal

Every single listing shows a struck-through price. When everything is always on sale:
- The "original price" stops being believed, so the discount stops persuading.
- You forfeit the **Black Friday / Cyber Monday spike**. Etsy explicitly notes sellers running discounts on *already-ranked* listings capture the surge; a shop that has been at 25% off since June has nothing left to announce on 27 November.
- You are leaving 25% margin on the table on items that would sell at full price.

**Fix:** End the blanket sale. Restore full price on your 8–10 strongest listings for 2–3 weeks to re-anchor, then run a *time-boxed* 20–25% Black Friday event on 27 Nov. The contrast is the marketing.

### 🟡 Problem 5 — 17 days of shop inactivity going into the ranking window

"Latest activity: Sep 20, 2026." Etsy's freshness and consistency signals reward regular activity, and the Q4 ranking window is *now*. Third-party Etsy timing analyses consistently cite **3–6 weeks for a listing to accumulate ranking signals** before peak search ([listifyai Q4 calendar](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-holiday-calendar-2026), [SellerApp](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/)). Christmas buyer search peaks ~**12 December**; working back 3–6 weeks puts your last safe publish date at **roughly 31 Oct – 17 Nov.**

You have about **three weeks of useful runway.** That is tight but workable — for *optimising existing* listings. Anything brand-new published after ~17 Nov is a December cold-start and should be treated as a January asset.
