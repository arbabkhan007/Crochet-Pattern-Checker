Each pattern is independent: restart stitch-count context at its boundary and at each new component. US/UK terminology is identified inside each pattern.

**Status:** internally consistent as written (see `verify.py` and the correction report). Corrections that change dimensions or fit are marked “confirm on a sample” inside the affected patterns; arithmetic correctness is not a substitute for one test-crochet of NS04, NS06, NS10, NS11, NS12, NS14 and NS16 before commercial release.

# Hamish the Highland Cow — NS01 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 14–15 cm (5.5–6 in) seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 12 rnds = 5 cm (2 in) in spiral single crochet
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Ginger / Caramel):** Worsted (#4), ~35 g (includes fringe)
  * **Yarn B (Oat Cream):** Worsted (#4), ~12 g (muzzle, horns, inner ears, belly patch)
  * **Yarn C (Dark Chocolate):** Worsted (#4), ~5 g (hooves, nostril embroidery)
  * **Yarn D (Rust — optional):** Worsted (#4), ~6 g (scarf)
  * **Eyes:** Two 12 mm black safety eyes (or black embroidery floss)
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~35 g), stitch marker, tapestry needle, pins, scissors

> **Safety:** Safety eyes and sewn/knotted parts can detach. Lock washers firmly from inside before stuffing, sew every seam twice, weave ends at least 5 cm, and check all parts by hand. Not suitable for children under 3 unless the finished item meets the toy-safety requirements of your market; embroidered eyes remove the plastic-eye hazard only.

### Abbreviations (US)
**MR:** magic ring · **ch:** chain · **sl st:** slip stitch · **sc:** single crochet · **hdc:** half double crochet · **inc:** 2 sc in same st · **dec:** invisible decrease · **BLO:** back loop only · **FO:** fasten off

---

### 1. Head (Yarn A)
Worked top-down in a continuous spiral.
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8:** [6 sc, inc] × 6 (48)
* **R9–R15 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (48)
* *Insert 12 mm safety eyes between **R10 and R11**, 7 stitches apart centre-to-centre. Lock washers firmly inside.*
* **R16:** [6 sc, dec] × 6 (42)
* **R17:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36)
* **R18:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R19:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* *Stuff head firmly. FO after R19 with a 45 cm sewing tail. Leave the 24-stitch neck opening unclosed.*

### 2. Muzzle (Yarn B)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5–R6 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* **R7:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* *FO with a 35 cm sewing tail. Embroider two diagonal nostrils (Yarn C) on the upper front and secure tails inside. Lightly stuff and flatten into a wide horizontal oval; sew centred below the eyes over **Head R13–R16**, following the curve of the head (the visible face is about 8 sts wide). Check placement against your sample before sewing permanently.*

### 3. Body (Yarn A)
Worked bottom-up in a continuous spiral.
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8:** [6 sc, inc] × 6 (48)
* **R9:** BLO sc in each st around (48) *(flat sitting base edge)*
* **R10–R16 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (48)
* **R17:** [6 sc, dec] × 6 (42)
* **R18:** sc in each st around (42)
* **R19:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36)
* **R20:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R21:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* *Stuff firmly, packing the flat base so it sits level. FO with a 45 cm tail; leave the 24-stitch neck open.*

### 4. Belly Patch (Yarn B — Optional)
* **Foundation:** Ch 6.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 3 sc, 3 sc in last ch; working along opposite side of chain: 3 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (12)
* **R2:** inc, 3 sc, inc × 3, 3 sc, inc × 2 (18)
* **R3:** sc, inc, 3 sc, [sc, inc] × 3, 3 sc, [sc, inc] × 2, sl st to first sc (24)
* *FO with a 30 cm sewing tail. Sew vertically, long axis upright, to the centre front of the body over **R10–R17** so the top of the patch stops at the first decrease round.*

### 5. Legs (Start with Yarn C, change to Yarn A)
Choose **Option A** (4 splayed legs: make 4 Standard Legs) or **Option B** (upright sit: 2 Standard Back Legs + 2 Shortened Front Legs).

#### Standard Legs
* **R1 (Yarn C):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** BLO sc in each st around (18) *(flat hoof sole)*
* **R5:** sc in each st around (18)
* **R6:** sc in each st around; change to **Yarn A** on the last pull-through (18)
* **R7 (Yarn A):** BLO sc in each st around (18) *(clean colour transition & hoof ridge)*
* **R8:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) *(stuff hoof firmly)*
* **R9–R13 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 3 (9) *(stuff upper leg lightly)*
* **R15–R16 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (9)
* *FO with a 35 cm sewing tail; leave 9-st top open.*

#### Shortened Front Legs (Option B only)
* Work **R1–R8** of Standard Legs (12).
* **R9–R10 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* *Stuff hoof firmly and R9–R10 lightly. FO with a 35 cm tail; leave 12-st top open.*

### 6. Flat Layered Ears (Make 2 Outer in Yarn A + 2 Inner in Yarn B)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* *Inner Ear (Yarn B): FO and weave in ends. Outer Ear (Yarn A): do not FO. Mark the first stitch of each layer, place one Inner and one Outer wrong sides together (cream facing front) with markers aligned, and work **18 sc in Yarn A** through corresponding stitches of both layers around the perimeter. FO with a 30 cm tail. Pinch 3 bottom stitches together, tack closed to cup the ear, and sew to **Head R8–R10** behind and outside the eyes.*

### 7. Horns (Yarn B — Make 2)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (6)
* **R5:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
* **R6–R7 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (8)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Stuff tip lightly. For a gentle upward curve, make one tiny hidden stitch pinching R4 and R5 on the inner side. Sew all 8 base stitches to **Head R6–R8** just above and forward of the ears.*

### 8. Tail & Fringe (Yarn A)
* **Tail:** Cut 6 strands of Yarn A (22 cm each). Attach with lark's-head knots into 2 adjacent stitches at the centre back on **Body R10**. Braid the 12 hanging ends for 4 cm, tie an overhand knot, trim tassel to 1.5 cm.
* **Fringe:** Cut 36–40 strands of Yarn A (14 cm each). Attach with lark's-head knots in a horseshoe across the forehead on **Head R6, R7 and R8** between the horns and ears. Trim into a V-fringe just above the muzzle.

### 9. Scarf (Yarn D — Optional)
* **Foundation:** Ch 92.
* **Row 1:** Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across; ch 2 (does not count as st), turn (90)
* **Row 2:** Hdc in each st across; FO and weave in ends (90)
* *Try the scarf on the finished cow; add or remove rows if you want a longer drape.*

### 10. Assembly
1. Align the 24 open stitches of **Head R19** with the 24 open stitches of **Body R21**, centring the face forward. Whipstitch through all 24 matched stitch pairs twice around (use the head tail for one pass and the body tail for the second), topping up neck stuffing before closing the final 3 pairs.
2. **Front Legs:** Pin centred over **Body R9–R11** on the front chest, leaving 8 body stitches between their inner edges. Angle forward so the hooves rest flat; sew around the open rim twice, catching every rim stitch.
3. **Back Legs:** Pin over **Body R9–R11** on the rear flanks, angled outward so the hooves project past the front legs on the table. Sew around the open rim twice. Check the cow sits level before final tightening.

# Kawaii Halloween Mini Set (Boo, Pip & Bramble) — NS02 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy–Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Each character approx. 4.5–5.0 cm (1.8–2.0 in) tall at stated gauge
* **Gauge:** 14 sc × 15 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 2.5 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **DK (#3) Cotton Yarn:** White (~8 g), Orange (~8 g), Charcoal/Black (~10 g), Purple (~4 g), Pale Pink (~2 g), Leaf Green (~2 g), Brown (~2 g)
  * **Eyes:** Three pairs of 6 mm black safety eyes (plus black & white embroidery floss)
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~15 g total), stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** These are miniatures with small plastic eyes and sewn parts — choking hazards. Lock washers firmly, sew parts twice, and do not offer to children under 3 unless the finished items meet your market's toy-safety requirements. Embroidered eyes are the safer option for young children.

### Character 1: Boo the Ghost

#### Ghost Body (White)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5–R10 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* *Insert 6 mm safety eyes between **R6 and R7**, 4 stitches apart. Embroider a tiny black mouth between the eyes and pink blush bars below.*
* **R11 (Frill — FLO):** Working in the **front loops only** of R10's stitches (the 24 back loops stay free inside for the base): `[(sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc) in next st, sl st in next st] × 12` (12 scallops). FO and weave in ends.

#### Flat Base (White)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Lightly stuff the ghost dome (~3 g), place the R4 disc against the inside of the skirt, and whipstitch its 24 stitches to the 24 unworked back loops of R10.*

#### Arms (White — Make 2)
* **R1:** 5 sc in MR (5)
* **R2–R3 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (5)
* *Do not stuff. Flatten the opening with **3 stitches on the top layer and 2 on the bottom layer**, so the 5th (unpaired) stitch sits at one fold. Work **2 sc through the two matched pairs**, then work **1 sl st through the unpaired fold stitch and the edge of the first pair** to close the fold. FO with a 15 cm tail and sew to the sides of the body at **R7–R8**, catching the fold stitch in the seam.*

#### Mini Witch Hat (Purple, with Orange Band)

* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
* **R4:** [3 sc, inc] × 2; change to **Orange** on last pull-through (10)
* **R5 (Orange):** sc in each st around; change to **Purple** on last pull-through (10)
* **R6 (Purple — FLO):** [sc, inc] × 5 (15)
* **R7:** [2 sc, inc] × 5, sl st to first sc (20)

# Axel the Axolotl — NS03 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy–Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 10.5–11.5 cm (4.25–4.5 in) seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 11.5 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Pastel Pink):** Worsted (#4), ~25 g (head, body, tail, limbs)
  * **Yarn B (Dark Pink / Magenta):** Worsted (#4), ~10 g (gill stalks and tail fin)
  * **Eyes:** Two 9 mm black safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~20 g), 10 locking stitch markers, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** Lock safety-eye washers firmly inside before stuffing and sew all parts twice. Safety eyes are a choking hazard if detached; not for children under 3 unless the finished item meets your market's toy-safety requirements.

### 1. Head, Body & Tail (Yarn A — one piece, top-down)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7–R12 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* *Insert 9 mm safety eyes between **R9 and R10**, 7 stitches apart. Embroider a wide smile between and slightly below the eyes.*
* **R13:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R14:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R15 (Neck):** [4 sc, dec] × 4 (20) *(stuff head firmly)*
* **R16:** sc in each st around (20)
* **R17:** [4 sc, inc] × 4 (24)
* **R18:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R19–R24 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (30)
* *Stuff neck and upper torso firmly.*
* **R25:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R26:** sc in each st around (24)
* **R27:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* **R28–R29 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (18)
* *Stuff R25–R29 **very lightly** so the tail can hinge backward. Place a locking marker in the centre-back (dorsal) stitch of **each round R29–R38** (10 markers).*
* **R30:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R31–R33 (3 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R34:** [2 sc, dec] × 3 (9)
* **R35–R36 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R37:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6)
* **R38:** sc in each st around (6)
* **Vertical tail-tip closure:** Do not stuff the tip. Fold the 6-stitch opening side-to-side (3 sts against 3 sts) so the fold runs vertically (spine to belly), with the R38 dorsal marker at the top corner. Ch 1 and work **3 sc through both layers** from top (dorsal) to bottom (ventral). FO and weave in tail.

### 2. Gill Stalks (Yarn B — Make 6: 3 per side)
* **Foundation:** Ch 7.
* **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook: [sc in next ch, (hdc, ch 1, hdc) in next ch] × 2, sc in last 2 ch (8)
* *FO with a 20 cm sewing tail. Arrange 3 stalks in a fan on each side of the head across **Head R7–R12** (about 4 stitches behind the eyes) and sew securely.*

### 3. Limbs (Yarn A — Make 4)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9)
* **R3–R6 (4 rnds):** sc in each st around (9)
* *Stuff the paw tip lightly. Flatten the opening with **one stitch at a side fold and 4 matched pairs**; work **4 sc through the four pairs**. When sewing the limb on, catch the fold stitch in the first seam stitch so no gap remains. FO with a 20 cm tail. Sew 2 arms to the sides of the body at **R17–R18** angled forward, and 2 legs to the lower front/sides at **R24–R25** so Axel sits upright.*

### 4. Scalloped Tail Fin (Yarn B)
Hold Axel with the tail tip pointing toward you.
* Join Yarn B with a sl st at the **top (dorsal) corner** of the 3-sc tail-tip seam.
* Working up the centre-back along the 10 marked dorsal stitches, from R38 toward R29:
  `[skip 1 marker, 5 hdc into next marker's stitch, skip 1 marker, sl st into next marker's stitch] × 2, 5 hdc into the 9th marker's stitch, sl st into the 10th marker's stitch (R29)` (3 shell fans; all 10 markers used).
* Continue without cutting yarn: work around the 3-sc tail-tip edge, then along the **bottom (ventral) centreline**, working **1 sc into the centre stitch of each round R38, R37, R36, R35, R34 and R33** (6 sc), ending with a sl st into the R33 centre stitch. FO and weave in ends.

# Coco the Capybara — NS04 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate (join-as-you-go legs)
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 9.5–10 cm (3.75–4 in) tall seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 11.5 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.0 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Warm Cinnamon / Camel Brown):** Worsted (#4), ~30 g
  * **Yarn B (Dark Espresso Brown):** Worsted (#4), ~6 g (muzzle, ears, foot tips)
  * **Yarn C (Orange & Leaf Green — optional):** Scrap (~3 g) for mini tangerine
  * **Eyes:** Two 8 mm black safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~25 g), stitch markers, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** Lock safety-eye washers firmly inside before the head is closed and sew all parts twice. Safety eyes are a choking hazard if detached; not for children under 3 unless the finished item meets your market's toy-safety requirements.

> **Corrections in this version:** leg tops now close to an exact 3-stitch strip (decrease to 6, then flatten), and the R4/R5 join map was rebuilt around marked centre lines so the front legs no longer overlap a back leg. Follow the stitch-position map exactly.

---

### 1. Back Legs (Make 2 — before the Body)
* **R1 (Yarn B):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3; change to **Yarn A** on last pull-through (9)
* **R3–R7 (5 rnds — Yarn A):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R8:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6) *(stuff paw lightly before this round)*
* **R9:** Flatten the 6-st opening and work **3 sc through both layers** (3). FO and weave in the short end. Set aside for Body R4.

### 2. Front Legs (Make 2 — before the Body)
* **R1 (Yarn B):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3; change to **Yarn A** on last pull-through (9)
* **R3–R8 (6 rnds — Yarn A):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R9:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6) *(stuff lower half lightly before this round)*
* **R10:** Flatten the 6-st opening and work **3 sc through both layers** (3). FO. Set aside for Body R5.

### 3. Body & Head (Yarn A — bottom-up, one piece)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)

### Abbreviations
**ch:** chain · **sl st:** slip stitch · **sc:** single crochet · **BLO:** back loop only · **sc2tog:** decrease through both loops (turned heel rows) · **dec:** invisible decrease (circular RS foot/toe rounds) · **RS/WS:** right/wrong side · **FO:** fasten off

### 1. Ridged Cuff & Leg (joined rounds, RS facing)
Ch 1 never counts; work the first sc into the same stitch as the join and end each round with sl st to first sc.

* **R1 (CC):** Ch 1, sc in each ch around, sl st to first sc (20)
* **R2–R4 (CC):** Ch 1, BLO sc in each st around, sl st to first sc; change to **MC** at end of R4 (20)
* **R5–R10 (MC):** Ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to first sc (20)
* *For an AC heel, change to **AC** on the R10 joining sl st. Do not turn.*

### 2. Heel Flap & Turn (turned rows over sts 1–10; sts 11–20 wait as instep)
Use **sc2tog through both loops** on Rows 7–9.
* **Row 1 (RS):** Ch 1, sc in same st as join and next 9 sts; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 2 (WS):** BLO sc in each st across; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 3 (RS):** Sc in each st across; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 4 (WS):** BLO sc in each st across; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 5 (RS):** Sc in each st across; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 6 (WS):** BLO sc in each st across; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 7 (RS):** Sc in first 6 sts, sc2tog over next 2; **leave last 2 sts unworked**, ch 1, turn (7)
* **Row 8 (WS):** Sc in first 3 sts, sc2tog over next 2; **leave last 2 sts unworked**, ch 1, turn (4) *(2 unworked sts now on each side — symmetrical)*
* **Row 9 (RS):** Sc in first 2 sts, sc2tog over last 2; ch 1, turn (3)
* **Row 10 (WS):** Sc in each of the 3 heel-centre sts, changing to **MC** on the last pull-through; **ch 1, TURN — RS (outside) now faces you** (3). FO AC if used.

### 3. Foot Pickup (joined rounds, RS facing)
* **Foot R1:** (1) 1 sc in each of the 3 Row-10 heel-centre sts (mark the first as round start); (2) **5 sc evenly up the left heel edge** — pick up through the 2 unworked Row-7 step stitches and 3 evenly spaced row-ends of Rows 1–6; (3) 1 sc in each of the 10 waiting instep sts (11–20); (4) **5 sc evenly down the right heel edge**, through the 2 unworked Row-8 step stitches and 3 evenly spaced row-ends. Sl st to first sc (**3 + 5 + 10 + 5 = 23 sts**).
  *First-stocking check: this is a sparse pickup. If holes show at the heel corners, make your first stocking a test piece and note where you added a pickup; keep the total at 23 by working one side pickup into a step stitch and row-end together.*
* **Foot R2 (evening round):** Ch 1, **7 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec**, sl st to first sc (**20**) *(consumes 23: 17 plain sc + 3 decreases)*
* **Foot R3–R7 (MC):** Ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to first sc (20)
* *Change to AC on the Foot R7 join for a contrast toe.*

### 4. Toe (joined rounds, RS facing)
* **Toe R1:** Ch 1, [3 sc, dec] × 4, sl st to first sc (16)
* **Toe R2:** Ch 1, [2 sc, dec] × 4, sl st to first sc (12)
* **Toe R3:** Ch 1, [sc, dec] × 4, sl st to first sc (8)
* **Toe R4:** Ch 1, dec × 4, sl st to first st (4)
* *FO with a 15 cm tail, thread through front loops of the 4 sts, pull tight, weave inside.*

### 5. Hanging Loop
The heel centre falls **between sts 5 and 6** of the heel flap. Join AC or CC under both loops of those two centre stitches at the back of **Cuff R4**, ch 12, sl st back into the same two-stitch point, FO, and knot/weave both tails inside the cuff. Thread the 24 stockings onto the support cord with a stopper knot beside each loop.

2. **Back Legs:** Sew the flattened 4-pair tops to the lower front/sides across **Goat Body R8–R10** (Lamb **R9–R11**), 4 body stitches between inner edges, legs projecting forward in a seated pose. Sew through both layers of each pair so the tops close as you sew.
3. **Arms:** Sew the flattened 4-pair tops to the upper sides at **Goat Body R19–R21** (Lamb **R20–R22**), angled slightly forward so the front hooves rest above the seated back legs.
4. **Tail:** Goat at **Body R8–R10** angled up; Lamb at **Body R9–R11** angled down.
5. Add sash (Goat) or collar (Lamb); tug-test every seam.
