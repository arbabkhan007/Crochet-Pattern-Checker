# Check Report — User-Supplied “Neat and Clean” NS01–NS17

**Scope:** Written audit of the 17 supplied texts. Every parenthetical stitch count was recomputed; every repeat was checked for stitches consumed vs produced; joins, pickups, flattened closures, component-opening matches, round/row references, symmetry maps, US/UK equivalents and gauge-derived size claims were cross-checked. This is **not** physical test-crocheting and not safety certification.

## Headline verdict

**Not production-ready as a set.** Most round-by-round arithmetic is actually sound, but **7 patterns contain blocking errors** (NS04, NS06, NS10, NS11, NS12, NS14, NS16), several more need local fixes or corrected claims, and the finished-size / yarn / fit statements remain untested assertions throughout.

### Blocking errors (must fix before anyone crochets from these)

| Pattern | Defect |
|---|---|
| **NS04** | Front-leg join overlaps a back leg (map below); 9-st leg tops are “closed” with only 3 sc with no fold/decrease instruction. |
| **NS06** | R5 as written consumes **36** sts and produces **40**, but the previous round has **44** and the round claims **(48)**. The body cannot be worked as written; every later round assumes 48. |
| **NS10** | Border works scallops into only **5 of the 9** interior side clusters per side — half of each blanket side is left unworked. |
| **NS11** | Specified **5.5 cm** base disc vs a base diameter of ≈ **4.55 cm** (30 sts at stated gauge); a rigid 5.5 cm disc also cannot pass the 30-st opening at R12. |
| **NS12** | 7.5 cm disc fits the 48-st base (Ø ≈ 7.6 cm) but the pattern says insert it at **R15**, after the opening has narrowed to 30 sts (Ø ≈ 4.8 cm). Physically impossible at that point. |
| **NS14** | “20 cm circumference — preserves full 5 cm hole” is geometrically false: 20 cm circumference = **6.4 cm** diameter. Header also says Mini has **24 scallops**; 168 sts ÷ 6-st repeat = **28** (the border note itself says 28). |
| **NS16** | Foot R2 `2 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 1 sc` consumes **19** sts and produces **16** — but Foot R1 has **23** sts and the round claims **(20)**. It cannot be worked. An arithmetically valid distribution needs 17 plain sc + 3 dec (e.g. `7 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec, 5 sc, dec` = 23 consumed → 20), with decrease positions re-pinned on a sample. |

## Pattern-by-pattern findings

### NS01 — Hamish the Highland Cow — PASS (sample-check placements)
- All counts recompute; Head R19 (24) matches Body R21 (24) exactly.
- Sample-check only: muzzle is a 7-round circular cap described as an “8 sts wide × 3 rnds tall” oval and sewn over Head R13–R16; belly patch sewn over Body R11–R19 spans the R17–R19 decrease zone. Both need a worked sample/photograph to confirm they sit as described.

### NS02 — Kawaii Halloween Mini Set — MINOR FIX
- Ghost body/base match at 24 sts; R11 FLO correctly leaves the 24 back loops of R10 for the base seam. Pumpkin, stem, leaf (14 → 15 sts) and bat counts all recompute.
- Ghost arms: a **5-st** opening flattened gives 2 matched pairs + 1 stitch at the fold; “work 2 sc across both layers” never says what happens to the fold stitch. Specify it (or decrease to 4 first). Same shorthand is used in NS03/NS05 — workable only if the odd stitch is explicitly placed at the fold.

### NS03 — Axel the Axolotl — MINOR FIX
- One-piece count chain 36 → 20 (neck) → 30 → 6 is fully consistent; dorsal markers R29–R38 = 10, and the fin repeat consumes exactly 10 markers (4 + 4 + fan + sl st). Tail-tip 6-st fold → 3 sc through both layers is correct.
- Limbs: **9-st** opening flattened to “4 sc across both layers” leaves the fold stitch unspecified (see NS02).
- The ventral fin pass (“along the bottom centreline … for 6 rounds”) names no exact stopping round or stitch count — clarify which 6 rounds and where it ends.

### NS04 — Coco the Capybara — FAIL
- The R4/R5 sequences *sum* correctly (18 → 24 → 30), but the **placement map is wrong**:
  - R4 produces Back Leg 2 over R4 sts **10–12**.
  - R5 then joins Front Leg 1 through R4 sts **11–13** — i.e. FL1 is sewn through two of the three stitches just created over BL2. It is not on the opposite/front semicircle, and the two “pairs” are not centred opposite each other (back-pair midpoint ≈ R4 st 6.5; front-pair midpoint ≈ st 16.5 of 24 — about 150°, not 180°).
  - Rebuild the map around marked front/back centre lines before publishing; do not just reword the claim.
- Leg tops: each leg ends at **9 sts**; “flatten and work **3 sc** through both layers” closes at most 3 pairs and leaves the rest of the rim unaccounted for. Either decrease to 8 and close with 4 sc, or state a fold that yields exactly 3 pairs. The body join (“work through the 3 stitches of BL1”) depends on this being exact.
- Ears repeat the 9 → 4 sc flattened closure (fold stitch unspecified).

### NS05 — Little Duck Plushie — MINOR FIX
- Body/head, wing (12 → 8 → 4 pairs) and beak (ch 5 → 10) counts all recompute; beak placement below the R17/R18 eye line is coherent.
- Feet: “flatten the **9-st** opening so **4 stitch pairs** line up” — 9 sts cannot form only 4 pairs; the 9th (fold) stitch must be specified or R3 decreased to 8 first.
- Wing base width: 4 sts at the stated ≈8 mm/st = **32 mm**, not the stated ≈28 mm.

### NS06 — Momo the Loaf Cat — FAIL
- **R5 is impossible as written** (see table above): `12 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 14 sc, inc, 2 sc, inc, 2 sc` consumes 36 / yields 40 against a 44-st previous round and a stated 48. Replace with a 40-sc + 4-inc distribution (44 consumed → 48) aligned to the marked oval ends; R6–R20 are all correct *if* R5 yields 48.
- Ear placement is not mirror-exact: right ear starts “2 stitches to the right of centre”, left ear starts “6 stitches to the left” and ends “2 stitches to the left”, while claiming 4 centre stitches between the ears. Depending on how the centre stitch is counted the gap is 3–5 sts. Give both ears as counted offsets from the Face Centre marker.

### NS07 — Pocket Positivity Trio — FIX TEXT
- All stitch counts recompute (Sunny’s 18 front loops → 9 petals is exact; Waddle patch ch 5 → 10; Spud 18 → 24 → 6 closes correctly).
- **Materials contradict themselves:** header says “Total ~16–18 m / ~6 g across all 3 pals”, but the per-pal amounts sum to **11.5 g** (2+2 + 3+1+1 + 2.5), which for DK is roughly 29 m, not 16–18 m. Reconcile or delete the total (Edition 2 deliberately stated no total).
- Waddle R10 “BLO dec × 6”: state which side the unworked front-loop ridge falls on, since it is visible at the base.

### NS08 — Ember the Baby Dragon — FIX / SAMPLE-CHECK
- Head (18) ↔ Body (18) match; snout, horns, legs, arms, tail and belly-patch counts all recompute; wing rows each consume/produce 10.
- Dorsal spike strip: 21 sc at the stated gauge ≈ **9.5 cm**, but it is asked to run crown (Head R2) → neck → between wings → along the tail with 2 + 2 + 3 spikes. Crown-to-tail-base alone is ≈ 8.7 cm of surface; three spikes on the tail need ≈ 4 cm more. Either lengthen the strip (≈13 cm / ~29 sts) or state that it ends at the tail base.

### NS09 — Shelby the Sea Turtle — COUNTS PASS, SIZE CLAIMS WRONG
- The appendage maps are **exactly** symmetric and are the strongest part of the set: Standard head at st 4, flippers at 9, 13, 19, 23 (gaps 4-3-5-3-4) → 35 sts; Bigger head at 8, flippers at 16, 24, 34, 42 → 53 sts. Shell/underside seams match 24/24 and 42/42; FLO appendages correctly reserve the back loops for the seam.
- But at the stated gauge (14 sc = 5 cm) a 24-st shell is Ø ≈ **2.7 cm** and a 42-st shell Ø ≈ **4.8 cm** before the small head fan — the claimed **6.0 cm / 8.5 cm** lengths are not achievable. Correct the sizes or change the gauge/counts; do not publish both.

### NS10 — Willow the Bunny Lovey — FAIL (border)
- Head and ears counts recompute (ears 18 → 12 → 8 → 4 pairs is exact); head/blanket attachment is coherent.
- **Border:** after R11 each side has 11 clusters including corners, i.e. **9 interior clusters**. R12 works the `5 dc in middle dc` scallop only **× 5**, then jumps to “sl st in space before corner”, silently skipping 4 clusters and their spaces on every side. Repeat must cover all 9 interior clusters with a defined corner transition.
- R2 also needs the slip-stitch path spelt out (from the R1 join across the first cluster into the corner space).

### NS11 — No-Sew Christmas Gnome — FAIL (base disc)
- Every table row recomputes in both US and UK columns, including the nose round (BO sits at st 15 of the 12-st face panel — acceptably centred) and the pom-pom (9 → 18 → 12 → 6).
- **Disc:** materials specify a **5.5 cm** disc for a base of 30 sts at 10.5 sc = 5 cm → Ø ≈ **4.55 cm**. A 5.5 cm rigid disc neither fits the base nor can be inserted at R12 through the 30-st opening. Specify ≈4.5 cm (flexible) and insert before stuffing.
- Clarify R17 “BLO”: as written it means the back loops of the R16 stitches; if the intent is to catch the unworked R15 loops to fold the brim, say so explicitly.

### NS12 — Bobble Christmas Tree — FAIL (disc timing)
- All 23 table rows recompute in US/UK; bobble tiers 12-10-9-7-6-4-3 with alternating offsets are internally consistent.
- **Disc timing:** the 7.5 cm disc matches the 48-st base (Ø ≈ 7.6 cm) but the instruction inserts it at **R15**, when the top opening is 30 sts (Ø ≈ 4.8 cm) — it cannot go in. Move insertion to R9/R10 (opening still 48) before the R11 decrease.
- Size claims also exceed the gauge: height 23 rnds at 11 rnds = 5 cm ≈ **10.5 cm**, base Ø ≈ 7.6 cm — not the claimed 14 cm × 9 cm.

### NS13 — Christmas Ornament Bundle — PASS (one clarification)
- Bauble sphere/cap counts, star map (fans at R2 sts 3, 7, 11, 15, 19; every one of the 20 sts accounted for; final sl st correctly re-uses St 1) and snowflake (R2’s sixth sl st lands in St 1 with no redundant join; picot-pointed R3) all check out.
- Clarify the bauble cap finish: after R18 the 6-st cap tube is still open; “sl st across top of cap” should state flattening and slip-stitching through both layers before the ch-16 loop.

### NS14 — Bobble Snowflake Tree Skirt — FAIL (text/geometry)
- The growth table itself is fully consistent (24 → 384, +12 per round; every bobble round consumes/produces correctly), and the border repeat (6 sts) divides 288/360/384 exactly (48/60/64 scallops).
- **Centre opening contradiction:** header “~5 cm diameter”, R1 note “20 cm circumference — preserves full 5 cm hole!”. 20 cm circumference ⇒ Ø 6.4 cm. Either correct the text to ≈6.4 cm (and keep the pole-fit check) or rebuild the ring at ≈19 ch for a true 5 cm — which changes every subsequent count.
- **Mini scallop contradiction:** header says stop at R13 = “168 sts / 24 scallops”; the border produces **28**. Fix the header.
- Terminology: the defined stitch (5 incomplete dc closed together) is a **bobble**, not a popcorn; drop “popcorn” from the BO definition.

### NS15 — Interchangeable Christmas Wreath — PASS (clarify support sequence)
- Torus geometry is coherent: 92 cm tube + ~3.8 cm tube Ø ⇒ ≈33 cm outside; 125 cm ⇒ ≈44 cm; mini 22 cm ⇒ ≈8.5 cm. Poinsettia (12 sts → 6 petals, last sl st into join), snowflake and bow piece counts (44×3, 28×2, 5×5) all recompute.
- Clarify *when* the support goes in: a foam/wire ring cannot be crocheted around as the tube grows in a spiral — state that the finished tube is threaded onto the ring (or the wire inserted) **before** the end-join whipstitch, and give a gauge so the ~3.8 cm tube thickness (and therefore all three diameters) is reproducible.

### NS16 — Mini Stocking Advent Garland — FAIL
- **Foot R2 cannot be worked** (see table above). Cuff, leg, heel-flap rows 1–10 (including the corrected “ch 1, TURN” restoring RS at Row 10), the 3 + 5 + 10 + 5 = 23 pickup and the toe (20 → 16 → 12 → 8 → 4) are otherwise consistent.
- The 5-st side pickups remain sparse: each heel side presents 6 row-ends (Rows 1–6) plus the 2 unworked step stitches, and the text asserts the pickup will “close the side step cleanly”. That is a sample-test claim, not an arithmetic one — soften the wording or add pickup points until a sample shows no holes.
- “Centre-back stitch of Cuff R4” — a 10-st heel has its centre *between* sts 5 and 6; the loop instruction already spans two stitches, so name them.

### NS17 — Fire Goat & Companion Lamb — FIX / SAMPLE-CHECK
- All head/body/limb/horn/ear arithmetic recomputes; both neck seams match 12 ↔ 12; the flame-sash peak map is exact (15 peaks at sts 1, 4, 7, …, 43 of 44).
- Sash fit claim is not supported by its own numbers: 44 sts = 22 cm around a 36-st (18 cm circumference) body leaves 1–2 sts of overlap only if the diagonal drops almost the full body height. Make the sash measured/modular (as Edition 2 had it) or state the actual overlap from a sample.
- Long ear: a 7-st foundation folded in half gives a 4-st (one side) / 3-st folded base ≈ 2 cm, not the stated “3-stitch / 1.5 cm” (that figure is correct only for the 6-st standard ear).
- Lamb collar (14 sts = 7 cm around a 12-st, 6 cm-circumference neck groove) is plausible but should be sample-confirmed, since it must sit in the seam groove and not on the 18-st rounds above/below it.

## Cross-cutting issues

1. **Flattened odd-count closures** (NS02 arms 5 sts, NS03 limbs 9 sts, NS05 feet 9 sts, NS04 ears 9 sts): “work N sc across both layers” is only reproducible if the pattern states where the unpaired fold stitch goes. NS04’s legs are the severe case (9 sts closed with 3 sc feeding a join).
2. **Unverified physical claims:** every pattern asserts finished sizes, yarn weights and fit (“sits stable”, “closes cleanly”, “1–2 stitch overlap”) with no test-crochet evidence. NS09 and NS12 contradict their own gauges outright; the rest are merely unproven.
3. **Safety:** NS01–NS08 (except embroidered-eye NS10/NS17) rely on plastic safety eyes — down to **5–6 mm eyes in 4–5 cm toys** (NS02, NS07) — with no choking-hazard warning, age guidance or compliance wording. As supplied, none of these texts supports a “production-ready” or toy-safe claim.
4. **What is genuinely good and should be kept:** NS09’s symmetric anchor maps, NS13’s star/snowflake stitch accounting, NS14’s growth table, NS15’s torus sizing, NS17’s sash peak map, and the NS01/NS08/NS17 matched neck openings all pass exactly as written.

## Bottom line

Do not publish this set as “every issue corrected”. Fix the 7 blocking defects, reconcile the NS07/NS09/NS12/NS14 claims with their own gauges, specify the odd-stitch closures, and then test-crochet at minimum NS04 (join map), NS06 (R5 replacement), NS10 (border), NS11/NS12 (discs), NS14 (centre ring) and NS16 (Foot R2 + heel pickup) before any size, fit or safety wording is restored.
