## Materials

### For the FIRE GOAT (Year of the Fire Goat 2027)

- **Worsted weight (size 4) cotton or acrylic yarn** in these colors:
  - **Cream / off-white:** main body, head, outer ears, arms, legs, tail (~80 yds / 73 m)
  - **Light tan / beige:** inner ears, muzzle, hooves cuffs (~20 yds / 18 m)
  - **Dark brown or gray:** horns (~10 yds / 9 m)
  - **Red:** fire-flame collar or vest sash (~15 yds / 14 m)
  - **Orange:** flame accents (~8 yds / 7 m)
  - **Golden yellow:** flame tips, flower accents (~8 yds / 7 m)
  - **Pink:** small amount for nose or inner ear detail (optional)
  - **Black:** embroidery thread or small amount of black yarn for eyes, mouth, nostrils
- **3.5 mm (E/4) crochet hook**
- **9–12 mm safety eyes** (2) — or use black yarn/embroidery floss to embroider eyes for a baby-safe version
- **Polyester fiberfill** stuffing
- **Yarn needle** (large-eye tapestry needle)
- **Stitch marker** (to mark the start of each round)
- **Scissors**
- **Optional extras:** small jingle bell for the collar, floral wire for bendable horns, glue for securing details, tiny fabric flowers or crocheted mini flowers for the floral crown.

### For the LAMB (companion plush)

- **White fluffy / chenille / worsted yarn:** head, body, outer ears, tail (~80 yds) — fluffy "fur" yarn gives an adorable lamb; if you don't have fluffy yarn, regular cream/white worsted works beautifully
- **Light tan / beige:** inner ears, face patch, legs, arms, hooves (~30 yds)
- **Red:** Lunar New Year collar (~10 yds)
- **Pink:** small amount for nose/inner ears
- **Black:** embroidery thread for eyes/mouth
- **Small jingle bell** (optional — a red collar with a gold bell is traditional for Lunar New Year animals)
- Same hook and tools as above.

> 💡 *You do NOT need both sets of colors — choose whichever animal you want to make, or make both for a complete Lunar New Year pair!*
>
> 💡 *The Lamb uses the same base instructions as the Goat, but swaps horns for ears (only), uses a rounder head and body (round counts are given in the [LAMB VARIATION] section), and replaces the flame collar with a simple red collar. The lamb-specific changes are noted in [LAMB VARIATION] boxes throughout the pattern.*

## Abbreviations (US Terms)

| Abbrev | Meaning |
|---|---|

| **sc** | single crochet |

| **dc** | double crochet |
| **inc** | increase — 2 sc in the same stitch |
| **dec** | decrease — invisible sc2tog (insert hook under front loop only of next 2 sts, yarn over, pull through 2 loops, yarn over, pull through remaining 2 loops) |

| **rnd** | round |

| **rep** | repeat |
| **( ) x N** | repeat the instructions in parentheses N times |
| **[N]** | the number of stitches you should have at the end of the round |

## Important amigurumi notes

- **Work in continuous spirals**, NOT joined rounds. Do not sl st at the end of each round and do not ch-1 between rounds. Use a stitch marker in the first stitch of each round and move it up as you go.
- **Crochet tightly.** If you can see stuffing through your stitches, switch to a smaller hook.
- **Stuff as you go,** especially for pieces where the opening becomes small (like the head). Stuff firmly for a plump, well-shaped plush — but not so firmly that stuffing pokes through the holes.
- **Invisible decreases** (dec) are strongly recommended for a smooth, professional-looking finish.
- **Magic Ring (MR):** If you don't know the magic ring, you can start with ch 2 and then work the required number of sc into the 2nd chain from the hook, but a magic ring gives a cleaner closed top.
- **Pattern numbers:** Instructions like "Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR [6]" mean: Round 1, work 6 single crochets into the magic ring, resulting in 6 stitches.

# 🐐 FIRE GOAT PATTERN

### 2027 Year of the Fire Goat

## HEAD (Cream yarn)

Start with cream yarn.

- **Rnd 1:** 6 sc in MR [6]
- **Rnd 2:** Inc in each st around [12]
- **Rnd 3:** (sc 1, inc) x 6 [18]
- **Rnd 4:** (sc 2, inc) x 6 [24]
- **Rnd 5:** (sc 3, inc) x 6 [30]
- **Rnd 6:** (sc 4, inc) x 6 [36]
- **Rnd 7:** (sc 5, inc) x 6 [42]
- **Rnd 8–14:** (7 rounds total) sc in each st around [42]

> 📝 *This creates the straight-sided portion of the head.*

- **Attach safety eyes now.** Place the eyes between Rnd 10 and Rnd 11, about 8 stitches apart (centered on the front). If you're embroidering eyes later, you can skip this and embroider them after stuffing.
- **Begin stuffing the head** with fiberfill. Continue stuffing firmly as you work the decreasing rounds.
- **Rnd 15:** (sc 5, dec) x 6 [36]
- **Rnd 16:** (sc 4, dec) x 6 [30]
- **Rnd 17:** (sc 3, dec) x 6 [24]
- **Rnd 18:** (sc 2, dec) x 6 [18]
- **Rnd 19:** (sc 1, dec) x 6 [12]
- Finish stuffing the head completely.
- **Rnd 20:** dec x 6 [6]

FO, leaving a long tail (about 12 inches / 30 cm) for sewing the head to the body. Thread tail onto yarn needle, weave through front loops of remaining 6 sts, pull tight to close. Set aside.

> 💡 *Easier-joint option: you can stop after Rnd 19 [12], finish stuffing, and leave the head OPEN — then whipstitch the 12-st opening directly to the 12-st neck opening of the body. Same result, easier sewing.*

> 🐑 **[LAMB VARIATION]** Work the **rounder lamb head** instead — see the full round counts in the [LAMB VARIATION] section below.

---

## MUZZLE (Light beige/tan yarn)

- **Rnd 1:** 6 sc in MR [6]
- **Rnd 2:** Inc in each st around [12]
- **Rnd 3:** (sc 1, inc) x 6 [18]
- **Rnd 4:** sc in each st around [18]
- **Rnd 5:** sc in each st around [18]

FO, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff very lightly (just enough to give it a slight puff, not a ball).

Sew the muzzle to the FRONT of the head, centered between the eyes, with the flat/closed edge at Rnd 1 and the open edge (where you fastened off) facing the head. Whipstitch around the edge to attach.

**Embroider the face:**

- Using black embroidery floss or black yarn and your yarn needle, embroider two small nostrils on the muzzle (two tiny horizontal stitches).
- Embroider a small Y-shaped or inverted-T mouth below the nostrils.
- If you did not use safety eyes, embroider two small French knots or oval shapes for eyes using black yarn (small V-shaped or curved "sleepy" eyes look cute on amigurumi).
- Optional: Add two tiny pink blush dots on the cheeks with pink yarn or pastel, by making two small X stitches.

---

## HORNS — make 2 (Dark brown or gray yarn)

- **Rnd 1:** 4 sc in MR [4]
- **Rnd 2:** (sc 1, inc) x 2 [6]
- **Rnd 3–5:** sc in each st around [6] (3 rounds)
- **Rnd 6:** BLO sc in each st around [6]
- **Rnd 7–9:** sc in each st around [6] (3 rounds)
- **Rnd 10:** (sc 1, inc) x 3 [9]
- **Rnd 11:** sc in each st around [9]

FO, leaving a long tail for sewing. **Do NOT stuff horns** — they will stand up better unstuffed, slightly curved. If you want them to be bendable/posable, insert a short piece of floral wire or a pipe cleaner inside before sewing.

Sew horns to the TOP of the head, between Rnd 2 and Rnd 5, spaced about 4 stitches apart, pointing slightly outward and backward.

> 🐑 **[LAMB VARIATION]** Skip the horns entirely! Lambs don't have horns. You'll make the same 2 ears as the goat, just in fluffy white + pink (see Ears below).

---

## EARS — make 2 (Cream outer + Light beige inner for goat; White fluffy outer + Pink inner for lamb)

**Outer ear (cream for goat / white fluffy for lamb):**

- Ch 7
- **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc 6 [6]
- **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, dec, sc 2, dec [4]
- **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st [4]
- **Row 4:** Ch 1, turn, dec, dec [2]

FO, leaving a long tail for sewing. Do NOT stuff.

**Inner ear (light beige for goat / light pink for lamb):** Repeat the same outer ear instructions using the inner ear color. FO.

Place the inner ear piece on top of the outer ear piece (wrong sides together). Sew the two layers together around the edge with the outer ear yarn tail, or simply hold them together when attaching. Fold the bottom of the ear (the ch-7 edge) in half and pinch it to create a slight curve, then sew it to the SIDE of the head, positioned below and slightly behind the horns (or at the same height on a lamb), approximately between Rnd 8 and Rnd 12. Angle ears slightly downward for a cute look.

> 🐑 **[LAMB VARIATION]** Make the **same 2 ears** (2 outer in fluffy white + 2 inner in pink) — that's still 4 pieces total, layered the same way as the goat's. Position two droopy ears on the sides of the head, around Rnd 9–13 on the lamb head.
>
> *Floppier option: chain 8 instead of 7 and work the ear over 7 stitches:*
> - **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc 7 [7]
> - **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, dec, sc 3, dec [5]
> - **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st [5]
> - **Row 4:** Ch 1, turn, dec, sc 1, dec [3]
>
> *Make the inner ear the same way.*

## BODY (Cream for goat / White fluffy for lamb)

Start at the bottom (the rear) and work up toward the neck.

- **Rnd 1:** 6 sc in MR [6]
- **Rnd 2:** Inc in each st around [12]
- **Rnd 3:** (sc 1, inc) x 6 [18]
- **Rnd 4:** (sc 2, inc) x 6 [24]
- **Rnd 5:** (sc 3, inc) x 6 [30]
- **Rnd 6:** (sc 4, inc) x 6 [36]
- **Rnd 7–8:** sc in each st around [36]
- **Rnd 9:** BLO sc in each st around [36] *(this creates a subtle ridge for the bottom to sit flat)*
- **Rnd 10–14:** sc in each st around [36] (5 rounds, working in both loops again)
- **Rnd 15:** (sc 4, dec) x 6 [30]
- **Rnd 16–17:** sc in each st around [30]
- **Rnd 18:** (sc 3, dec) x 6 [24]
- **Rnd 19:** sc in each st around [24]

**Begin stuffing the body firmly** from the bottom up. Continue stuffing as you decrease.

- **Rnd 20:** (sc 2, dec) x 6 [18]
- **Rnd 21:** sc in each st around [18]
- **Rnd 22:** (sc 1, dec) x 6 [12]
- **Rnd 23:** sc in each st around [12]

FO, leaving a long tail (about 18 inches / 45 cm) for sewing the head and attaching limbs later. Finish stuffing so the body is firm and round but not lumpy. The body should sit flat on its base (the BLO ridge at Rnd 9 helps).

> 🐑 **[LAMB VARIATION]** Work the **rounder lamb body** instead — see the full round counts in the [LAMB VARIATION] section below.

---

## ARMS / FRONT LEGS — make 2 (Light beige hooves + Cream upper, or all cream if you prefer no cuffs)

Start with light beige for the hoof/cuff tip:

- **Rnd 1:** 5 sc in MR [5]
- **Rnd 2:** Inc in each st around [10]
- **Rnd 3:** BLO sc in each st around [10] *(creates a defined hoof edge)*
- **Rnd 4:** sc in each st around [10]

Switch to cream (main color):

- **Rnd 5–12:** sc in each st around [10] (8 rounds)
- **Rnd 13:** (sc 3, dec) x 2 [8]

FO, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff the bottom hoof portion lightly; you do NOT need to stuff the upper arm all the way to the top — leave the top 1–2 inches unstuffed so the arms flop naturally. Flatten the opening and sew closed across the top, or just sew the open tube directly to the body.

Sew arms to the SIDES of the body, about 3–4 rounds below the neck opening (between Rnd 19–21 on the goat body; Rnd 20–22 on the rounder lamb body), angled slightly forward.

---

## LEGS / BACK LEGS — make 2 (Light beige hooves + Cream upper, or all cream if you prefer no hooves)

Start with light beige for the hooves:

- **Rnd 1:** 6 sc in MR [6]
- **Rnd 2:** Inc in each st around [12]
- **Rnd 3:** BLO sc in each st around [12]
- **Rnd 4–5:** sc in each st around [12]

Switch to cream (main color):

- **Rnd 6–12:** sc in each st around [12] (7 rounds)
- **Rnd 13:** (sc 2, dec) x 3 [9]

FO, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff the hoof and lower leg portion firmly; leave the upper portion lightly stuffed so the legs bend at the "knee." Flatten the top opening.

Sew legs to the BOTTOM of the body, angled slightly forward so the goat can sit stably. Position them approximately 4–5 stitches apart at the front of the body (toward the face). Pin them first and check that the plush sits without tipping before sewing.

---

## TAIL (Cream with optional light beige tip)

- **Rnd 1:** 4 sc in MR [4]
- **Rnd 2:** (sc 1, inc) x 2 [6]
- **Rnd 3–6:** sc in each st around [6] (4 rounds)

FO, leaving a tail for sewing. Do not stuff. Sew tail to the BACK of the body, centered, approximately 3–4 rounds below the neck opening, pointing upward.

> 🐑 **[LAMB VARIATION]** Work the **rounder, fluffier lamb tail** — see the [LAMB VARIATION] section below. Sew it angled down like a little lamb tail.

# 🔥 FIRE GOAT OUTFIT & ACCESSORIES

## Flame Collar / Sash (Red base with orange and yellow flames)

This is a simple flat piece that ties around the goat's neck like a New Year collar or vest sash.

**With red yarn, ch 36** (or a chain long enough to wrap around the plush's neck with extra length to tie — typically ch 32–40 depending on your tension).

- **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc in each ch across [35]
- **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across [35]
- FO red. Weave in ends.

**Add flame points along one long edge:**
Join orange yarn at one short end.

- Working along one long edge of the red strip, ch 3, sl st in next st, (ch 3, sl st in next st) repeat evenly all the way across to create small flame points along the top edge. (You should have about 12–15 small flame "peaks" depending on spacing.)
- FO orange, weave in ends.

**Add yellow flame tips:**
Join golden yellow yarn at the tip of one orange flame point. Sl st into the top of an orange ch-3 point, ch 2, sl st into the next orange peak's tip, repeating around. This gives the flames bright yellow tips.

- FO yellow, weave in ends.

**Tie the collar around the goat's neck** with the flame points facing upward. Tie a small bow or knot at the front (or side) and trim excess. For an authentic Lunar New Year look, tuck a tiny jingle bell or tiny crocheted gold coin under the collar.

## Mini Flower Crown (Optional — for a festive floral-crowned Fire Goat)

Make 3–4 tiny flowers (red, orange, yellow) and 2 small leaves (green):

**Tiny flower (make 3–4):**

- In any flower color (red/orange/yellow), ch 3, sl st into first ch to form a ring.
- *Petal:* (ch 2, 2 dc in ring, ch 2, sl st in ring) — repeat 5 times for 5 petals.
- FO, leaving a tail for sewing.

**Small leaf (make 2, green):**

- Ch 5, sc in 2nd ch from hook, hdc in next ch, sc in next ch, sl st in last ch.
- FO, leaving tail.

Sew flowers and leaves in a cluster onto the top of the head between the horns (or to one side), using their yarn tails. Arrange in a small "crown" shape.

## Red Lunar New Year Collar (for the Lamb)

This is a simple single-color collar (the classic red ribbon that Lunar New Year animals wear).

- With red yarn, ch 32 (adjust to fit around the lamb's neck).
- **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc in each ch across [31]
- **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across [31]
- FO. Weave in ends.
- Tie around the lamb's neck and add a small jingle bell or a tiny gold/yellow crocheted "coin" charm at the center. The bell makes a lovely soft jingle when the lamb is picked up.

# ASSEMBLY (for both Goat and Lamb)

Assemble in this order, pinning pieces first with sewing pins to check placement before you commit with stitches:

1. **Embroider the face** (eyes if not using safety eyes, nose, mouth, blush) on the head BEFORE attaching it to the body.
2. **Sew ears and horns** to the head (lamb: ears only).
3. **Sew the muzzle** to the head (for the goat; lamb: optional face patch).
4. **Sew head to body:** Position the head atop the neck opening. The head will tilt slightly forward naturally. Use the long yarn tail from the body (or head) to whipstitch all around the neck joint, working through both layers. Make sure the head is firmly attached — add extra stitches at the back of the neck for stability. Weave in ends. *(Easier option: leave the head open at [12] sts and whipstitch opening-to-opening.)*
5. **Sew arms** to the sides of the body (goat: between Rnd 19–21; lamb: between Rnd 20–22).
6. **Sew legs** to the bottom, checking that the plush sits.
7. **Sew tail** to the back (lamb: angled down).
8. **Add accessories:** Tie on the flame collar (goat) or red collar (lamb); sew on flower crown (goat) if desired; attach bell.

# Beginner Tips & Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Stuffing is poking through stitches | You're crocheting too loosely — go down a hook size or tighten your tension. |
| My amigurumi is oval/lopsided | Make sure you're counting stitches carefully at the end of each round. A single missed inc/dec changes the shape. Use a stitch marker. |
| Head wobbles on the body | Use more stuffing in the neck, and make extra stitches at the back of the neck joint when sewing. |
| Legs are uneven lengths | Measure before sewing — all legs should have the same number of rounds. Flatten the tops to the same height. |
| Fluffy yarn is hard to see stitches with | Work in good light, feel the stitches with your fingers, and count by touch. It's normal to "crochet blind" with fluffy yarn! |
| Magic ring won't close | Make sure you pull the yarn tail firmly before starting Rnd 2, and weave the tail in securely. |
