🧶 MOMO THE LOAF CAT - crochet pattern (PDF + audio + video)

A cat in the classic loaf position: paws tucked under, wider than it is tall, worked in one piece up from a 48-stitch oval base. The ears are worked as three short rows straight off the body and the tail is crocheted onto the side, so there are no seams - only the face to embroider.

Finished size: 7.9 x 5.2 x 4.3 cm. The 48-stitch oval base measures about 79 x 52 mm (4.5 mm per stitch, 4.3 mm per round). Check it after R4 - if the oval is much longer or rounder than that, your foundation chain is too loose or tight; re-chain at a steadier tension.

🧩 WHAT YOU WILL MAKE
• Base & body - one piece - Start with the ch-9 oval. Four rounds of increases bring it to 48 stitches; R5 in back loops turns the edge; then the sides go straight up before the crown decreases.
• Ears & tail - worked on, not sewn - Both are worked directly onto the body. Do the ears when you reach R12 (before the head closes), and the tail after the body is finished.

## FAQ

**Is this a physical item?**  
No - this listing is for the digital pattern (PDF + audio + video). You make Momo yourself.

**What terms does the pattern use?**  
US terms. MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = increase (2 sc in one st).

**How long does it take?**  
About 2 - 2.5 hours for a advanced beginner crocheter.

**Can I sell what I make?**  
Yes - small-batch sales are welcome with credit to Novality Crochet Studio. Mass production needs written permission.

**Can I get a refund?**  
Digital downloads cannot be refunded once delivered, but message us with any problem and we will help you finish.

**Momo is round, not oval.**  
The foundation chain sets the length - ch 9 gives 7 straight stitches each side. Keep the 6-stitch straight runs in R2-R6 exactly as written; putting increases in them rounds the shape off.
