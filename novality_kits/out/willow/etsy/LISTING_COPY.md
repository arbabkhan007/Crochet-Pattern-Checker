A bunny comforter: a soft, firmly stuffed head above a light, drapey granny-square blanket. Long floppy ears to hold, an embroidered face with no small parts, and a blanket that grows evenly from the centre out.

Finished size: 26 cm / 10 in blanket. About 4.5 mm per stitch and 4.3 mm per round on the head. ## FAQ

**Is this a physical item?**  
No - this listing is for the digital pattern (PDF + audio + video). You make Willow yourself.

**What terms does the pattern use?**  
US terms. MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, dc = double crochet.

**How long does it take?**  
About 2 - 3 hours for a advanced beginner crocheter.

**Can I sell what I make?**  
Yes - small-batch sales are welcome with credit to Novality Crochet Studio. Mass production needs written permission.

**Can I get a refund?**  
Digital downloads cannot be refunded once delivered, but message us with any problem and we will help you finish.

**The blanket is round, not square.**  
You are missing corner groups. Every corner must be (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) into the ch-2 space, all four, every round. Count corners before you join each round.
