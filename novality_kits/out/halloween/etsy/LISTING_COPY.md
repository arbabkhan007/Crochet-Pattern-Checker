🧶 KAWAII HALLOWEEN MINI SET - crochet pattern (PDF + audio + video)

Three palm-sized Halloween minis in DK cotton: Boo the ghost with a ruffled hem and a tiny witch hat, Pip the pumpkin with six needle-sculpted ribs, and Bramble the bat with scalloped picot wings. Each is about 5 cm tall and takes an hour or so.

Finished size: 5 - 5.5 cm each. 24 sc around measures about 27 mm across when stuffed in DK on 2.5 mm (3.5 mm per stitch, 3.2 mm per round). Gauge is not critical for these minis - a looser gauge simply gives slightly bigger toys - but keep it tight enough that stuffing does not show.

**Boo's ruffle will not ripple.**  
R14 must be a full 2 sc in EVERY stitch (24 to 48) and worked loosely, with R13 in back loops only so there is a ridge to flare from.
