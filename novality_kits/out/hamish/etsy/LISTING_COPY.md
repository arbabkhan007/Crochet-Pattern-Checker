🧶 HAMISH THE HIGHLAND COW - crochet pattern (PDF + audio + video)

A shaggy, sturdy Highland cow with a flattened brow, a wide oat-cream muzzle, curved horns and a weather-beaten fringe of knotted yarn. Head, muzzle, body, legs, ears, horns and tail are worked separately so every proportion can be pinned and checked before a single seam.

Finished size: 15 cm / 6 in sitting. 48 sc around measures about 70 mm across when stuffed (4.5 mm per stitch, 4.3 mm per round). Check on the head at R8. A slightly tight gauge is right for a cow this size - it keeps the head from sagging under the fringe.

✨ WHAT YOU GET (instant download)
• 13-page designed PDF pattern - every round in a clear table with stitch counts, photos and notes exactly where you need them
• Narrated audio walkthrough (~17 min, 10 chapters) - every round read aloud so you can crochet without looking at the page
• Tutorial video (1080p, ~17 min) - round-by-round slides that highlight the row being spoken, with captions and chapter markers
• Techniques explained step by step: magic ring & spiral, invisible decrease, the flattened brow, back-loop hoof line, locking safety eyes, knotted fringe, whip-stitch seams
• Assembly guide with exact placement for every piece
• Troubleshooting page (7 common snags) and 5 colourway ideas

🧩 WHAT YOU WILL MAKE
• Head - Yarn A - A 48-stitch sphere with one straight round, so the top flattens into a brow. Eyes go between R10 and R11, 10 stitches apart - lock the washers at R13, before the head narrows.
• Muzzle - Yarn B - A wide oval, not a circle - that is what makes a cow's nose. Stuff it firmly; it takes the nostrils and the mouth.
• Body - Yarn A - An egg with a wide, flat seat. Hamish sits, so the base is the widest part and it is stuffed hardest.
• Legs - make 4 - Start each leg in dark brown for the hoof, change to ginger after the BLO round. Back legs are stuffed firmly and sewn splayed; front legs hang.
• Ears & horns - Ears are flat teardrops that sit just below the horns, pointing out and slightly down. Horns are short curved cones in oat cream.
• Tail, fringe & scarf

✅ EVERY STITCH COUNT VERIFIED
All 76 rounds were machine-checked with a deterministic pattern validator: zero errors. The maths adds up, so you can trust the counts.

🧶 YOU WILL NEED
• Yarn A - Ginger: Worsted #4, ginger / rust, about 70 g. Head, body, legs, ears and tail.
• Yarn B - Oat cream: Worsted #4, oat cream, about 15 g. Muzzle, horns and inner ears.
• Yarn C - Dark chocolate: Worsted #4 or floss, dark brown, a few metres. Nostrils, mouth, hooves.
• Yarn D - Rust (optional): Worsted #4 in a contrasting rust or a small tartan fabric square for the scarf.
• Hook: 3.5 mm (US E/4) throughout.
• Eyes: Two 9 mm safety eyes (they sit under the fringe, so 9 mm rather than 6 mm keeps them visible).
• Stuffing & notions: Polyester fibre filling about 45 g; yarn needle; stitch markers; pins; a 6 cm piece of card for cutting fringe.

## FAQ

**Is this a physical item?**  
No - this listing is for the digital pattern (PDF + audio + video). You make Hamish yourself.

**What terms does the pattern use?**  
US terms. MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, inc = increase (2 sc in one st).

**How long does it take?**  
About 6 - 8 hours for a intermediate crocheter.

**Can I sell what I make?**

**Can I get a refund?**

**The head sags forward.**  
Stuff the head harder, sew the neck seam twice with a doubled strand, and check the head sits on R17-R19 of the body, not higher.
