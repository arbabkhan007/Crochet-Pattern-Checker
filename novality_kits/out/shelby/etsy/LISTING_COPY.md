🧶 SHELBY THE SEA TURTLE BAG CHARM - crochet pattern (PDF + audio + video)

A thirty-minute sea turtle charm in DK cotton. The shell is a small dome, the underside is a flat disc whose last round grows the head and four flippers as five bumps, and the two are crocheted together edge to edge around a pinch of stuffing. Add a split ring and Shelby lives on your keys or bag.

Finished size: 3.7 cm / 1.5 in across. 24 sc around measures about 27 mm across in DK on 2.5 mm (3.5 mm per stitch, 3.2 mm per round). The shell dome is about 27 mm wide; with the flippers Shelby spans about 37 mm and is 32 mm long. Gauge is not critical for a charm.

• Shell - main colour - A 24-stitch dome with a back-loop rim. Embroider the shell markings before joining - it is easier flat.

• Joining & the bigger Shelby - Join shell to underside with single crochet through both layers, working the bumps into single shell stitches so they stand free.

## FAQ

**Is this a physical item?**  
No - this listing is for the digital pattern (PDF + audio + video). You make Shelby yourself.

**What terms does the pattern use?**  
US terms. MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, hdc = half double crochet.

**How long does it take?**  
About 30 minutes for a easy crocheter.

**Can I sell what I make?**

**Can I get a refund?**  
Digital downloads cannot be refunded once delivered, but message us with any problem and we will help you finish.

**The cluster round does not come out at 35.**  
Nineteen plain stitches and five anchors consume the 24; 19 + 4 (head) + 12 (four flippers) = 35. If you ended with stitches to spare, one plain run is too short; if you ran out, you worked a plain stitch after the last flipper.
