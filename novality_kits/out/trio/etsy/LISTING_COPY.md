## FAQ

**Is this a physical item?**  
No - this listing is for the digital pattern (PDF + audio + video). You make Pocket Positivity Trio yourself.

**What terms does the pattern use?**  
US terms. MR = magic ring, ch = chain, sc = single crochet, hdc = half double crochet.

**How long does it take?**  
About 20 - 35 min each for a easy / beginner crocheter.

**Can I sell what I make?**  
Yes - small-batch sales are welcome with credit to Novality Crochet Studio. Mass production needs written permission.

**Can I get a refund?**  
Digital downloads cannot be refunded once delivered, but message us with any problem and we will help you finish.

**Petal edges curl, or will not close.**  
Each petal repeat is exactly two stitches of Rnd 3: sl st in one, (sc, hdc, sc) in the next. Nine repeats = 18. If you ran out of stitches you skipped a slip stitch; if you had stitches left you doubled one.
