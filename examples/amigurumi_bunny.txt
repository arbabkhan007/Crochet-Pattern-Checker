Amigurumi Bunny
Difficulty: Intermediate
Category: Amigurumi
Hook: 3.5mm
Yarn: DK weight, white for body, pink for ears

A cute stuffed bunny with long ears and a round body. Worked in continuous rounds.

HEAD (make 1)
Round 1: 6 sc into magic ring (6)
Round 2: inc x 6 (12)
Round 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Round 4: (2 sc, inc) x 6 (24)
Round 5: (3 sc, inc) x 6 (30)
Round 6: (4 sc, inc) x 6 (36)
Round 7-12: sc in each st around (36)
Round 13: (4 sc, dec) x 6 (30)
Round 14: (3 sc, dec) x 6 (24)
Round 15: (2 sc, dec) x 6 (18)
Round 16: (sc, dec) x 6 (12)
Round 17: dec x 6 (6)

BODY (make 1)
Round 1: 6 sc into magic ring (6)
Round 2: inc x 6 (12)
Round 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Round 4: (2 sc, inc) x 6 (24)
Round 5: (3 sc, inc) x 6 (30)
Round 6-10: sc in each st around (30)
Round 11: (3 sc, dec) x 6 (24)
Round 12: (2 sc, dec) x 6 (18)

EARS (make 2)
Round 1: 4 sc into magic ring (4)
Round 2: (sc, inc) x 2 (6)
Round 3: (2 sc, inc) x 2 (8)
Round 4-10: sc in each st around (8)

ARMS (make 2)
Round 1: 6 sc into magic ring (6)
Round 2: (sc, inc) x 3 (9)
Round 3-7: sc in each st around (9)

LEGS (make 2)
Round 1: 6 sc into magic ring (6)
Round 2: (sc, inc) x 3 (9)
Round 3: (2 sc, inc) x 3 (12)
Round 4-6: sc in each st around (12)
Round 7: (2 sc, dec) x 3 (9)

ASSEMBLY
1. Stuff head and body firmly
2. Sew head to body
3. Attach ears to top of head
4. Sew arms to sides of body
5. Attach legs to bottom
6. Add safety eyes and embroider nose
7. Weave in all ends
