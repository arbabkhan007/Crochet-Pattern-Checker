Crochet Mini Stocking — 24 Christmas Stockings for an Advent Garland
Beginner Friendly | US Crochet Terms | DK / Light Worsted Yarn
Finished size: approx. 4" (10 cm) tall x 2" (5 cm) wide
Hook: 4.0 mm (G/6). Construction: cuff-down, joined rounds; heel worked flat in short rows; toe decreased closed.

CUFF (Cuff Color)
Foundation: Ch 20 loosely. Join with sl st to first ch to form a ring. (20 sts)
Rnd 1 (setup): Ch 1, sc in each ch around. Join with sl st to first sc. (20)
Rnd 2-4 (ribbed cuff): Ch 1, working in BLO, sc in each st around. Join with sl st to first sc. (20)

LEG (Main Color)
Rnd 5: Ch 1, sc in each st around (both loops). Join with sl st. (20)
Rnd 6-10: Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (20)

HEEL FLAP (worked flat in rows over next 10 sts)
Row 1 (RS): Ch 1, sc in next 10 sts only. Leave remaining 10 sts unworked. TURN. (10)
Row 2 (WS): Ch 1, sc in BLO of each of the 10 sts. TURN. (10)
Row 3: Ch 1, sc in each of the 10 sts. TURN. (10)
Row 4: Ch 1, sc in BLO of each of the 10 sts. TURN. (10)
Row 5: Ch 1, sc in each of the 10 sts. TURN. (10)
Row 6: Ch 1, sc in BLO of each of the 10 sts. DO NOT TURN. (10)

TURN THE HEEL (short rows)
Row 7: Ch 1, sc 6, dec, TURN (leave last 2 sts unworked). (7)
Row 8: Ch 1, sc 4, dec, TURN (leave last st unworked). (5)
Row 9: Ch 1, sc 3, dec, TURN. (4)
Row 10: Ch 1, sc 2, dec. DO NOT TURN. (3)

FOOT (resume rounds)
Pickup round: Ch 1, 5 sc along right edge of heel flap, 1 sc in each of 10 instep sts, 5 sc along left edge of heel flap, 1 sc in each of 3 remaining center heel sts. Join with sl st. (approximately 23)
Evening round: Ch 1, sc in each st around with 3 decreases evenly placed to end with 20 sts. Join with sl st. (20)
Foot rounds: Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (20)
Rnd 6-9 (foot, 4 more rounds): Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (20)

TOE (Accent Color)
Rnd 1: Ch 1, (sc 3, dec) 4 times. Join with sl st. (16)
Rnd 2: Ch 1, (sc 2, dec) 4 times. Join with sl st. (12)
Rnd 3: Ch 1, (sc 1, dec) 4 times. Join with sl st. (8)
Rnd 4: Ch 1, dec 4 times. Join with sl st. (4)

Fasten off, weave tail through front loops of remaining 4 sts, pull tight to close.

HANGING LOOP
Ch 12-15, sl st back into same spot on cuff. Fasten off.
