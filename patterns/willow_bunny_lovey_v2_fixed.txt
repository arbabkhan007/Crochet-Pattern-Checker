# Willow the Bunny Lovey — v2 (VALIDATED & CORRECTED)
# Design Code NS 10 — Novality Store / Novality Crochet Studio
# No small parts | Advanced beginner | 2-3 hours
#
# Willow is a bunny comforter: a soft, firmly stuffed head above a light, drapey
# granny-square blanket. Long floppy ears to hold, an embroidered face with no
# small parts, and a blanket that grows evenly from the centre out.
#
# FINISHED SIZE (verified against the stated gauge of 4.3 mm/stitch, 3.8 mm/round):
#   Blanket about 26 cm (10 in) square after 20 rounds (approx. 37 cm / 14.5 in
#   corner to corner); head about 4.9 cm across and 5.7 cm tall; roughly 25 cm
#   from the ear tips to the opposite corner, depending on how the ears flop.
#   DK cotton on a 3.5 mm hook (3.0 mm for the head if your tension is loose).
#
# SAFETY — A BABY ITEM, READ THIS
#   No safety eyes, buttons, beads or removable parts; the face is embroidered
#   and every floss end is knotted inside the head. That is safer, but it is not
#   a tested toy — do not describe Willow as "baby-safe" without ASTM F963 /
#   EN 71 testing. Comforters are not recommended in the cot for babies under 12
#   months and should be used with supervision; put that in your listing. Sew
#   every seam twice and weave ends in at least 5 cm. Say "embroidered face, no
#   small parts" — a claim you can stand behind.
#
# MATERIALS & GAUGE
#   Yarn: DK (#3) 100% cotton (or a baby-safe certified blend), about 60 g
#         (approx. 140-150 m) — cream, sage, dusty pink or pale grey. Buy two
#         50 g balls so you never run short. Only the head is stuffed.
#   Hook: 3.5 mm (US E/4) for the blanket; 3.0 mm for the head if tension is loose.
#   Eyes/floss: none — face embroidered in dark brown or charcoal cotton floss,
#         ends knotted inside.
#   Also: about 8 g fibre fill, tapestry needle, stitch marker.
#   Gauge: each dc approx. 4.3 mm wide, each round approx. 3.8 mm deep. Check on
#         the blanket after Rnd 3: 9 dc along an edge = approx. 39 mm; narrower
#         and the blanket comes out small.
#
# CONSTRUCTION NOTES
#   The HEAD and EARS are worked in a continuous spiral (no joins; marker in the
#   first stitch). The BLANKET is worked in JOINED rounds: each round closes with
#   a slip stitch and the next begins with a starting chain (ch 3 — it counts as
#   a dc; you do not turn). Work every stitch through both loops unless noted.
#
# TECHNIQUES USED
#   1. Magic ring & spiral — the head and each ear begin with a magic ring;
#      pull the tail tight after round one.
#   2. The granny-square corner — every blanket corner is (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)
#      into one corner space. Those four corners make the piece square instead
#      of round. Miss one corner group and the whole square pulls out of true —
#      check all four at the end of every round.
#   3. The border round — one final round of single crochet around the edge
#      with 3 sc into each corner space squares the edge and stops the blanket
#      curling. Do not skip it.
#
# SIZING THE BLANKET
#   The square grows approx. 1.3 cm per side per round: 18 rounds ~ 23 cm,
#   20 rounds ~ 26 cm, 22 rounds ~ 28 cm. Add rounds to make a bigger lovey.

=== HEAD (make 1) ===
Round 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Round 2: inc in each st around (12)
Round 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Round 4: (2 sc, inc) x 6 (24)
Round 5: (3 sc, inc) x 6 (30)
Round 6: (4 sc, inc) x 6 (36)
Round 7: sc in each st around (36)
Round 8: sc in each st around (36)
Round 9: sc in each st around (36)
Round 10: sc in each st around (36)
Round 11: (4 sc, dec) x 6 (30)
Round 12: (3 sc, dec) x 6 (24)
Round 13: (2 sc, dec) x 6 (18)
Round 14: (sc, dec) x 6 (12)
Round 15: dec x 6 (6)
# Fasten off and close with the tail. The finished head is about 4.9 cm across
# and 5.7 cm tall (15 rounds). Stuff FIRMLY — a soft head collapses when gripped
# and the face distorts. (Ears are sewn to Rnds 4-6; face is embroidered on
# Rnds 8-10.) NOTE: the decrease ladder must run 36 > 30 > 24 > 18 > 12 > 6 —
# that is [4 sc, dec], [3 sc, dec], [2 sc, dec], [sc, dec], dec, one round each.


=== EARS (make 2, do not stuff) ===
Round 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Round 2: (sc, inc) x 3 (9)
Round 3: (2 sc, inc) x 3 (12)
Round 4: sc in each st around (12)
Round 5: sc in each st around (12)
Round 6: sc in each st around (12)
Round 7: sc in each st around (12)
Round 8: sc in each st around (12)
Round 9: (2 sc, dec) x 3 (9)
Round 10: sc in each st around (9)
Round 11: (sc, dec) x 3 (6)
# Flatten and fasten off with a long tail. Sew to Rnds 4-6 of the head, about
# 10 stitches apart, pinching each base so it flops forward.

=== BLANKET (granny square, joined rounds) ===
# With a 3.5 mm hook, ch 4 and sl st to the first ch to form a ring.
# Rnd 1: ch 3 (counts as first dc), 2 dc in the ring, [ch 2, 3 dc in the ring] x 3,
#        ch 2, sl st to the top of the ch-3. [12 dc, 4 corner spaces]
# Rnd 2: sl st into the next corner space; ch 3, 2 dc in that space, ch 2, 3 dc in
#        the same space (first corner); then (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) into each remaining
#        corner space; sl st to close. [24 dc]
# Rnds 3-20: into every corner space work (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc), and into every
#        space along a side work 3 dc. Each round adds one 3-dc group to every
#        side (+12 dc per round). Check all four corners before closing each round.
# Border: work one final round of sc all the way around — 1 sc into each dc and
#        3 sc into each corner space — then sl st and fasten off. At Rnd 20 that
#        is 240 sc + 12 corner sc = 252 sc. This firms the edge and stops the
#        square curling. Open dc clusters and crisp (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) corners
#        keep the square true round after round.
# DC CHECKPOINT (total double crochets at the end of the round):
#   Rnd 3 = 36 | Rnd 5 = 60 | Rnd 10 = 120 | Rnd 15 = 180 | Rnd 20 = 240
#   (60 dc per edge = approx. 26 cm at the stated gauge)

=== ASSEMBLY ===
# Face (embroidery only): two small oval eyes about 8 stitches apart across
#   Rnds 8-9, a small inverted-triangle nose centred between and below them, and
#   a shallow Y for the mouth. KNOT every end inside the head and bury the tails
#   before closing. Skip blush — chalk rubs off on bedding and is not washable.
# Ears: sew to Rnds 4-6 of the head, about 10 stitches apart, pinching each base
#   so it flops forward.
# Joining: centre the head over one corner of the square, overlapping the
#   blanket by about 3 cm. Sew all the way around the base of the head, THEN sew
#   it a second time — this is the only structural seam and it will be pulled,
#   chewed and washed. Weave every end in at least 5 cm.

=== LISTING & CARE (for your listing) ===
# Materials: 100% cotton, embroidered face, no safety eyes, no buttons, beads
#   or removable parts.
# Care: machine wash cool on a gentle cycle inside a mesh bag; reshape while
#   damp and dry flat. Do not tumble dry — cotton can shrink and the stuffing
#   may clump.
# Supervision: comforters are not recommended in the cot for babies under 12
#   months; use with supervision.
# Colorways: cream, sage, dusty pink, pale grey and butter yellow — neutrals
#   outsell brights in the baby category.

=== TROUBLESHOOTING ===
# Blanket curls / not square: you skipped the sc border, or missed a
#   (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) corner — check all four corners at the end of every round.
# Came out too small / head flops: tighter than 4.3 mm/dc — go up a hook size or
#   add rounds (22 rounds = ~28 cm). Stuff the head firmly and sew the base seam
#   twice.
