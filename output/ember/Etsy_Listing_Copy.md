## Description

```
🌟 MEET EMBER — THE BABY DRAGON WITHOUT FIRE 🌟

A chunky, big-headed baby dragon with a stubby snout, scalloped wings and a ridge of spikes down her back. She sits beautifully — with folding haunches and splayed front legs — and is the perfect make for a cosy weekend project.

🧶 WHAT'S IN THE PDF
• 13 clearly laid-out pages, US terminology
• Full materials & gauge section
• Colour-coded stitch-count tables for every piece
• Head, snout, body, legs, horns, tail, wings and continuous spike ridge
• Step-by-step assembly order and troubleshooting guide
• Safety notes and Terms of Use

📏 FINISHED SIZE
• Height (seated): about 11 cm (4.3 in)
• Wingspan: about 12.5 cm (5 in)
• Tail: about 5 cm (2 in)

🪡 SKILL LEVEL
Intermediate — you'll need to know sc, inc, dec, working in a spiral, and basic flat rows.

🧵 MATERIALS
• Worsted #4 yarn: ~30 g main, ~20 g belly/wings, ~10 g spikes
• 3.5 mm (US E/4) hook
• 2 × 10 mm safety eyes (or embroidered eyes)
• Polyester fibre fill, tapestry needle, stitch markers, pins

🛍️ PERMITTED USE
You may make and sell finished Embers in small batches with credit to "Novality Store". The pattern itself may not be resold or redistributed.

INSTANT DIGITAL DOWNLOAD — print or read on your device. Happy crocheting!
```
