# Simple Scarf

**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Created:** September 12, 2026

## Steps

1. Chain 20 stitches
2. Single crochet in second chain from hook
3. Single crochet in each chain across (19 stitches)
4. Chain 1 and turn
5. Repeat rows 2-4 for 60 inches
6. Fasten off and weave in ends

## Tips

- Take your time with each step
- Practice the basic stitches first
- Don't worry about perfection - handmade items have character!
