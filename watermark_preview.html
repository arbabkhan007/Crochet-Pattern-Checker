Materials:

  • Worsted weight yarn - 500 yards
  • 5.0mm crochet hook
  • Stitch markers

Instructions:

Row 1: Ch 20, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across

Row 2: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across
