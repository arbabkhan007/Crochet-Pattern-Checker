# Bobble Snowflake Tree Skirt — NS14 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** Dual US & UK (`US sc = UK dc` · `US dc = UK tr` · `BO = 5-dc bobble`)
* **Skill Level:** Easy–Intermediate
* **Finished Sizes (3 stopping points):**
  * **Mini / Tabletop (stop after R13 — 168 sts / 28 scallops):** ≈ 48 cm (19 in) diameter
  * **Standard (stop after R23 — 288 sts / 48 scallops):** ≈ 78 cm (31 in) diameter
  * **Alt Large (stop after R29 — 360 sts / 60 scallops):** ≈ 96 cm (38 in) diameter
  * **Large (stop after R31 — 384 sts / 64 scallops):** ≈ 102 cm (40 in) diameter
  * **Centre opening:** ≈ **6.4 cm (2.5 in) diameter** at stated gauge (24-ch ring, 20 cm circumference — always test against your actual tree pole before working past R2)
* **Gauge:** 12 US dc (UK tr) = 10 cm (4 in)
* **Hook:** 5.0–5.5 mm (US H/8–I/9)
* **Materials:** Worsted/Aran (#4): MC Forest Green — Mini ~55 g, Standard ~130 g, Large ~230 g · CC Oat Cream — Mini ~40 g, Standard ~95 g, Large ~170 g
* **Notions:** 12 locking stitch markers, tapestry needle, blocking pins

### Special Stitch — Bobble (BO)
Work 5 incomplete US dc (UK tr) in the same stitch until 6 loops remain on hook; yarn over and pull through all 6; push the cluster firmly to the Right Side and snug the loop tight. BO counts as 1 stitch. *(This is a bobble, not a popcorn.)* Bobbles sit inside each 12-spoke sector, clear of the increase columns.

---

### Centre Ring & 12-Sector Growth (joined rounds, do not turn)
Starting ch 2 never counts as a stitch; work the first stitch into the same stitch as the join and sl st to the first stitch at the end of each round. Mark the **second stitch of each increase** on R2 and move the 12 markers up each round.
**Colour rule:** plain rounds in **MC**; complete Bobble Rounds (**R4, R7, R10, R13, R16, R19, R22, R25, R28, R31**) in **CC**.

### Optional Surface-Crochet Spokes (CC)
Before the border, join CC on the RS at the foundation ring aligned with one increase marker. Holding the yarn behind the work, work relaxed surface slip stitches radially outward along that increase column to the outer marker. FO. Repeat for all 12 columns.

### Scalloped Border (CC)
* **US:** Join CC in any stitch of the final round; ch 1, sc in same st as join, skip 2 sts, 5 dc in next st, skip 2 sts, [sc in next st, skip 2 sts, 5 dc in next st, skip 2 sts] around, sl st to first sc, FO.
* **UK:** Join CC in any stitch; ch 1, dc in same st as join, skip 2 sts, 5 tr in next st, skip 2 sts, [dc in next st, skip 2 sts, 5 tr in next st, skip 2 sts] around, sl st to first dc, FO.
* *(Repeat = 6 sts. Produces **28 scallops on R13**, **48 on R23**, **60 on R29**, **64 on R31**.)*
* Block the skirt flat before first use.
