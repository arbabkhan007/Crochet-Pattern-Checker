# Bobble Christmas Tree — NS12 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** Dual US & UK (`US sc = UK dc` · `US BO = 5-dc bobble / UK 5-tr bobble`)
* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Finished Size:** Approx. **10.5 cm (4.1 in) tall × 7.6 cm (3 in) base diameter** at stated gauge (the previously claimed 14 × 9 cm exceeds this gauge)
* **Gauge:** 10 sc × 11 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 4.0 mm (US G-6)
* **Materials:**
  * **Worsted (#4) Pine Green:** ~45 g (optional scraps of red/gold/white for surface berries)
  * **Notions:** Fiberfill (~30 g), **7.5 cm cardboard or stiff-felt base disc**, stitch marker, tapestry needle

> **Correction in this version:** the base disc is inserted **after R9, while the opening is still 48 sts (Ø ≈ 7.6 cm)**. The supplied version inserted it at R15, when the opening had narrowed to 30 sts (Ø ≈ 4.8 cm), where it cannot physically go in.

### Abbreviations
**MR:** magic ring · **sc (UK dc)** · **inc:** 2 sc in same st · **sc2tog (UK dc2tog)** · **BLO:** back loop only · **BO:** 5-dc bobble (UK 5-tr): 5 incomplete dc (UK tr) in same st (6 loops on hook), yarn over, pull through all 6, push bobble to the outside.

---

### Complete One-Piece Bobble Tree (bottom-up, continuous spiral)
Decreases fall on the plain rounds; bobble rounds stagger the bobbles (alternating start offset) and step down 12 → 10 → 9 → 7 → 6 → 4 → 3 bobbles toward the tip, so density increases upward and bobbles do not stack in straight vertical lines.

| Rnd | US Instruction | UK Instruction | Sts | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | 6 sc in MR | 6 dc in MR | (6) | Base centre |
| **R2** | inc in each st around | inc in each st around | (12) | Base |
| **R3** | [sc, inc] × 6 | [dc, inc] × 6 | (18) | Base |
| **R4** | [2 sc, inc] × 6 | [2 dc, inc] × 6 | (24) | Base |
| **R5** | [3 sc, inc] × 6 | [3 dc, inc] × 6 | (30) | Base |
| **R6** | [4 sc, inc] × 6 | [4 dc, inc] × 6 | (36) | Base |
| **R7** | [5 sc, inc] × 6 | [5 dc, inc] × 6 | (42) | Base |
| **R8** | [6 sc, inc] × 6 | [6 dc, inc] × 6 | (48) | Full width |
| **R9** | **BLO** sc in each st around — **insert the 7.5 cm disc now**, flat against the base inside, before continuing | **BLO** dc around — **same** | (48) | Base edge + disc |
| **R10** | [BO, 3 sc] × 12 | [BO, 3 dc] × 12 | (48) | Tier 1: 12 bobbles |
| **R11** | [6 sc, sc2tog] × 6 | [6 dc, dc2tog] × 6 | (42) | Decrease |
| **R12** | 2 sc, [BO, 3 sc] × 10 | 2 dc, [BO, 3 dc] × 10 | (42) | Tier 2: 10 bobbles |
| **R13** | [5 sc, sc2tog] × 6 | [5 dc, dc2tog] × 6 | (36) | Decrease |
| **R14** | [BO, 3 sc] × 9 | [BO, 3 dc] × 9 | (36) | Tier 3: 9 bobbles |
| **R15** | [4 sc, sc2tog] × 6 | [4 dc, dc2tog] × 6 | (30) | Decrease |
| **R16** | 2 sc, [BO, 3 sc] × 7 | 2 dc, [BO, 3 dc] × 7 | (30) | Tier 4: 7 bobbles |
| **R17** | [3 sc, sc2tog] × 6 *(stuff firmly above the disc from here)* | [3 dc, dc2tog] × 6 *(same)* | (24) | Decrease |
| **R18** | [BO, 3 sc] × 6 | [BO, 3 dc] × 6 | (24) | Tier 5: 6 bobbles |
| **R19** | [2 sc, sc2tog] × 6 | [2 dc, dc2tog] × 6 | (18) | Decrease |
| **R20** | 2 sc, [BO, 3 sc] × 4 *(top up stuffing)* | 2 dc, [BO, 3 dc] × 4 *(same)* | (18) | Tier 6: 4 bobbles |
| **R21** | [sc, sc2tog] × 6 | [dc, dc2tog] × 6 | (12) | Decrease |
| **R22** | [BO, 3 sc] × 3 | [BO, 3 dc] × 3 | (12) | Tier 7: 3 bobbles |
| **R23** | sc2tog × 6; FO and close | dc2tog × 6; FO and close | (6) | Tip |

*Optional berries: with red/gold/white scraps, sew small French knots or 3-st bobbles between the green bobbles after stuffing.*
