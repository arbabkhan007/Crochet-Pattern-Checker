# Interchangeable Christmas Wreath — NS15 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** Dual US & UK (`US sc = UK dc` · `US hdc = UK htr` · `US dc = UK tr` · `US tr = UK dtr`)
* **Skill Level:** Beginner–Easy
* **Finished Sizes:** Mini Ornament: 22 cm tube → ≈ 8.5 cm outside Ø · Standard: 92 cm tube → ≈ 33 cm outside Ø · Large: 125 cm tube → ≈ 44 cm outside Ø · Decorations: Poinsettia ≈ 8 cm, Snowflake ≈ 8.5 cm, Bow ≈ 9 cm
* **Gauge:** 10 sc × 11 rnds = 5 cm in spiral sc (24-st tube ≈ 12 cm circumference ≈ 3.8 cm tube Ø — the diameters above depend on this gauge)
* **Hook:** 3.5–4.0 mm (3.0 mm with DK for Mini)
* **Materials:**
  * **Wreath base (Forest Green worsted #4):** Mini ~15 g (DK) · Standard ~95 g · Large ~130 g
  * **Support core (required for door sizes):** 30 cm / 40 cm foam or wire wreath ring, **or** a loop of 12–14 gauge craft wire inside the tube (plus ~80–110 g firm fiberfill; Mini ~4 g, no wire)
  * **Decorations (worsted #4):** Red ~15 g, White ~8 g, Yellow ~3 g, Leaf Green ~5 g
  * **Hanging ribbon:** at least 60 cm load-rated woven ribbon

---

### 1. Wreath Base Tube (Forest Green, continuous spiral)
* **Standard & Large (24-st tube):**
  * **Foundation:** Ch 24, sl st to first ch without twisting.
  * **R1:** Sc in each ch around | *UK: dc in each ch* (24)
  * **R2+:** Sc (UK dc) in each st around until the tube measures **92 cm** (Standard) or **125 cm** (Large). Stuff firmly every 6–8 rounds as you go if using the internal-wire method.
* **Mini Ornament (10-st tube, DK, 3.0 mm):** Ch 10, join, work 10 sc (UK dc) per round until tube measures **22 cm**. Stuff firmly.
* **Support & ring join (door sizes) — read before closing:** The tube is crocheted straight, *not* around the ring. When it reaches full length: **(a) foam/wire ring:** gather/thread the open tube evenly around the ring (or slide the wire loop inside the tube), distributing stuffing around the support; **(b)** top up stuffing at the ends; **(c)** FO with a 50 cm tail, align the 24 final-round stitches with the 24 foundation chains, and whipstitch all 24 matched pairs. Check the ring hangs as a true circle before final tightening. Mini: whipstitch the 10 end pairs the same way.
* Hang door wreaths **only** from the load-rated ribbon wrapped twice around the top of the tube — not from the crochet fabric or decoration ties.

### 2. Removable Poinsettia

| Step | US | UK | Sts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 Centre** | Yellow: 6 sc in MR, sl st to first sc | Yellow: 6 dc in MR, sl st | (6) |
| **R2 Centre** | Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around, sl st, FO | Ch 1, 2 dc in each st, sl st, FO | (12) |
| **Petals (Red)** | Join Red in any R2 st: `[ch 3, (2 tr, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook [tip], 2 tr) in next st, ch 3, sl st in next st] × 6`, FO (last sl st lands in join st) | Join Red: `[ch 3, (2 dtr, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch [tip], 2 dtr) in next st, ch 3, sl st in next st] × 6`, FO | 6 petals |
| **Leaves (Green ×3)** | Ch 8; sc in 2nd ch, hdc in next, dc in next 3, hdc in next, sc in last; FO, 15 cm tail | Ch 8; dc in 2nd ch, htr, tr × 3, htr, dc; FO | (7) |
| **Assemble & tie** | Sew 3 leaves to the back, tips peeking between petals. Fold a 55 cm green tie in half, lark's-head it under 2 back stitches; tie onto wreath. | Same | – |

### 3. Removable Snowflake (White)

| Rnd | US | UK | Sts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | MR, ch 3 (counts as dc), 11 dc, sl st to top of ch-3 | MR, ch 3 (counts as tr), 11 tr, sl st | (12) |
| **R2** | `[Ch 5, skip next st, sl st in next st] × 6` (6th sl st in top of ch-3; no extra sl st) | Same | 6 ch-5 sps |
| **R3** | In each ch-5 sp: `(sl st, ch 3, 2 tr, ch 3, sl st in 3rd ch [picot], 2 tr, ch 3, sl st)`; sl st to first sl st, FO | `(sl st, ch 3, 2 dtr, ch 3, sl st in 3rd ch [picot], 2 dtr, ch 3, sl st)`; FO | 6 arms |
| **Tie** | Block flat; fold a 55 cm white tie in half, lark's-head under 2 back stitches. | Same | – |

### 4. Removable Bow (Red)

| Piece | US | UK | Sts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bow loop strip** | Ch 45; Row 1: sc in 2nd ch and across (44), ch 1, turn; Rows 2–3: sc across (44). FO with 25 cm tail; sew short ends together, pinch seam to centre to form two loops. | Ch 45; Row 1: dc in 2nd ch and across (44); Rows 2–3: dc across. FO; same assembly. | 44 × 3 |
| **Tails strip** | Ch 29; Row 1: sc in 2nd ch and across (28), ch 1, turn; Row 2: sc across (28). FO; fold in half so two tails hang. | Ch 29; dc across (28) × 2 rows. FO; fold. | 28 × 2 |
| **Centre band** | Ch 6; Row 1: sc in 2nd ch and across (5), ch 1, turn; Rows 2–5: sc across (5). FO with 25 cm tail. | Ch 6; dc across (5), Rows 2–5 dc across. FO. | 5 × 5 |
| **Assemble & tie** | Pinched tails behind pinched bow loops; wrap centre band tightly around both, sew ends at back through all layers. Fold a 55 cm red tie in half and attach at the back. | Same | – |

*Tie decorations on firmly and tug-test; remove decorations before storing the wreath so ties do not distort the tube.*
