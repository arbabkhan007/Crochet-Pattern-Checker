# Year of the Fire Goat 2027 Plushie Set — NS17 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 16–17 cm (6.25–6.75 in) tall seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 20 sc × 22 rnds = 10 cm (4 in)
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials (per animal):**
  * **Main Body (Worsted #4), ~73 m / 80 yd / ~40 g:** Warm Cream (Goat) or Wool White (Lamb)
  * **Muzzle, Hooves & Goat Inner Ears (Light Tan):** ~18 m / 20 yd (~10 g) per animal
  * **Lamb Inner Ears (Pale Pink):** ~4 m / 4.5 yd (Lamb only)
  * **Goat Horns (Espresso Brown):** ~9 m / 10 yd (Goat only)
  * **Sash/Collar (Scarlet Red):** ~16 m (Goat sash) | ~6 m (Lamb collar), plus Burnt Orange & Golden Yellow scraps for flames
  * **Embroidery:** Black & pink floss (eyes, nose, blush)
  * **Notions:** Fiberfill (~40 g per animal), stitch marker, tapestry needle, pins, scissors

> **Corrections in this version:** the goat sash is now **measured/modular** (the fixed 44-st strip cannot leave only 1–2 sts of overlap on every body), and the long-ear folded base is correctly stated as 4 sts on the outer side.

### Abbreviations
**MR:** magic ring · **ch:** chain · **sl st:** slip stitch · **sc:** single crochet · **hdc:** half double crochet · **dc:** double crochet · **inc:** 2 sc in same st · **dec:** invisible decrease (spiral rounds) · **sc2tog:** decrease through both loops (flat ear rows) · **BLO:** back loop only · **FO:** fasten off

## PART A — Fire Goat

### 1. Goat Head (Warm Cream)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6) · **R2:** inc around (12) · **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18) · **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24) · **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30) · **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36) · **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8–R14 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (42)
* *Embroider two black eyes between **R10 and R11**, 8 sts apart centre-to-centre; knot tails inside.*
* **R15:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36) · **R16:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30) · **R17:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) · **R18:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff firmly)* · **R19:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* *FO with a 35 cm tail; leave the 12-st neck open.*

### 2. Muzzle (Light Tan — shared)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6) · **R2:** inc around (12) · **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18) · **R4–R5:** sc around (18)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Embroider nostrils and a Y-mouth, knot ends inside. Lightly stuff; sew centred below the eyes over **Goat Head R11–R15** (Lamb: **R12–R16**).*

### 3. Goat Horns (Espresso Brown — Make 2)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4) · **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6) · **R3–R5:** sc around (6) · **R6:** BLO sc around (6) *(bend ridge)* · **R7–R9:** sc around (6) · **R10:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9) · **R11:** sc around (9)
* *Do not stuff. Tack one hidden stitch pinching the inner side of R5 to R7 across the R6 ridge for a backward curve. FO with a 25 cm tail; sew all 9 base stitches to **Head R3–R5**, 4 stitches apart.*

### 4. Layered Ears (shared — 2 Outer + 2 Inner per animal)
*Goat: Cream outer / Tan inner. Lamb: White outer / Pink inner. Use sc2tog on turned rows.*
#### Standard Ear (6 rows, ≈2.7 cm)
* Ch 7. **Row 1 (RS):** Sc in 2nd ch and next 5 ch; ch 1, turn (6)
* **Row 2 (WS):** Sc across; ch 1, turn (6)
* **Row 3 (RS):** Sc2tog, 2 sc, sc2tog; ch 1, turn (4)
* **Row 4 (WS):** Sc across; ch 1, turn (4)
* **Row 5 (RS):** Sc across; ch 1, turn (4)
* **Row 6 (WS):** Sc2tog × 2; FO (2)
#### Optional Long Floppy Ear (8 rows, ≈3.6 cm)
* Ch 8. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch and next 6 ch; ch 1, turn (7)
* **Row 2:** Sc across; ch 1, turn (7)
* **Row 3:** Sc2tog, 3 sc, sc2tog; ch 1, turn (5)
* **Rows 4–5:** Sc across; ch 1, turn (5)
* **Row 6:** Sc2tog, sc, sc2tog; ch 1, turn (3)
* **Row 7:** Sc across; ch 1, turn (3)
* **Row 8:** Sc, sc2tog; FO (2)
* *Assembly: place Inner and Outer wrong sides together and whipstitch the full perimeter. Fold the foundation base in half — **standard ear: 3-st folded edge (≈1.5 cm); long ear: 4 sts on the outer layer / 3 on the inner (≈2 cm fold)** — tack the fold, and sew to **Goat Head R8–R10** below the horns (Lamb: **R9–R11**, angled downward).*

### 5. Goat Body (Warm Cream, bottom-up)
* **R1:** 6 · **R2:** inc around (12) · **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18) · **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24) · **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30) · **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** BLO sc around (36) *(base crease)*
* **R8–R14 (7 rnds):** sc around (36)
* **R15:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30) · **R16–R17:** sc around (30) · **R18:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) *(stuff firmly from here)* · **R19:** sc around (24) · **R20:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) · **R21:** sc around (18) · **R22:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) · **R23:** sc around (12)
* *FO with a 45 cm tail; leave the 12-st neck open.*

### 6. Arms / Front Legs (shared — Make 2)
*Start Light Tan; change to body colour at R5.*
* **R1 (Tan):** 5 sc in MR (5) · **R2:** inc around (10) · **R3:** BLO sc around (10) *(hoof sole)* · **R4:** sc around; change to body colour on last pull-through (10) · **R5:** BLO sc around (10) · **R6–R10:** sc around (10) · **R11:** [3 sc, dec] × 2 (8)
* *Stuff R1–R7 lightly. Flatten the 8-st top into 4 pairs; FO with a 25 cm tail (the top is closed when sewn to the body in Assembly).*

### 7. Back Legs (shared — Make 2)
*Start Light Tan; change to body colour at R6.*
* **R1 (Tan):** 6 sc in MR (6) · **R2:** inc around (12) · **R3:** BLO sc around (12) · **R4–R5:** sc around; change to body colour at end of R5 (12) · **R6:** BLO sc around (12) · **R7–R12:** sc around (12) · **R13:** [sc, dec] × 4 (8)
* *Stuff R1–R8 firmly, R9–R13 lightly. Flatten the 8-st top into 4 pairs; FO with a 25 cm tail.*

### 8. Goat Tail (Warm Cream)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4) · **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6) · **R3–R6:** sc around (6)
* *Do not stuff. FO with a 20 cm tail; sew all 6 open-edge stitches to the centre lower back just above the base crease at **Body R8–R10**, tip angled upward.*

### 9. Measured Diagonal Flame Sash (Scarlet Red, Burnt Orange, Golden Yellow)
*The sample size is 44 sts ≈ 22 cm, but bodies vary — measure first.*
1. On the **stuffed, assembled goat**, wrap a tape diagonally from one upper shoulder across the chest/back to the opposite lower waist and back to the start. Add 1 cm overlap. At gauge (2 sc = 1 cm), your stitch target = measured cm × 2. *(Sample: 44 sts.)*
2. **Foundation:** With Scarlet Red, ch (target + 1). **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across; ch 1, turn (target sts). **Row 2:** Sc in each st across; FO and weave in red ends.
3. **Flame peaks:** Join Burnt Orange in St 1 of Row 2: `ch 3, sl st in same st (peak), [sl st in next 3 edge sts, ch 3, sl st in same 3rd st (peak)]` repeated until 1 edge st remains; sl st in last st, FO. *(44 sts gives exactly 15 peaks at sts 1, 4, 7, …, 43.)*
4. With Golden Yellow, work one small stitch through the tip of each orange peak. Wrap the sash diagonally, overlap the ends at the back, and sew securely to the body.

### 10. Optional Flower Crown (Goat)
* **Flowers (3–4):** Ch 3, sl st to first ch; `[ch 2, 2 dc in ring, ch 2, sl st in ring] × 5`, FO with tail.
* **Leaves (2, Green):** Ch 5; from 2nd ch: sc, hdc, sc, sl st; FO. Sew flowers and leaves between the horns.

## PART C — Assembly (both animals)
1. **Neck seam (12 ↔ 12):** Align Head (Goat R19 / Lamb R20) with Body (Goat R23 / Lamb R24), face forward. Whipstitch all 12 pairs once with the head tail (top up stuffing before the last 2 pairs), then a second reinforcing pass in the opposite direction with the body tail. Knot and bury both tails.
2. **Back Legs:** Sew the flattened 4-pair tops to the lower front/sides across **Goat Body R8–R10** (Lamb **R9–R11**), 4 body stitches between inner edges, legs projecting forward in a seated pose. Sew through both layers of each pair so the tops close as you sew.
3. **Arms:** Sew the flattened 4-pair tops to the upper sides at **Goat Body R19–R21** (Lamb **R20–R22**), angled slightly forward so the front hooves rest above the seated back legs.
4. **Tail:** Goat at **Body R8–R10** angled up; Lamb at **Body R9–R11** angled down.
5. Add sash (Goat) or collar (Lamb); tug-test every seam.
