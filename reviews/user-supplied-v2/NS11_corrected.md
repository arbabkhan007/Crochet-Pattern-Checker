# No-Sew Christmas Gnome — NS11 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** Dual US & UK (`US sc = UK dc` · `US BO = 5-dc bobble / UK 5-tr bobble`)
* **Skill Level:** Easy — one piece, base to hat tip, zero sewing
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 14.5 cm (5.7 in) tall at stated gauge (32 rnds)
* **Gauge:** 10.5 sc × 11 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Worsted (#4):** Colour A Forest Green/Slate (Coat) ~12 g · Colour B Snow White (Beard & Pom-Pom) ~8 g · Colour C Warm Peach/Beige (Nose & Face) ~5 g · Colour D Festive Red (Hat) ~15 g
  * **Notions:** Fiberfill (~25 g), **optional 4.5 cm flexible base disc** (see correction note), stitch marker, tapestry needle

> **Correction in this version:** the base disc is now **4.5 cm and flexible**. The supplied 5.5 cm disc cannot fit: the 30-st base is Ø ≈ 4.55 cm at this gauge, and a rigid 5.5 cm disc also cannot pass the opening. Insert the disc right after R8 (flex/roll it through the open top and press it flat on the base) — not at R12.

### Abbreviations
**MR:** magic ring · **ch:** chain · **sl st:** slip stitch · **sc (UK dc)** · **inc:** 2 sc in same st · **dec:** invisible decrease · **BLO/FLO:** back/front loop only · **BO:** 5-dc bobble (UK 5-tr): 5 incomplete dc (UK tr) in same st, 6 loops on hook, yarn over and pull through all 6, push bobble to the front.

---

### Complete One-Piece Gnome (bottom-up, continuous spiral)
Change colour on the final pull-through of the stitch before the new colour; carry unused yarn loosely inside across the front panel (or cut and knot inside).

| Rnd | US Instruction | UK Instruction | Sts | Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | With **A**: 6 sc in MR | With **A**: 6 dc in MR | (6) | Base |
| **R2** | inc in each st around | inc in each st around | (12) | Base |
| **R3** | [sc, inc] × 6 | [dc, inc] × 6 | (18) | Base |
| **R4** | [2 sc, inc] × 6 | [2 dc, inc] × 6 | (24) | Base |
| **R5** | [3 sc, inc] × 6 | [3 dc, inc] × 6 | (30) | Base |
| **R6** | **BLO** sc in each st around | **BLO** dc in each st around | (30) | Base edge |
| **R7–R8** | sc in each st around *(optional: insert 4.5 cm flexible disc now, flat on the base)* | dc in each st around *(same)* | (30) | Coat |
| **R9** | **A:** 12 sc, **B:** 6 sc, **A:** 12 sc | **A:** 12 dc, **B:** 6 dc, **A:** 12 dc | (30) | Beard tip |
| **R10** | **A:** 11 sc, **B:** 8 sc, **A:** 11 sc | **A:** 11 dc, **B:** 8 dc, **A:** 11 dc | (30) | Beard |
| **R11** | **A:** 10 sc, **B:** 10 sc, **A:** 10 sc | **A:** 10 dc, **B:** 10 dc, **A:** 10 dc | (30) | Beard |
| **R12** | **A:** 9 sc, **B:** 12 sc, **A:** 9 sc | **A:** 9 dc, **B:** 12 dc, **A:** 9 dc | (30) | Beard |
| **R13** | **A:** 9 sc, **C:** 12 sc, **A:** 9 sc *(FO B)* | **A:** 9 dc, **C:** 12 dc, **A:** 9 dc *(FO B)* | (30) | Face |
| **R14** | **A:** 9 sc, **C:** 5 sc, **BO (nose)** in next st, 6 sc, **A:** 9 sc | **A:** 9 dc, **C:** 5 dc, **BO (nose)**, 6 dc, **A:** 9 dc | (30) | Nose |
| **R15** | **A:** 9 sc, **C:** 12 sc, **A:** 9 sc; change to **D** at end *(FO A & C)* | **A:** 9 dc, **C:** 12 dc, **A:** 9 dc; change to **D** *(FO A & C)* | (30) | Face |
| **R16** | With **D — FLO:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 | With **D — FLO:** [4 dc, inc] × 6 | (36) | Hat brim flare |
| **R17** | **BLO** [4 sc, dec] × 6 *(back loops of the R16 stitches — not the reserved R15 loops)* | **BLO** [4 dc, dec] × 6 *(same)* | (30) | Brim turn |
| **R18** | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (30) | Hat |
| **R19** | [3 sc, dec] × 6 *(stuff body firmly, over the disc if used)* | [3 dc, dec] × 6 *(same)* | (24) | Hat taper |
| **R20–R21** | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (24) | Hat |
| **R22** | [2 sc, dec] × 6 | [2 dc, dec] × 6 | (18) | Hat taper |
| **R23–R24** | sc in each st around *(stuff hat lightly)* | dc in each st around *(same)* | (18) | Hat |
| **R25** | [sc, dec] × 6 | [dc, dec] × 6 | (12) | Hat taper |
| **R26–R27** | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (12) | Hat tip |
| **R28** | [2 sc, dec] × 3; change to **B** | [2 dc, dec] × 3; change to **B** | (9) | Pom base |
| **R29** | With **B:** inc in each st around | With **B:** inc in each st around | (18) | Pom-pom |
| **R30** | sc in each st around | dc in each st around | (18) | Pom-pom |
| **R31** | [sc, dec] × 6 *(stuff pom-pom)* | [dc, dec] × 6 *(same)* | (12) | Pom-pom |
| **R32** | dec × 6; FO and close | dec × 6; FO and close | (6) | Finish |

*The colour bands R9–R15 are full-circumference rounds with a front panel in B/C — the beard/face is a centred front section of the full round, as written. The R16 FLO brim leaves the R15 back loops inside the hat; R17 works into the back loops of R16 only, which folds the brim down.*
