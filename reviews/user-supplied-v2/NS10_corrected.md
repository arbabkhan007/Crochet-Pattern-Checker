# Willow the Bunny Lovey — NS10 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Finished Size:** Blanket square ≈ 26 × 26 cm (10.25 × 10.25 in); head height ≈ 5 cm (2 in); ears ≈ 8.5 cm (3.3 in) at stated gauges
* **Gauge:** Head (3.0 mm): 11.5 sc × 13 rnds = 5 cm · Blanket (3.5 mm): 4 granny clusters × 8 rnds = 10 cm
* **Hooks:** 3.0 mm (head & ears), 3.5 mm (blanket)
* **Materials:**
  * **DK (#3) Cotton Yarn:** Yarn A Soft Cream/Oat ~45 g; Yarn B Dusty Rose/Sage ~20 g
  * **Embroidery:** Dark brown & pale pink floss (sleeping eyes & nose — no plastic parts)
  * **Notions:** Fiberfill (~15 g, head only), stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Correction in this version:** the scalloped border now covers **all 9 interior clusters** on each side (the supplied version scalloped only 5 of 9 and left the rest of each side unworked).

---

### 1. Bunny Head (Yarn A — 3.0 mm)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7–R12 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* *Embroider two curved sleeping-eye arcs across **R9–R10**, 5 stitches apart, and a small pink V-nose centred between them over **R10–R11**. Secure all ends inside before stuffing.*
* **R13:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R14:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R15:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R16:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R17:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO with a **50 cm tail**. Weave through front loops of the 6 sts to close, keeping the tail at the bottom of the head for blanket attachment.*

### 2. Ears (Yarn A — 3.0 mm, Make 2)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4–R14 (11 rnds):** sc in each st around (18)
* **R15:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R16–R19 (4 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R20:** [sc, dec] × 4 (8)
* *Do not stuff. Flatten and work **4 sc across both layers** (4). Fold the 4-st edge in half (2 pairs), tack closed, FO with a 30 cm tail, and sew to the crown over **Head R3–R5**, spaced 4 stitches apart.*

### 3. Granny Square Blanket (Yarns A & B — 3.5 mm)
Joined rounds. Each corner is `(3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)`; each side cluster is `3 dc` in the space between clusters of the previous round (no chains between side clusters).
* **R1 (Yarn A):** In MR, ch 3 (counts as dc), 2 dc, [ch 2, 3 dc] × 3, ch 2, sl st to top of ch-3 (4 corner clusters + 4 ch-2 corners)
* **R2 (Yarn A):** Sl st in next 2 dc and into the first corner ch-2 sp; ch 3, (2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same corner sp, [(3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in next corner sp] × 3, sl st to top of ch-3.
* **R3–R11 (9 rnds):** Work `(3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)` in each corner ch-2 sp and `3 dc` in each side space between clusters around.
  * *Stripes:* R1–R3 A · R4–R5 B · R6–R8 A · R9–R10 B · R11 A. *(After R11 each side has 11 clusters including corners — 9 interior clusters per side.)*
* **R12 (Scalloped border — Yarn A):** Sl st into a corner ch-2 sp, then repeat the following 4 times (once per side):
  `[(sc, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, sc) in corner ch-2 sp, (sl st in next space between clusters, 5 dc in middle dc of next interior cluster) × 9, sl st in space before next corner]`
  then sl st to first sc. FO and weave in ends.
  *(Count check per side: 1 corner fan + 9 five-dc scallops, one in every interior cluster, with a sl st in each of the 10 spaces between the 11 clusters.)*

### 4. Assembly
Position the closed bottom of the head over the **centre of the blanket (R1–R2)** so the blanket drapes evenly on all four sides. With the 50 cm head tail, sew in a tight circle uniting **Head R16–R17** to the centre posts of **Blanket R1–R2**, making **three full passes** so the head cannot pull loose. Weave the remaining tail inside the head. Tug-test the head seam before giving the lovey to a child, and re-check it after washing.
