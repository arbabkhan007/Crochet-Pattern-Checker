# Little Duck Plushie — NS05 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Beginner–Easy
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 12–13 cm (4.75–5 in) tall at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 6.25 sc × 7 rnds = 5 cm (2 in) (approx. 8 mm per st, 7 mm per rnd)
* **Hook:** 4.5 mm (US 7)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Pastel Yellow):** Super-Bulky Chenille (#6), ~40 g
  * **Yarn B (Warm Orange):** Super-Bulky Chenille (#6), ~10 g (beak and feet)
  * **Eyes:** Black yarn/floss for embroidered eyes (recommended); or 10–12 mm safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~35 g), stitch marker, wide-eye tapestry needle, scissors
* **Chenille start tip:** If chenille strips in a magic ring, ch 2 and work R1 into the 2nd ch from hook.

> **Safety:** If safety eyes are used, lock washers firmly inside before stuffing and check by hand; embroidered eyes are recommended for children under 3. Sew all parts twice.

---

### 1. Body & Head (Yarn A — bottom-up, one piece)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (or 2nd ch of ch-2) (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6–R9 (4 rnds):** sc in each st around (30)
* **R10:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R11 (Neck):** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff body firmly)*
* **R12:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R13:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R14–R18 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (30)
* *Embroider eyes (or insert safety eyes and lock washers inside) between **R17 and R18**, 7 stitches apart.*
* **R19:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R20:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff head firmly)*
* **R21:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R22:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and weave tail through front loops of remaining 6 sts to close.*

### 2. Tapered Wings (Yarn A — Make 2)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3–R5 (3 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R6:** [sc, dec] × 4 (8)
* *Do not stuff. Flatten the 8-st opening and work **4 sc across both layers** (4) — base is 4 sts ≈ 32 mm wide at gauge. FO with a 25 cm tail and sew vertically to the sides of the body across **R6–R9**.*

### 3. Beak (Yarn B)
* **Foundation:** Ch 5.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 2 sc, 3 sc in last ch; working along opposite side: 2 sc, 2 sc in first working ch, sl st to first sc (10)
* *FO with a 25 cm tail. Pin first, check size against your duck, then sew horizontally across **Head R15–R16**, centred between and just below the R17–R18 eyes.*

### 4. Webbed Feet (Yarn B — Make 2)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9)
* **R3:** 7 sc, dec (8) *(place the decrease at the back/heel side so the front edge stays smooth)*
* **R4 (Webbed toes):** Flatten the 8-st opening into **4 matched pairs**. Working through both layers: `[(sc, hdc, sc) in first pair, sl st in next pair, (sc, hdc, sc) in next pair, sl st in last pair]` to close the front of the foot with two toes.
* *FO with a 25 cm tail. Sew the narrow R1 heel end to the front underside of the body at **R4–R5**, spaced 4 stitches apart, toes pointing forward.*
