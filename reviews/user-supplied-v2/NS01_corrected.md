# Hamish the Highland Cow — NS01 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 14–15 cm (5.5–6 in) seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 12 rnds = 5 cm (2 in) in spiral single crochet
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Ginger / Caramel):** Worsted (#4), ~35 g (includes fringe)
  * **Yarn B (Oat Cream):** Worsted (#4), ~12 g (muzzle, horns, inner ears, belly patch)
  * **Yarn C (Dark Chocolate):** Worsted (#4), ~5 g (hooves, nostril embroidery)
  * **Yarn D (Rust — optional):** Worsted (#4), ~6 g (scarf)
  * **Eyes:** Two 12 mm black safety eyes (or black embroidery floss)
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~35 g), stitch marker, tapestry needle, pins, scissors

> **Safety:** Safety eyes and sewn/knotted parts can detach. Lock washers firmly from inside before stuffing, sew every seam twice, weave ends at least 5 cm, and check all parts by hand. Not suitable for children under 3 unless the finished item meets the toy-safety requirements of your market; embroidered eyes remove the plastic-eye hazard only.

### Abbreviations (US)
**MR:** magic ring · **ch:** chain · **sl st:** slip stitch · **sc:** single crochet · **hdc:** half double crochet · **inc:** 2 sc in same st · **dec:** invisible decrease · **BLO:** back loop only · **FO:** fasten off

---

### 1. Head (Yarn A)
Worked top-down in a continuous spiral.
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8:** [6 sc, inc] × 6 (48)
* **R9–R15 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (48)
* *Insert 12 mm safety eyes between **R10 and R11**, 7 stitches apart centre-to-centre. Lock washers firmly inside.*
* **R16:** [6 sc, dec] × 6 (42)
* **R17:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36)
* **R18:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R19:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* *Stuff head firmly. FO after R19 with a 45 cm sewing tail. Leave the 24-stitch neck opening unclosed.*

### 2. Muzzle (Yarn B)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5–R6 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* **R7:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* *FO with a 35 cm sewing tail. Embroider two diagonal nostrils (Yarn C) on the upper front and secure tails inside. Lightly stuff and flatten into a wide horizontal oval; sew centred below the eyes over **Head R13–R16**, following the curve of the head (the visible face is about 8 sts wide). Check placement against your sample before sewing permanently.*

### 3. Body (Yarn A)
Worked bottom-up in a continuous spiral.
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8:** [6 sc, inc] × 6 (48)
* **R9:** BLO sc in each st around (48) *(flat sitting base edge)*
* **R10–R16 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (48)
* **R17:** [6 sc, dec] × 6 (42)
* **R18:** sc in each st around (42)
* **R19:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36)
* **R20:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R21:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* *Stuff firmly, packing the flat base so it sits level. FO with a 45 cm tail; leave the 24-stitch neck open.*

### 4. Belly Patch (Yarn B — Optional)
* **Foundation:** Ch 6.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 3 sc, 3 sc in last ch; working along opposite side of chain: 3 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (12)
* **R2:** inc, 3 sc, inc × 3, 3 sc, inc × 2 (18)
* **R3:** sc, inc, 3 sc, [sc, inc] × 3, 3 sc, [sc, inc] × 2, sl st to first sc (24)
* *FO with a 30 cm sewing tail. Sew vertically, long axis upright, to the centre front of the body over **R10–R17** so the top of the patch stops at the first decrease round.*

### 5. Legs (Start with Yarn C, change to Yarn A)
Choose **Option A** (4 splayed legs: make 4 Standard Legs) or **Option B** (upright sit: 2 Standard Back Legs + 2 Shortened Front Legs).

#### Standard Legs
* **R1 (Yarn C):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** BLO sc in each st around (18) *(flat hoof sole)*
* **R5:** sc in each st around (18)
* **R6:** sc in each st around; change to **Yarn A** on the last pull-through (18)
* **R7 (Yarn A):** BLO sc in each st around (18) *(clean colour transition & hoof ridge)*
* **R8:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) *(stuff hoof firmly)*
* **R9–R13 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 3 (9) *(stuff upper leg lightly)*
* **R15–R16 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (9)
* *FO with a 35 cm sewing tail; leave 9-st top open.*

#### Shortened Front Legs (Option B only)
* Work **R1–R8** of Standard Legs (12).
* **R9–R10 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* *Stuff hoof firmly and R9–R10 lightly. FO with a 35 cm tail; leave 12-st top open.*

### 6. Flat Layered Ears (Make 2 Outer in Yarn A + 2 Inner in Yarn B)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* *Inner Ear (Yarn B): FO and weave in ends. Outer Ear (Yarn A): do not FO. Mark the first stitch of each layer, place one Inner and one Outer wrong sides together (cream facing front) with markers aligned, and work **18 sc in Yarn A** through corresponding stitches of both layers around the perimeter. FO with a 30 cm tail. Pinch 3 bottom stitches together, tack closed to cup the ear, and sew to **Head R8–R10** behind and outside the eyes.*

### 7. Horns (Yarn B — Make 2)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (6)
* **R5:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
* **R6–R7 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (8)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Stuff tip lightly. For a gentle upward curve, make one tiny hidden stitch pinching R4 and R5 on the inner side. Sew all 8 base stitches to **Head R6–R8** just above and forward of the ears.*

### 8. Tail & Fringe (Yarn A)
* **Tail:** Cut 6 strands of Yarn A (22 cm each). Attach with lark's-head knots into 2 adjacent stitches at the centre back on **Body R10**. Braid the 12 hanging ends for 4 cm, tie an overhand knot, trim tassel to 1.5 cm.
* **Fringe:** Cut 36–40 strands of Yarn A (14 cm each). Attach with lark's-head knots in a horseshoe across the forehead on **Head R6, R7 and R8** between the horns and ears. Trim into a V-fringe just above the muzzle.

### 9. Scarf (Yarn D — Optional)
* **Foundation:** Ch 92.
* **Row 1:** Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across; ch 2 (does not count as st), turn (90)
* **Row 2:** Hdc in each st across; FO and weave in ends (90)
* *Try the scarf on the finished cow; add or remove rows if you want a longer drape.*

### 10. Assembly
1. Align the 24 open stitches of **Head R19** with the 24 open stitches of **Body R21**, centring the face forward. Whipstitch through all 24 matched stitch pairs twice around (use the head tail for one pass and the body tail for the second), topping up neck stuffing before closing the final 3 pairs.
2. **Front Legs:** Pin centred over **Body R9–R11** on the front chest, leaving 8 body stitches between their inner edges. Angle forward so the hooves rest flat; sew around the open rim twice, catching every rim stitch.
3. **Back Legs:** Pin over **Body R9–R11** on the rear flanks, angled outward so the hooves project past the front legs on the table. Sew around the open rim twice. Check the cow sits level before final tightening.
