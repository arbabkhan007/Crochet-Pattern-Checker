# Momo the Loaf Cat — NS06 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 10.5 cm (4.2 in) long × 7.5 cm (3 in) wide × 7.5 cm (3 in) tall at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 11.5 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Ginger, Calico, or Grey):** Worsted (#4), ~30 g
  * **Yarn B (White):** Worsted (#4), ~4 g (chest & tucked-paw embroidery)
  * **Yarn C (Pink):** Scrap for nose
  * **Eyes:** Two 8 mm black safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~25 g), 2 stitch markers, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** Lock safety-eye washers firmly inside before stuffing and check by hand; safety eyes are a choking hazard if detached. Not for children under 3 unless the finished item meets your market's toy-safety requirements.

> **Correction in this version:** R5 was rewritten — the supplied line consumed 36 sts and produced 40 against a 44-st round. The line below consumes 44 and produces 48, with the increase pairs on the narrow ends.

---

### 1. Oval Loaf Body (Yarn A)
Worked bottom-up from an oval foundation chain.
* **Foundation:** Ch 13.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 10 sc, 3 sc in last ch; working along opposite side: 10 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (26)
* **R2:** inc, 10 sc, inc × 3, 10 sc, inc × 2 (32)
* **R3:** sc, inc, 10 sc, [sc, inc] × 3, 10 sc, [sc, inc] × 2 (38)
* **R4:** 2 sc, inc, 10 sc, [2 sc, inc] × 3, 10 sc, [2 sc, inc] × 2 (44)
* **R5:** 11 sc, inc, sc, inc, 11 sc, inc, sc, inc, 16 sc (48) *(the two inc–sc–inc groups should fall on the narrow end curves; if your round start puts them on a long side, work to an end curve before starting this sequence and adjust the final plain-sc run to end the round)*
* **R6:** sc in each st around (48)
* **R7:** BLO sc in each st around (48) *(flat bottom edge)*
* **Orientation:** Fold the oval lengthwise and place a marker **in the space at the exact centre of the narrow front curve** (between the two middle stitches — Face Centre line) and likewise at the narrow back curve (Tail Centre line). Count ear/eye positions outward from these centre lines.
* **R8–R13 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (48)
* *Insert 8 mm safety eyes at the front curve between **R11 and R12**, in the **3rd stitch to the left and the 3rd stitch to the right of the Face Centre line** (6 sts apart centre-to-centre). Lock washers inside.*
* **R14:** [6 sc, dec] × 6 (42)
* **R15:** [5 sc, dec] × 6 (36)
* **R16:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R17:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) *(stuff firmly, keeping the BLO base flat)*
* **R18:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* **R19:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R20:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and weave tail through front loops of remaining 6 sts to close.*

### 2. Surface-Crocheted Ears (Yarn A — worked onto the head)
Hold the cat facing you. Ear bases occupy stitches **3–6 out from the Face Centre line** on each side, leaving the 2 stitches nearest the line on each side (4 stitches total) unworked between the ears.
* **Right Ear:** Join Yarn A with a sl st into the surface of **R14 at the 3rd stitch to the right** of the Face Centre line; ch 1:
  * **Row 1:** Work **4 sc** diagonally up and outward from R14 to R15, ending at the 6th stitch to the right; ch 1, turn (4)
  * **Row 2:** Sc2tog × 2; ch 1, turn (2)
  * **Row 3:** Sc2tog; ch 1 to lock, FO and weave in tail (1)
* **Left Ear:** Join Yarn A with a sl st into **R15 at the 6th stitch to the left** of the Face Centre line; ch 1. Work **4 sc** diagonally down and inward to R14, ending at the **3rd stitch to the left**; then repeat Rows 2–3. The ears now mirror each other, with 4 unworked front stitches between their inner (3rd-stitch) edges.

### 3. Tail (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2–R11 (10 rnds):** sc in each st around (6)
* *Do not stuff. Flatten and work **3 sc across both layers** (3). FO with a 25 cm tail. Sew the tail base to the narrow back curve at **R8–R9**, curve the tail around one lower flank, and tack the tip against **R8–R9** on that side.*

### 4. Face, Chest & Tucked-Paw Embroidery
1. **Nose & mouth:** Embroider a pink triangle nose centred on the Face Centre line between the eyes over **R10–R11**, with two small curved mouth lines below it at R10.
2. **Chest & paws (Yarn B):** Embroider a vertical satin-stitch oval on the centre chest over **R8–R9**, flanked by two small horizontal white ovals along the bottom front edge (**R7–R8**) for the tucked paws.
