# Shelby the Sea Turtle Bag Charm — NS09 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Finished Size at stated gauge:** Standard Shelby ≈ **3.5 cm** long; Bigger Shelby ≈ **5.5 cm** long (shell Ø ≈ 2.7 cm / 4.8 cm plus head fan — the previously claimed 6.0 / 8.5 cm are not achievable at this gauge; measure your sample)
* **Gauge:** 14 sc × 15 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 2.5 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **DK (#3) Cotton Yarn:**
    * *Standard:* Shell (Yarn A, ~4 m / 1.5 g), Body (Yarn B, ~5 m / 1.5 g)
    * *Bigger:* Shell (Yarn A, ~9 m / 3 g), Body (Yarn B, ~10 m / 3.5 g)
  * **Eyes:** Black floss for two French-knot eyes
  * **Notions:** Pinch of fiberfill (~2–5 g), lobster-clasp keyring, stitch marker, tapestry needle

---

### Option 1: Standard Shelby (24-st shell)

#### Top Shell Dome (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5–R6 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around, sl st to first sc (24)
* *FO with a **40 cm sewing tail**.*

#### Underside, Head & Flippers (Yarn B)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5 (Head & 4 flippers — FLO):** With the right side of the disc facing you, work every stitch in **front loops only**, leaving all 24 back loops free on the inside for the shell seam:
  `3 sc, (sc, hdc, hdc, sc) in next st [Head — st 4], 4 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Front Left Flipper — st 9], 3 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Back Left Flipper — st 13], 5 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Back Right Flipper — st 19], 3 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Front Right Flipper — st 23], 1 sc, sl st to first sc` (**35 sts** worked across the 24 front loops; closing sl st not counted)
* *Symmetry check: 4 sc between Head and each front flipper, 3 sc between front and back flippers on each side, 5 sc across the rear. FO and weave in end.*

---

### Option 2: Bigger Shelby (42-st shell)

#### Top Shell Dome (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7:** [5 sc, inc] × 6 (42)
* **R8–R10 (3 rnds):** sc in each st around, sl st to first sc (42)
* *FO with a **55 cm sewing tail**.*

#### Underside, Head & Flippers (Yarn B)
* Work **R1–R7** as for the Bigger Top Shell (42).
* **R8 (Head & 4 flippers — FLO):**
  `7 sc, (sc, hdc, hdc, sc) in next st [Head — st 8], 7 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Front Left — st 16], 7 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Back Left — st 24], 9 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Back Right — st 34], 7 sc, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st [Front Right — st 42], sl st to first sc` (**53 sts** across the 42 front loops)
* *FO and weave in end.*

---

### Assembly & Keyring Loop (both sizes)
1. Embroider two French-knot eyes on the upper side of the 4-st Head fan.
2. Place Top Shell and Underside **wrong sides together** (shell RS up, underside RS down).
3. With the Yarn A shell tail, whipstitch each of the 24 (or 42) shell edge stitches to the 24 (or 42) **exposed inside back loops** of the underside. The seam sits behind the FLO head/flippers without folding them.
4. Stuff lightly before closing the final 5 stitches.
5. **Hanging loop:** Join Yarn B at the tip of the Head, ch 12 through the keyring clasp ring, sl st back into the same stitch, FO, weave tails inside the shell. Tug-test the loop before use.
