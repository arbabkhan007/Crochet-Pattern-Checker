# Pocket Positivity Trio (Sunny, Waddle & Spud) — NS07 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Beginner–Easy
* **Finished Size:** Each pal approx. 4.0–4.5 cm (1.6–1.8 in) tall at stated gauge
* **Gauge:** 14 sc × 15 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 2.5 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **DK (#3) Cotton Yarn — the per-pal amounts below total ≈ 11.5 g** (metreage varies by brand, typically ≈ 23–29 m for DK; weigh a finished pal before estimating yarn for multiples):
    * *Sunny:* Soil Brown (~2 g), Sunflower Yellow (~2 g)
    * *Waddle:* Charcoal/Black (~3 g), Cream/White (~1 g), Orange (~1 g)
    * *Spud:* Golden Tan (~2.5 g)
  * **Eyes:** Three pairs of 5 mm black safety eyes, or embroidery floss (see safety note)
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~10 g total), stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** These are 4 cm miniatures with 5 mm plastic eyes and tiny sewn parts — choking hazards. Embroidered eyes are strongly recommended for young children. If safety eyes are used, lock washers before stuffing and check every part by hand; do not offer to children under 3 unless the finished items meet your market's toy-safety requirements.

### Pal 1: Sunny the Pocket Sunflower

#### Brown Centre Cushion (Soil Brown)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** BLO sc in each st around (18) *(leaves all 18 R3 front loops exposed around the perimeter)*
* *Insert 5 mm safety eyes (or embroider) between **R2 and R3**, 4 stitches apart. Embroider a tiny smile and pink blush.*
* **R5:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) *(stuff lightly)*
* **R6:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and weave tail through front loops of remaining 6 sts to close.*

#### Sunflower Petals (Sunflower Yellow)
* Hold the cushion with the **R1 front face (eyes) toward you**.
* Join Yellow with a sl st into any exposed front loop of **R3**.
* **Petals:** Into each of the 18 front loops around: `[(sc, hdc, dc, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook, dc, hdc, sc) in first loop, sl st in next loop] × 9` (9 pointed petals).
* *Sl st to the base of the first petal, FO, weave ends into the back.*

### Pal 2: Waddle the Pocket Penguin

#### Body (Charcoal/Black)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4–R8 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (18)
* *Before stuffing: insert 5 mm safety eyes between **R3 and R4**, 4 stitches apart, and lock washers inside (or embroider).*
* **R9:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R10:** dec × 6 (6) *(worked through both loops so the base closes cleanly with no loop ridge)*
* *FO and weave tail through front loops of the 6 sts to close flat.*

#### Chest Patch (Cream/White)
* **Foundation:** Ch 5.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 2 sc, 3 sc in last ch; opposite side: 2 sc, 2 sc in first working ch, sl st to first sc (10)
* *FO with a 20 cm tail. Sew vertically to the front belly over **Body R5–R8**, directly below the eye line.*

#### Beak (Orange)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* *FO with a 15 cm tail. Sew centred between the eyes over **Body R4**, just above the top edge of the chest patch.*

#### Flippers (Charcoal/Black — Make 2)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3:** sc in each st around (6)
* *Flatten and work **3 sc across both layers** (3). FO with a 15 cm tail; sew to the sides at **R5–R6**.*

#### Feet (Orange — Make 2)
* In MR: ch 1, (sc, hdc, sc), sl st in MR; pull ring tight. FO with a 15 cm tail; sew to the front bottom edge at **R9–R10**.

### Pal 3: Spud the Positive Potato

#### Potato Body (Golden Tan)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4–R5 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (18)
* **R6:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R7–R9 (3 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* *Insert 5 mm safety eyes between **R4 and R5**, 4 stitches apart (or embroider). Embroider a smile, blush, and 4–5 tiny brown French-knot “potato eyes” on sides/back.*
* **R10:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* **R11:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R12:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and close the remaining 6 sts.*

#### Tiny Hands (Golden Tan — Make 2, optional)
* Ch 4; sc in 2nd ch from hook and next 2 ch (3). FO with a 15 cm tail; sew to the front sides at **R6–R7** angled inward (to hold a mini paper sign if wished).
