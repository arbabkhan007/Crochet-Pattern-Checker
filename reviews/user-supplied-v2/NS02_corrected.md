# Kawaii Halloween Mini Set (Boo, Pip & Bramble) — NS02 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Easy–Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Each character approx. 4.5–5.0 cm (1.8–2.0 in) tall at stated gauge
* **Gauge:** 14 sc × 15 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 2.5 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **DK (#3) Cotton Yarn:** White (~8 g), Orange (~8 g), Charcoal/Black (~10 g), Purple (~4 g), Pale Pink (~2 g), Leaf Green (~2 g), Brown (~2 g)
  * **Eyes:** Three pairs of 6 mm black safety eyes (plus black & white embroidery floss)
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~15 g total), stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** These are miniatures with small plastic eyes and sewn parts — choking hazards. Lock washers firmly, sew parts twice, and do not offer to children under 3 unless the finished items meet your market's toy-safety requirements. Embroidered eyes are the safer option for young children.

### Character 1: Boo the Ghost

#### Ghost Body (White)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5–R10 (6 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* *Insert 6 mm safety eyes between **R6 and R7**, 4 stitches apart. Embroider a tiny black mouth between the eyes and pink blush bars below.*
* **R11 (Frill — FLO):** Working in the **front loops only** of R10's stitches (the 24 back loops stay free inside for the base): `[(sc, hdc, dc, hdc, sc) in next st, sl st in next st] × 12` (12 scallops). FO and weave in ends.

#### Flat Base (White)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Lightly stuff the ghost dome (~3 g), place the R4 disc against the inside of the skirt, and whipstitch its 24 stitches to the 24 unworked back loops of R10.*

#### Arms (White — Make 2)
* **R1:** 5 sc in MR (5)
* **R2–R3 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (5)
* *Do not stuff. Flatten the opening with **3 stitches on the top layer and 2 on the bottom layer**, so the 5th (unpaired) stitch sits at one fold. Work **2 sc through the two matched pairs**, then work **1 sl st through the unpaired fold stitch and the edge of the first pair** to close the fold. FO with a 15 cm tail and sew to the sides of the body at **R7–R8**, catching the fold stitch in the seam.*

#### Mini Witch Hat (Purple, with Orange Band)
* **R1 (Purple):** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
* **R4:** [3 sc, inc] × 2; change to **Orange** on last pull-through (10)
* **R5 (Orange):** sc in each st around; change to **Purple** on last pull-through (10)
* **R6 (Purple — FLO):** [sc, inc] × 5 (15)
* **R7:** [2 sc, inc] × 5, sl st to first sc (20)
* *FO with a 25 cm tail and sew slightly tilted onto the top of Boo's head over **R1–R4**.*

### Character 2: Pip the Pumpkin

#### Pumpkin Body (Orange)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7–R11 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* *Face: place eyes between **R8 and R9**, spaced 3 stitches apart within one 6-stitch segment between increase columns.*
* **R12:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R13:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R15:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R16:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO, leaving a **60 cm tail**. Close the 6-stitch opening, then wrap the tail vertically around the outside 6 times, aligned with the 6 increase columns of R6, passing the needle up through the centre bottom and down through the centre top each time to cinch 6 ribs. Secure and bury the tail.*

#### Stem (Brown)
* **R1:** 5 sc in MR (5)
* **R2:** BLO sc in each st around (5)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (5)
* **R5:** inc in each st around, sl st to first sc (10)
* *FO with a 20 cm tail. Do not stuff; sew the 10-stitch flared base over the top centre dimple.*

#### Symmetrical Leaf (Leaf Green)
* **Foundation:** Ch 7.
* **R1 (RS):** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 4 sc, 3 sc in last ch (tip); working along opposite side of chain: 4 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (14)
* **R2 (RS — do not turn):** Sc in next 4 sts (up Side A), inc in each of next 3 sts (around the tip), sc in next 4 sts (down Side B), sl st in next st; leave last 2 base sts unworked (15)
* *FO with a 15 cm tail and sew the leaf base tight against the base of the stem.*

### Character 3: Bramble the Bat

#### Head-and-Body (Charcoal/Black)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6–R12 (7 rnds):** sc in each st around (30)
* *Insert 6 mm safety eyes between **R8 and R9**, 5 stitches apart. With white floss, embroider a tiny smile and two V-fangs between the eyes.*
* **R13:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R15:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R16:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and weave the tail through the front loops of the 6 remaining stitches to close.*

#### Ears (Make 2 Outer in Charcoal + 2 Inner in Pale Pink)
* **Outer Ear (Charcoal):**
  * **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
  * **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
  * **R3:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
  * **R4:** [3 sc, inc] × 2 (10)
  * *Flatten and work **5 sc across both layers** (5). FO with a 20 cm tail.*
* **Inner Ear (Pale Pink — flat single layer):**
  * **Foundation:** Ch 4.
  * **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and next 2 ch; ch 1, turn (3)
  * **Row 2:** Sc3tog; FO with a 15 cm tail (1)
  * *Sew the pink triangle onto the front of the charcoal ear, then sew the 5-stitch ear base to the top of the head over **R3–R5**.*

#### Bat Wings (Charcoal — Make 2)
* **Foundation:** Ch 13.
* **Row 1:** Starting in 2nd ch from hook: **dc in next 4 ch, hdc in next 4 ch, sc in last 4 ch**; ch 1, turn (12) *(tall dc at outer wingtip; short sc at inner body edge)*
* **Row 2:** **Sc in first 3 sts, hdc in next 4 sts, dc in last 5 sts**; ch 1, turn (12) *(gradient: short sts at inner edge building to tall sts at wingtip)*
* **Row 3 (Scalloped lower edge — from outer wingtip back to inner edge):** `[(sc, hdc, dc, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook, dc, hdc, sc) in next st, sl st in next 3 sts] × 3` (3 pointed scallops)
* *FO, leaving a 20 cm tail at the inner sc edge. Sew the **short vertical row-end edge at the narrow sc end** (Rows 1–3) to the sides of the body vertically across **R9–R11**.*
