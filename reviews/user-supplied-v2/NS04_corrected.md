# Coco the Capybara — NS04 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate (join-as-you-go legs)
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 9.5–10 cm (3.75–4 in) tall seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 11.5 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.0 mm
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Warm Cinnamon / Camel Brown):** Worsted (#4), ~30 g
  * **Yarn B (Dark Espresso Brown):** Worsted (#4), ~6 g (muzzle, ears, foot tips)
  * **Yarn C (Orange & Leaf Green — optional):** Scrap (~3 g) for mini tangerine
  * **Eyes:** Two 8 mm black safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~25 g), stitch markers, tapestry needle, scissors

> **Safety:** Lock safety-eye washers firmly inside before the head is closed and sew all parts twice. Safety eyes are a choking hazard if detached; not for children under 3 unless the finished item meets your market's toy-safety requirements.

> **Corrections in this version:** leg tops now close to an exact 3-stitch strip (decrease to 6, then flatten), and the R4/R5 join map was rebuilt around marked centre lines so the front legs no longer overlap a back leg. Follow the stitch-position map exactly.

---

### 1. Back Legs (Make 2 — before the Body)
* **R1 (Yarn B):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3; change to **Yarn A** on last pull-through (9)
* **R3–R7 (5 rnds — Yarn A):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R8:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6) *(stuff paw lightly before this round)*
* **R9:** Flatten the 6-st opening and work **3 sc through both layers** (3). FO and weave in the short end. Set aside for Body R4.

### 2. Front Legs (Make 2 — before the Body)
* **R1 (Yarn B):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3; change to **Yarn A** on last pull-through (9)
* **R3–R8 (6 rnds — Yarn A):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R9:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6) *(stuff lower half lightly before this round)*
* **R10:** Flatten the 6-st opening and work **3 sc through both layers** (3). FO. Set aside for Body R5.

### 3. Body & Head (Yarn A — bottom-up, one piece)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* *Before R4, note the stitch positions: R4 joins the back legs over R3 sts 1–3 and 8–10 (rear). The rear-pair centre falls between R4 sts 6 and 7; the **front centre is R4 st 19** — mark it when you reach it in R4 and carry the marker up for the face.*
* **R4 (join Back Legs — rear):** Work 3 sc through both the 3 sts of **BL1** and R3 sts 1–3; [sc, inc] × 2 (R3 sts 4–7); work 3 sc through both the 3 sts of **BL2** and R3 sts 8–10; [sc, inc] × 4 (R3 sts 11–18) (24)
* **R5 (join Front Legs — front, centred opposite the back pair):** [sc, inc] × 4, 5 sc (R4 sts 1–13); work 3 sc through both the 3 sts of **FL1** and R4 sts 14–16; 3 sc, inc (R4 sts 17–20); work 3 sc through both the 3 sts of **FL2** and R4 sts 21–23; inc (R4 st 24) (30)
  *Check: FL1/FL2 sit at R4 sts 14–16 and 21–23, centred on front-centre st 19, clear of both back legs (R4 sts 1–3 and 10–12).*
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7–R11 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* **R12:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R13 (Neck):** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) *(stuff lower body firmly)*
* **R14:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R15:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R16–R20 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* *Centre the face on the front-centre marker line, directly above the front-leg pair. Insert 8 mm safety eyes between **R17 and R18**, 9 stitches apart, symmetric about the marker.*
* **R21:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R22:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) *(stuff head firmly)*
* **R23:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* **R24:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R25:** dec × 6 (6)
* *FO and weave tail through front loops of remaining 6 sts to close.*

### 4. Muzzle (Yarn B)
* **Foundation:** Ch 6.
* **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 3 sc, 3 sc in last ch; opposite side: 3 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (12)
* **R2:** inc, 3 sc, inc × 3, 3 sc, inc × 2 (18)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (18)
* *FO with a 30 cm tail. Lightly stuff and sew vertically between the eyes over **R15–R19**. With light tan/cream scrap, embroider a vertical line with an inverted V at the bottom of the muzzle.*

### 5. Ears (Yarn B — Make 2)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9)
* **R3:** sc in each st around (9)
* **R4:** 7 sc, dec (8)
* *Flatten and work **4 sc across both layers** (4). FO with a 20 cm tail and sew to **R20–R21** slightly behind and above the eyes.*

### 6. Mini Tangerine (Optional — Yarn C)
* **R1 (Orange):** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** sc in each st around (12)
* **R4:** dec × 6 (6)
* *Stuff lightly, close the opening, add a tiny 3-chain green leaf at the top, and tack onto the crown (R24–R25).*
