# Ember the Baby Dragon — NS08 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** US Crochet Terms
* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Finished Size:** Approx. 12–13 cm (4.75–5 in) tall seated at stated gauge — confirm on a sample
* **Gauge:** 11 sc × 11.5 rnds = 5 cm (2 in)
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (US E-4)
* **Materials:**
  * **Yarn A (Crimson Red or Emerald Green):** Worsted (#4), ~30 g
  * **Yarn B (Warm Gold / Mustard):** Worsted (#4), ~12 g (horns, belly patch, dorsal spikes)
  * **Eyes:** Two 9 mm black safety eyes
  * **Notions:** Polyester fiberfill (~25 g), stitch marker, tapestry needle, pins, scissors

> **Safety:** Lock safety-eye washers firmly inside before the head is closed; sew all parts twice. Not for children under 3 unless the finished item meets your market's toy-safety requirements.

> **Correction in this version:** the dorsal spike strip was lengthened (21 → 29 sts) so it can actually run from crown to tail with its 7 spikes; the original strip was ~3.5 cm too short for that path.

---

### 1. Head (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6 (36)
* **R7–R11 (5 rnds):** sc in each st around (36)
* *Insert 9 mm safety eyes between **R8 and R9**, 7 stitches apart.*
* **R12:** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R13:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24) *(stuff firmly)*
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* *FO with a 35 cm sewing tail; leave the 18-stitch neck opening unclosed.*

### 2. Snout (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* *FO with a 25 cm tail. Lightly stuff, embroider two nostrils on top, and sew centred below the eyes over **Head R9–R12**.*

### 3. Horns (Yarn B — Make 2)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3–R4 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (6)
* **R5:** [2 sc, inc] × 2 (8)
* *FO with a 20 cm tail. Lightly stuff tip; sew to the crown over **Head R3–R5**, spaced 5 stitches apart.*

### 4. Body (Yarn A, bottom-up)
* **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** BLO sc in each st around (30) *(flat sitting base)*
* **R7–R9 (3 rnds):** sc in each st around (30)
* **R10:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R11–R12 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (24)
* **R13:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18)
* **R14:** sc in each st around (18)
* *Stuff firmly, keeping R1–R6 flat. FO and sew the 18-st Body R14 opening to the 18-st Head R14 opening, stitch for stitch.*

### 5. Legs & Arms (Yarn A)
* **Back Legs (Make 2):**
  * **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6) · **R2:** inc in each st around (12) · **R3–R5:** sc around (12) · **R6:** [sc, dec] × 4 (8)
  * *Stuff lightly, flatten top, work **4 sc across both layers** (4). FO with a 20 cm tail. Sew to lower sides of body at **R7–R8**, angled forward.*
* **Front Arms (Make 2):**
  * **R1:** 6 sc in MR (6) · **R2:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9) · **R3–R7:** sc around (9) · **R8:** [sc, dec] × 3 (6)
  * *Stuff lower paw only, flatten top, work **3 sc across both layers** (3). FO with a 20 cm tail; sew to upper sides of body at **R11–R12**.*

### 6. Tail (Yarn A)
* **R1:** 4 sc in MR (4)
* **R2:** [sc, inc] × 2 (6)
* **R3:** sc in each st around (6)
* **R4:** [sc, inc] × 3 (9)
* **R5–R6 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (9)
* **R7:** [2 sc, inc] × 3 (12)
* **R8–R9 (2 rnds):** sc in each st around (12)
* **R10:** [3 sc, inc] × 3 (15)
* *Stuff lightly. FO with a 25 cm tail; sew the 15-st open base to the centre lower back over **Body R6–R9** so the tail rests on the table as a third tripod point.*

### 7. Wings (Yarn A — Make 2)
* **Foundation:** Ch 11.
* **Row 1 (RS):** Starting in 2nd ch from hook: **sc in first 4 ch, hdc in next 3 ch, dc in last 3 ch**; ch 1, turn (10) *(inner edge = sc, wingtip = dc)*
* **Row 2 (WS):** **Dc in first 4 sts, hdc in next 3 sts, sc in last 3 sts**; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 3 (RS):** **Sc in first 2 sts, hdc in next 4 sts, dc in last 4 sts**; ch 1, turn (10)
* **Row 4 (scalloped lower edge, wingtip → inner edge):** `[(sc, hdc, dc, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook, dc, hdc, sc) in next st, sl st in next 2 sts] × 3, sl st in last st` (3 wing points)
* *FO with a 25 cm tail at the inner sc edge. Sew the short vertical row-end edge at the narrow sc end to the upper back over **Body R11–R13**, leaving 4 stitches between the wings for the spike strip.*

### 8. Dorsal Spike Strip & Belly Patch (Yarn B)
* **Dorsal Spike Strip (7 spikes, lengthened):**
  * **Foundation:** Ch 30.
  * **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across; ch 1, turn (29)
  * **Row 2:** `[(hdc, dc, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook, dc, hdc) in next st, sl st in next 3 sts] × 7, sc in last st` (7 spikes, at sts 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25)
  * *FO with a 40 cm sewing tail. Pin the flat Row-1 base edge along the centre spine from the crown (**Head R2**) down the neck, between the wings, and along the top of the tail to its tip (2 spikes on head, 2 on back, 3 on tail). Check the strip reaches the tail tip on your sample before sewing; sew securely.*
* **Belly Patch:**
  * **Foundation:** Ch 6.
  * **R1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, 3 sc, 3 sc in last ch; opposite side: 3 sc, 2 sc in first working ch (12)
  * **R2:** inc, 3 sc, inc × 3, 3 sc, inc × 2, sl st to first sc (18)
  * *FO with a 25 cm tail; sew vertically to the front belly over **Body R7–R13**.*
