# Christmas Ornament Bundle — NS13 (Corrected)

* **Terminology:** Dual US & UK (`US sc = UK dc` · `US hdc = UK htr` · `US dc = UK tr` · `US tr = UK dtr`)
* **Skill Level:** Beginner–Easy
* **Finished Sizes:** Bauble ≈ 6 cm diameter · Star ≈ 7.5 cm point-to-point · Snowflake ≈ 8.5–9 cm tip-to-tip
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm (worsted #4) or 3.0 mm (DK #3)
* **Materials:** Small amounts of worsted/DK in Red, Gold/Yellow, Snow White (~8–12 g per ornament); fiberfill (~8 g, bauble only); stitch marker, tapestry needle, blocking pins

---

### Ornament A: Classic Striped Bauble (continuous spiral)
* **R1 (Red):** 6 sc in MR | *UK: 6 dc* (6)
* **R2:** inc in each st around (12)
* **R3:** [sc, inc] × 6 (18)
* **R4:** [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24)
* **R5:** [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30)
* **R6:** [4 sc, inc] × 6; change to **White** on last pull-through (36)
* **R7–R8 (White):** sc in each st around; change to **Gold** at end of R8 (36)
* **R9 (Gold):** sc in each st around; change to **White** at end (36)
* **R10–R11 (White):** sc in each st around; change to **Red** at end of R11 (36)
* **R12 (Red):** [4 sc, dec] × 6 (30)
* **R13:** [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24)
* **R14:** [2 sc, dec] × 6 *(stuff firmly into a sphere)* (18)
* **R15:** [sc, dec] × 6 (12)
* **R16:** dec × 6; change to **Gold** on last pull-through (6)
* **R17–R18 (Gold cap):** BLO sc in each st around on R17, then both loops on R18 (6)
* **Cap closure & hanging loop:** The cap is still an open 6-st tube after R18. Flatten the top of the cap, and working through both layers, sl st in each of the 3 pairs to close it. Then join yarn (or use the working tail before fastening off) at the centre of the closed cap top: **ch 16, sl st into the same centre point** to form the hanging loop. FO and weave in ends.

### Ornament B: Five-Point Star (joined rounds)

| Rnd | US Instruction | UK Instruction | Sts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | Gold: MR, ch 2 (does not count), **10 hdc** in ring, sl st to first hdc | Gold: MR, ch 2 (does not count), **10 htr**, sl st to first htr | (10) |
| **R2** | Ch 1, **2 sc** in each st around, sl st to first sc | Ch 1, **2 dc** in each st around, sl st to first dc | (20) |
| **R3 (5 points)** | The R2 join sl st in St 1 is the first valley anchor: `[skip next st, (dc, tr, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook [picot], tr, dc) in next st, skip next st, sl st in next st] × 4`, skip St 18, `(dc, tr, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook, tr, dc)` in St 19, skip St 20, sl st into St 1 (same st as R2 join) | Same with UK terms: `(tr, dtr, ch 2, sl st in 2nd ch from hook [picot], dtr, tr)` | 5 points; all 20 R2 sts used |
| **Loop** | Join yarn at one picot tip, ch 16, sl st in same tip, FO; block flat | Same | – |

### Ornament C: Six-Point Lace Snowflake (joined rounds)

| Rnd | US Instruction | UK Instruction | Sts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | White: MR, ch 3 (counts as dc), **11 dc** in ring, sl st to top of ch-3 (St 1) | White: MR, ch 3 (counts as tr), **11 tr**, sl st to top of ch-3 | (12) |
| **R2** | `[Ch 5, skip next st, sl st in next st] × 6` — the 6th sl st lands in St 1 at the base of the first ch-5; do **not** add an extra sl st | Same | 6 ch-5 spaces |
| **R3 (6 pointed arms)** | In each ch-5 space: `(sl st, ch 3, 2 tr, ch 3, sl st in 3rd ch from hook [central picot], 2 tr, ch 3, sl st)`; sl st to first sl st, FO | In each ch-5 space: `(sl st, ch 3, 2 dtr, ch 3, sl st in 3rd ch from hook [picot], 2 dtr, ch 3, sl st)`; FO | 6 pointed arms |
| **Finish** | Pin all 6 picot tips, mist/block flat, tie a 16 cm hanging loop through one picot | Same | – |
