# Celestial Wyvern — Corrected

> Corrected edition. Validates with 0 defects (`verify_wyvern.py --fixed`).
> See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and the reasoning behind it.

**Skill Level:** Advanced

**Finished Size:** Approx. 8½ in / 22 cm tall, with an 8 in / 20 cm wingspan

**Gauge:** 20 sts x 22 rows = 4 in (10 cm) in single crochet, flat, with a 3.5 mm hook.
Worked in the round, Rounds 1–5 of a flat circle should measure approx. 2 in (5 cm) across.

## Materials

- Worsted weight yarn:
  - **Color A — Navy:** approx. 150 g / 300 yds (head, torso, tail)
  - **Color B — Gold:** approx. 25 g / 50 yds (neck)
  - **Color C — Silver:** approx. 50 g / 100 yds (wings)
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing
- Stitch markers
- Tapestry needle
- **2 x 9 mm safety eyes** (12 mm reads oversized on a head this size)

## Abbreviations (US terms)

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `tr` = treble crochet
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `ch` = chain
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## Notes — read before starting

1. **All pieces except the Wings are worked in continuous spirals. Do not join, do not turn.**
   Mark the first stitch of each round and move the marker up as you go.
2. The Wings are worked in flat, turned rows. The `ch 1` at the start of each row does **not**
   count as a stitch.
3. Stitch counts are given in brackets at the end of every round. Check them every round.
4. Stuff each piece firmly as directed. Stuffing prompts are placed at the last point where the
   piece is still open enough to reach.

## 1. Head & Neck

*(Worked from the snout tip back to the nape, then continuing into the neck, in a continuous spiral)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Rounds 5-9:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

> Insert the 9 mm safety eyes between Rounds 6 and 7, 5 sts apart, and secure the backs.

**Round 10:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

> **Stuff the head firmly.** Shape the snout as you go.

**Round 12:** Switch to Color B, working in BLO: [sc 3, inc] 3 times. (15 sts)

**Rounds 13-16:** sc in each st around. (15 sts)

> For a longer, more serpentine neck, repeat Rounds 13-16 once or twice more.

**Round 17:** [sc 4, inc] 3 times. (18 sts)

> **Stuff the neck firmly** — an unstuffed neck is what makes the head flop.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing to the body.

## 2. Leg & Foot Assembly (make 2)

### Claws / Toes (make 3 per leg, 6 in total)

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Round 3:** sc in each st around. (6 sts)

Fasten off Toes 1 and 2. **Do NOT fasten off Toe 3** — its working yarn begins the joining round.

> Stuff each toe very lightly before joining; they seal shut in the next round.

### Joining the Toes into the Foot & Leg

Arrange the three toes side by side in the order Toe 3, Toe 2, Toe 1. The joining round walks the
outer perimeter, wrapping around Toe 1 at the far end.

**Round 4 (joining):** sc 3 across Toe 3, sc 3 across Toe 2, sc 6 across Toe 1, sc 3 across Toe 2,
sc 3 across Toe 3. (18 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 4, dec] 3 times. (15 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 3, dec] 3 times. (12 sts)

**Rounds 7-10:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 2, dec] 3 times. (9 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for closing the crotch later. **Stuff the leg firmly.**

## 3. Torso / Body

*(Worked bottom to top)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Rounds 7-12:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

### Joining the Legs to the Body

Hold each leg against the body with its 9 open stitches facing up. Six of those stitches — the
outer side — are worked into the round; the remaining three sit against the body and are sewn to
the three skipped body stitches afterwards.

**Round 13 (joining):** sc 15 across Body, sc 6 across the outer side of Leg 1, **skip the next
3 Body sts**, sc 15 across Body, sc 6 across the outer side of Leg 2, **skip the next 3 Body sts**.
(42 sts)

> Body stitches: 15 + 3 skipped + 15 + 3 skipped = 36 ✔  Stitches made: 15 + 6 + 15 + 6 = 42 ✔
>
> **Now close the crotch.** Using each leg's long tail, sew that leg's 3 remaining stitches to the
> 3 skipped Body stitches beside it. Do this before Round 14 while the gap is still easy to reach.

**Round 14:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

**Round 15:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Rounds 16-18:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 20:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

> **Stuff the torso firmly**, packing extra stuffing up into the shoulders.

**Round 21:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Top up the stuffing before sewing the neck on.

## 4. Wings (make 2)

*(Worked in flat, turned rows. Enlarged from the original so the wing reads as a wing and so there
are enough row ends to carry the feather edging.)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 22, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across, turn. (21 sts)

**Row 2:** Ch 1, sc 19, dec, turn. (20 sts)

**Row 3:** Ch 1, dec, sc 18, turn. (19 sts)

**Row 4:** Ch 1, sc 17, dec, turn. (18 sts)

**Row 5:** Ch 1, dec, sc 16, turn. (17 sts)

**Row 6:** Ch 1, sc 15, dec, turn. (16 sts)

**Row 7:** Ch 1, dec, sc 14, turn. (15 sts)

**Row 8:** Ch 1, sc 13, dec, turn. (14 sts)

**Row 9:** Ch 1, dec, sc 12, turn. (13 sts)

**Row 10:** Ch 1, sc 11, dec, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 11:** Ch 1, dec, sc 10, turn. (11 sts)

**Row 12:** Ch 1, sc 9, dec. (10 sts) **Do not turn** — continue directly into the edging.

> Work every row through **both loops**. The original alternated FLO and BLO, which loosens the
> fabric; a wing needs to hold its shape. If you want the ridged texture, work *every* row FLO so
> it is at least consistent.

### Wing Feather Edging

Worked down the **shaped (decreasing) row-end edge** — the side where the decreases stack up.
There are 12 row ends; each feather fan spans 3 of them, giving 4 fans.

**Edging:** Ch 1, then working into the row ends:
\*[hdc, dc, tr] in next row end, [tr, dc, hdc] in next row end, sl st in next row end\*;
repeat from \* 3 more times. (4 feather fans over 12 row ends)

Fasten off, leaving a tail for attachment.

> The sl st between fans is the anchor that stops the edge ruffling. Do not omit it.

## 5. Tail

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Rounds 2-5:** sc around. (4 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Rounds 7-10:** sc around. (6 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Rounds 12-15:** sc around. (8 sts)

> **Start stuffing the tail now**, a little at a time, and keep topping it up as you work. The
> tip stays unstuffed so it can curl.

**Round 16:** [sc 3, inc] 2 times. (10 sts)

**Round 17:** [sc 4, inc] 2 times. (12 sts)

**Rounds 18-20:** sc around. (12 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

---

## 6. Assembly

1. **Head / Neck to Torso.** The neck's Round 17 (18 sts) and the torso's Round 21 (18 sts) match
   exactly. Line the two openings up, pin at four points, and whip stitch all the way around,
   adding stuffing through the gap before you close the last few stitches.
2. **Wings.** Sew the straight foundation-chain edge of each wing (Row 1, 21 sts) flat against the
   back of the torso over Rounds 16-18, with the two wings 4 sts apart either side of the spine.
   The feather edging should face down and outwards.
3. **Tail.** Sew the open Round 20 edge (12 sts) to the back of the torso between Rounds 4 and 7,
   centred on the spine, angling the tail down and back.
4. **Finishing.** Check the crotch seam from Round 13 is fully closed and invisible; top up the
   stuffing anywhere the fabric looks slack; with Color B, embroider a small nostril on each side
   of the snout at Round 2; weave in all remaining ends.
