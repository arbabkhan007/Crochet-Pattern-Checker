# Celestial Wyvern

**Skill Level:** Advanced

**Gauge:** 20 sts x 22 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in single crochet with 3.5 mm hook

## Materials

- Worsted Weight Yarn (Color A: Navy, Color B: Gold, Color C: Silver)
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing, stitch markers, tapestry needle, 12 mm safety eyes

## Abbreviations

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `tr` = treble crochet
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `ch` = chain
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## 1. Head & Neck

*(Worked top to snout, then transitioning to neck in continuous spiral)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5–9:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

*Insert 12 mm safety eyes between Rounds 6 and 7, 5 sts apart.*

**Round 10:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

*Stuff head firmly.*

**Round 12:** Switch to Color B in BLO, [sc 3, inc] 3 times. (15 sts)

**Round 13–16:** sc in each st around. (15 sts)

**Round 17:** [sc 4, inc] 3 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off, leave long tail for sewing to body.

## 2. Leg & Foot Assembly (Make 2)

### Claws/Toes (Make 3 per leg = 6 total)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Round 3:** sc around. (6 sts)

Fasten off for Toes 1 & 2. **Do NOT fasten off Toe 3.**

### Joining Toes into Foot & Leg

**Round 4 (Joining):** sc 3 across Toe 3, sc 3 across Toe 2, sc 6 across Toe 1, sc 3 across Toe 2, sc 3 across Toe 3. (18 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 4, dec] 3 times. (15 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 3, dec] 3 times. (12 sts)

**Round 7–10:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 2, dec] 3 times. (8 sts)

Fasten off and stuff leg.

## 3. Torso / Body

*(Worked bottom to top)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 7–12:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

### Joining Legs to Body

**Round 13:** sc 10 across Body, sc 6 across inner side of Leg 1, sc 12 across Body, sc 6 across inner side of Leg 2, sc 8 across Body. (42 sts)

**Round 14:** sc around all sts. (42 sts)

**Round 15:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 16–18:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 20:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off and stuff torso.

## 4. Wings (Make 2)

*(Worked in flat rows)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 16, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across, turn. (15 sts)

**Row 2:** Ch 1, FLO [sc 13, dec], turn. (14 sts)

**Row 3:** Ch 1, BLO [dec, sc 12], turn. (13 sts)

**Row 4:** Ch 1, FLO [sc 11, dec], turn. (12 sts)

**Row 5:** Ch 1, [sc 10, dec], turn. (11 sts)

### Wing Feather Edging

Working along the row ends:

**Row 6:** *[sl st, hdc, dc, tr, dc, hdc, sl st]* into first row end, repeat across remaining row ends.

Fasten off leaving a tail for attachment.

## 5. Tail

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Round 2–5:** sc around. (4 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Round 7–10:** sc around. (6 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Round 12–15:** sc around. (8 sts)

**Round 16:** [sc 3, inc] 2 times. (10 sts)

**Round 17–20:** sc around. (12 sts)

Fasten off.

## 6. Assembly

1. Attach Head/Neck (Round 17 of Neck) to top opening of Torso (Round 21 of Torso).
2. Attach Wings to Rows 16–18 on back of Torso, 4 sts apart.
3. Attach Tail to base of Torso between Rounds 4–7.
