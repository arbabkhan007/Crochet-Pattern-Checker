# Abyssal Leviathan — Corrected

> Corrected edition. Validates with 0 defects (`verify_leviathan.py --fixed`).
> See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and the reasoning behind it.

**Skill Level:** Grandmaster / Experimental

**Finished Size:** Approx. 9 in / 23 cm head-to-tail, with 4 in / 10 cm primary tentacles

**Gauge:** 24 sts x 26 rnds = 4 in (10 cm) in sc with a 3.0 mm hook

> ⚠️ **Not a children's toy.** This pattern contains internal craft wire. See the wire note in
> Assembly before you begin.

## Materials

- Fingering weight yarn:
  - **Color A — Midnight Blue:** approx. 80 g / 340 yds
  - **Color B — Bio-Luminescent Teal:** approx. 15 g / 65 yds
  - **Color C — Deep Purple:** approx. 20 g / 85 yds
- 3.0 mm crochet hook (go down to 2.75 mm if your fabric is open enough to show stuffing)
- Polyfill stuffing — **dark grey or black if you can get it**; white ghosts through these colours
- Stitch markers
- Tapestry needle
- **2 x 14 mm safety eyes**
- 1.5 mm craft wire, approx. 24 in, plus round-nose pliers

## Abbreviations & Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `fpdc` = front post double crochet
- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## Notes — read before starting

1. **All pieces except the Dorsal Fin are worked in continuous spirals.** Do not join, do not turn,
   except where a row is explicitly labelled as a turned short row.
2. Mark the first stitch of every round.
3. Stitch counts are in brackets at the end of each round. Check them every round.
4. Three joins in this pattern are stated as three numbers — **worked / skipped / held**. Confirm
   *worked + skipped = the body's previous round* and *held = skipped* before you continue.

## 1. Cranial Vault & Curved Mantle

*(Worked snout tip back toward the mantle opening, in a continuous spiral)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Rounds 8-13:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

> **Insert the 14 mm safety eyes between Rounds 10 and 11, 9 sts apart**, set low and forward on the
> snout. Secure the backs before stuffing.

**Round 14:** hdc in each st around. (42 sts)

> This round is hdc, not sc, on purpose — it is what gives Round 15's post stitches a post tall
> enough to wrap. A round of sc has no usable post.

### Bio-Luminescent Ridge (front post alignment)

**Round 15:** Switch to Color B. [sc 5, **fpdc** 1 around the post of the corresponding Round 14
st] 7 times. (42 sts)

> `fpdc`, not `bpdc` — a **front** post stitch stands the ridge proud on the outside of the work.
> A back post stitch would push it to the inside of the stuffed head. Work the 5 sc through both
> loops.

**Round 16:** Switch to Color A, sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Short-Row Brow Crest

*These three rows work over only 16 of the 42 sts. The other 26 are held for Round 18.*

**Row 17a (partial):** sc 16, turn. (16 sts)

**Row 17b (partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 12, dec, turn. (14 sts)

**Row 17c (partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 10, dec, turn. (12 sts)

**Round 18 (rejoining round):** sc 12 across Row 17c, **3 sc down the first raw row-end edge**,
sc 26 across the unworked Round 16 sts, **3 sc up the second raw row-end edge**. (44 sts)

> Perimeter check: 12 live + 26 held + (3 × 2) raw row-ends = 44 ✔. Those two sets of 3 sc are what
> close the brow; skipping them leaves a gap at each corner.

**Round 19:** [sc 4, dec] 4 times, [sc 3, dec] 4 times. (36 sts)

> Consumes 4×6 + 4×5 = 44 ✔, makes 4×5 + 4×4 = 36 ✔. A plain `[sc 5, dec] x6` only eats 42 of the 44.

**Round 20:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

> **Stuff the cranial vault firmly**, keeping stuffing clear of the bio-luminescent ridge.

**Round 22:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for assembly.

## 2. Bifurcating Primary Tentacle (make 2)

### Base Trunk — starts OPEN

**Round 1:** With Color A, ch 12, join with sl st to form a ring, being careful not to twist.
**Leave this end open — it is the body socket.** (12 sts)

**Rounds 2-20:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

> Thread the craft wire in now, before the fork. Feed it up the trunk so a few inches protrude from
> the open base. Stuff the trunk lightly around the wire as you go.

### Branching Split

**Round 21 (fork round):** sc 6, **ch 2, skip 6 sts**. Continue into the 1st st of Round 21 to form
the Branch Alpha loop. (8 sts)

> Consumes all 12 trunk sts — 6 worked, 6 skipped and held for Branch Beta. The ch-2 bridge is
> shared: Alpha works its top, Beta its underside.

**Branch Alpha**

**Rounds 22a-28a:** sc in the 6 base sts, sc 2 across the ch-2 bridge. (8 sts)

**Round 29a:** [dec] 4 times. (4 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

**Branch Beta**

Attach Color A to the 6 skipped sts of Round 21.

**Rounds 22b-28b:** sc in the 6 skipped sts, sc 2 across the underside of the ch-2 bridge. (8 sts)

**Round 29b:** [sc 2, dec] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Round 30b:** [dec] 3 times. (3 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

## 3. Secondary Lateral Arms (make 4)

*This piece was already correct; only stuffing and tail length have changed.*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Rounds 3-15:** sc in each st around. (8 sts)

> Run a 3 in length of wire up the arm and stuff very lightly around it — at 0.42 in across these
> will not hold a curve otherwise. Leave this end open; it is the body socket.

Fasten off, leaving a **10 in tail** for the gusset seam.

## 4. Segmented Abyssal Body & Tentacle Hub

*(Worked bottom to top, with six integrated limb sockets)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Round 8:** [sc 6, inc] 6 times. (48 sts)

**Rounds 9-14:** sc in each st around. (48 sts)

### Multi-Socket Limb Integration

Hold each limb against the body with its open end facing up. You work across the limb's **outer**
(outward-facing) stitches; the smaller **inner** group is held and sewn afterwards to the body
stitches you skip beneath it.

**Round 15:**

- sc 4 across Body
- sc 8 across the **outer** edge of Primary Tentacle 1 *(4 inner sts held)*, **skip 4 Body sts**
- sc 4 across Body
- sc 5 across the **outer** edge of Arm 1 *(3 held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 5 across the **outer** edge of Arm 2 *(3 held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 12 across Body
- sc 5 across the **outer** edge of Arm 3 *(3 held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 5 across the **outer** edge of Arm 4 *(3 held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 4 across Body
- sc 8 across the **outer** edge of Primary Tentacle 2 *(4 held)*, **skip 4 Body sts**
- sc 4 across Body. **(64 sts)**

> **The three-number check.**
> Body worked 28 + Body skipped 20 = **48** = Round 14 ✔
> Limb sts held: 2×4 + 4×3 = **20** = Body skipped **20** ✔
> Made: 28 + (2×8) + (4×5) = **64** ✔
>
> **Close the sockets now.** Using each limb's tail, sew its held sts to the skipped Body sts
> directly beneath it — 4 to 4 for each tentacle, 3 to 3 for each arm. Do this before Round 16.

**Round 16:** sc in each st around. (64 sts)

**Round 17:** [sc 6, dec] 8 times. (56 sts)

**Round 18:** [sc 5, dec] 8 times. (48 sts)

**Rounds 19-21:** sc in each st around. (48 sts)

**Round 22:** [sc 6, dec] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Round 23:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)

> **Stuff the body firmly**, feeding the tentacle wires down into the body cavity as you go.

**Round 24:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 25:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 26:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off. Top up the stuffing before attaching the head.

## 5. Dorsal Fin Sail

*(Flat rows. Extended from 5 rows to 12 so the sail reads as a sail and so the webbing has enough
row ends to be spaced properly.)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 21, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across, turn. (20 sts)

**Row 2:** Ch 1, FLO sc 18, dec, turn. (19 sts)

**Row 3:** Ch 1, BLO dec, sc 15, dec, turn. (17 sts)

**Row 4:** Ch 1, FLO sc 16, inc, turn. (18 sts)

**Row 5:** Ch 1, [sc 4, dec] 3 times, turn. (15 sts)

**Row 6:** Ch 1, sc 13, dec, turn. (14 sts)

**Row 7:** Ch 1, dec, sc 12, turn. (13 sts)

**Row 8:** Ch 1, sc 11, dec, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 9:** Ch 1, dec, sc 10, turn. (11 sts)

**Row 10:** Ch 1, sc 9, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 11:** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, turn. (9 sts)

**Row 12:** Ch 1, sc 7, dec. (8 sts) **Do not turn** — continue into the webbing.

### Fin Webbing Edging

Worked down the **shaped (decreasing) row-end edge** — the side the decreases stack on, opposite the
foundation chain. There are 12 row ends; each repeat spans 2, giving 6 webbed scallops.

**Edging:** Ch 1, then working into the row ends:
\*sl st in next row end, [hdc, dc, hdc] in next row end\*; repeat from \* 5 more times. (24 sts)

> Fullness check: 24 sts ÷ 6 sts/in = 4.00 in of edging over a 12 ÷ 6.5 = 1.85 in edge = **2.2×**,
> right in the 2–2.5× range a shell edging wants. The original's 5 sts into *every* row end came to
> 5.4×, which bunches instead of webbing. The sl st between fans is the anchor — do not omit it.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail.

## 6. Assembly & Interlocking Directives

1. **Wire.** Cut two 12 in lengths of 1.5 mm craft wire. **Turn a small closed loop in both ends of
   every piece of wire with round-nose pliers before inserting it** — an un-capped 1.5 mm end will
   work its way through fingering-weight fabric within a few bends. Feed one length up through each
   Primary Tentacle and down into the body cavity, and bind the loops to the body's inner wall with
   a few stitches so they cannot migrate. Wire the four Lateral Arms the same way with shorter
   lengths. **Because of the wire, this is a display piece, not a child's toy.**
2. **Limb sockets.** If not already done at Round 15, sew each limb's held sts to the skipped Body
   sts beneath it — 4 to 4 for each tentacle, 3 to 3 for each arm. Every pair matches stitch for
   stitch.
3. **Head.** Attach the Cranial Vault opening (Round 22, 18 sts) to the top opening of the Body
   (Round 26, 18 sts). The counts match exactly. Pin at four points and whip stitch around, adding
   stuffing before you close the last few sts.
4. **Dorsal Fin.** Sew the fin's foundation edge (Row 1, 20 sts = 3.33 in) to the back of the Body
   **across Rounds 6–26** (21 rounds = 3.23 in). Rounds 8–18 span only 1.69 in and would force you
   to gather the fin into a ruffle.
5. **Finishing.** Confirm all six socket seams are closed and invisible; check no wire end is
   palpable through the fabric; with Color B, embroider two or three small bio-luminescent spots
   along each flank; weave in all remaining ends.
