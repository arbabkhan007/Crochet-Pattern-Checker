# Abyssal Leviathan

**Skill Level:** Grandmaster / Experimental

**Gauge:** 24 sts x 26 rnds = 4 inches (10 cm) in sc with 3.0 mm hook

## Materials

- Fingering Weight Yarn (Color A: Midnight Blue, Color B: Bio-Luminescent Teal, Color C: Deep Purple)
- 3.0 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing, tapestry needle, 14 mm safety eyes, 1.5 mm craft wire

## Abbreviations & Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `bpdc` = back post double crochet
- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## 1. Cranial Vault & Curved Mantle

*(Worked snout tip back toward the mantle opening in continuous spiral)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)
**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)
**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)
**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)
**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)
**Round 8-14:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Bio-Luminescent Ridge (Front Post Alignment)

**Round 15:** Switch to Color B in FLO, [sc 5, bpdc 1 in previous round] 7 times. (42 sts)
**Round 16:** Switch to Color A, sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Short-Row Brow Crest

**Row 17a (Partial):** sc 16, turn. (16 sts)
**Row 17b (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 12, dec, turn. (14 sts)
**Row 17c (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 10, dec, turn. (12 sts)
**Round 18 (Rejoining Round):** sc 12 across Row 17c, sc 3 down raw row-end edge, sc 26 across unworked Round 16 sts, sc 3 up raw row-end edge. (44 sts)
**Round 19:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)
**Round 20:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 21:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

*Stuff cranial vault firmly.*

**Round 22:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off.

## 2. Bifurcating Primary Tentacle (Make 2)

### Base Trunk

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 8 sc. (8 sts)
**Round 2:** [sc 3, inc] 2 times. (10 sts)
**Round 3-12:** sc around. (10 sts)
**Round 13:** [sc 4, inc] 2 times. (12 sts)
**Round 14-20:** sc around. (12 sts)

### Branching Split (Splitting into Branch Alpha & Branch Beta)

**Round 21 (Fork Round):** sc 6, ch 2, skip 6 sts. Continue into 1st st of Round 21 to form Branch Alpha loop. (8 sts)

Branch Alpha:

**Round 22a-28a:** sc in 6 base sts, sc 2 across ch-2 bridge. (8 sts)
**Round 29a:** [dec] 4 times. (4 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

Branch Beta:

Attach Color A to the 6 skipped sts of Round 21.

**Round 22b-28b:** sc in 6 skipped sts, sc 2 across underside of ch-2 bridge. (8 sts)
**Round 29b:** [sc 2, dec] 2 times. (6 sts)
**Round 30b:** [dec] 3 times. (3 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

## 3. Secondary Lateral Arms (Make 4)

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)
**Round 2:** [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)
**Round 3-15:** sc around. (8 sts)

Fasten off. Leave 2-inch tail for attachment.

## 4. Segmented Abyssal Body & Tentacle Hub

*(Worked bottom to top with 6 integrated limb sockets)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)
**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)
**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)
**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)
**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)
**Round 8:** [sc 6, inc] 6 times. (48 sts)
**Round 9-14:** sc in each st around. (48 sts)

### Multi-Socket Tentacle Integration

**Round 15:**

- sc 4 across Body
- sc 4 across inner edge of Primary Tentacle 1 (leaving 8 sts outer)
- sc 4 across Body
- sc 3 across inner edge of Arm 1 (leaving 5 sts outer)
- sc 3 across inner edge of Arm 2 (leaving 5 sts outer)
- sc 12 across Body
- sc 3 across inner edge of Arm 3 (leaving 5 sts outer)
- sc 3 across inner edge of Arm 4 (leaving 5 sts outer)
- sc 4 across Body
- sc 4 across inner edge of Primary Tentacle 2 (leaving 8 sts outer)
- sc 4 across Body. (54 sts)

**Round 16:** sc in all 54 sts around (working across outer remaining sts of attached limbs). (54 sts)
**Round 17:** [sc 7, dec] 6 times. (48 sts)
**Round 18-20:** sc around. (48 sts)
**Round 21:** [sc 6, dec] 6 times. (42 sts)
**Round 22:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)
**Round 23:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 24:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 25:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off and stuff body.

## 5. Dorsal Fin Sail

*(Worked in flat rows)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 21, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across, turn. (20 sts)
**Row 2:** Ch 1, FLO sc 18, dec, turn. (19 sts)
**Row 3:** Ch 1, BLO dec, sc 15, dec, turn. (17 sts)
**Row 4:** Ch 1, FLO sc 15, inc, turn. (18 sts)
**Row 5:** Ch 1, [sc 4, dec] 3 times, turn. (15 sts)

### Fin Webbing Edging

**Row 6:** Working down raw row ends: *[sl st, hdc, dc, hdc, sl st]* into each row end across. (25 sts)

Fasten off.

## 6. Assembly & Interlocking Directives

1. Insert 1.5 mm craft wire through Primary Tentacles and Body before stuffing.
2. Attach Head/Neck opening (Round 22 of Cranial Vault, 18 sts) to top opening of Body (Round 25 of Body, 18 sts).
3. Sew Dorsal Fin Sail foundation row (Row 1, 20 sts) to back of Body across Rounds 8-18.
4. Cinch unworked inner gap stitches of Secondary Arms to Body sockets using yarn tails.
