# Classic Amigurumi Bear — Corrected

> Corrected edition. Validated with 0 errors by `StitchCountValidator` and by the independent
> verifier in `verify_bear.py`. See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and why it was made.

**Skill Level:** Easy / Beginner

**Finished Size:** Approx. 5 in / 12.5 cm tall

**Gauge:** Not critical, but your fabric must be dense enough that the stuffing does not show
through. Aim for about 4 sc per inch with the hook below.

## Materials

- Worsted weight yarn (Aran / #4) in brown — approx. 100 g / 200 yds
- Small amount of black yarn, for embroidering the eyes and nose
- 4.0 mm crochet hook (deliberately smaller than the yarn label suggests, for a dense fabric)
- Polyester fibrefill stuffing
- Stitch marker
- Tapestry needle

## Terms & Abbreviations (US terms)

- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = increase (2 sc in same stitch)
- `dec` = decrease (invisible decrease)
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `[ ] x N` = repeat everything in brackets N times

## Notes — read before you start

1. **All pieces are worked in continuous spiral rounds. Do not join, do not chain up, do not turn.**
2. Place a stitch marker in the first stitch of each round and move it up as you go.
3. The stitch count at the end of each round is given in brackets. Check it every round.
4. Head and Body are worked as **one piece**, starting at the top of the head. Watch for the
   stuffing prompts — once the neck closes you cannot easily reach the head again.

## Pattern Instructions

### Head & Body (worked in one piece, make 1)

Start at the top of the head.

**Round 1:** Magic ring with 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] x 6. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] x 6. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] x 6. (30 sts)

**Rounds 6-10:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

> Attach the muzzle and ears and embroider the eyes now, while the head is still open and easy to
> work on. See Assembly.

**Round 11:** [sc 3, dec] x 6. (24 sts)

**Round 12:** [sc 2, dec] x 6. (18 sts)  ← *neck*

> **Stuff the head firmly now.** Shape it round as you go. Do not continue until the head is full —
> after the next few rounds you will not be able to reach it.

**Round 13:** [sc 2, inc] x 6. (24 sts)

**Round 14:** [sc 3, inc] x 6. (30 sts)

**Rounds 15-19:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

**Round 20:** [sc 3, dec] x 6. (24 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 2, dec] x 6. (18 sts)

> **Stuff the body firmly**, pushing extra stuffing into the neck to keep the head from flopping.
> Top up the stuffing again after Round 22.

**Round 22:** [sc 1, dec] x 6. (12 sts)

**Round 23:** [dec] around. (6 sts)

Fasten off leaving a 6 in tail. Thread the tail onto the tapestry needle, run it through the front
loop of each of the 6 remaining stitches, pull tight to cinch the hole closed, and weave the end
into the body.

### Ears (make 2)

**Setup:** Magic ring.

**Round 1:** 6 sc into the magic ring, pull the ring closed. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [sc 1, inc] x 3. (9 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 2, inc] x 3. (12 sts)

**Round 4:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a 10 in tail for sewing. Do not stuff — flatten the ear before attaching.

### Muzzle (make 1)

**Round 1:** Magic ring with 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 3, inc] x 3. (15 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a 10 in tail for sewing.

### Arms (make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring with 5 sc. (5 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (10 sts)

**Rounds 3-8:** sc in each st around. (10 sts)

> Stuff the arm lightly — arms should stay soft and floppy.

**Round 9:** [dec] x 5. (5 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a 10 in tail for sewing.

### Legs (make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring with 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] x 6. (18 sts)

**Rounds 4-8:** sc in each st around. (18 sts)

> Stuff the leg firmly.

**Round 9:** [sc 1, dec] x 6. (12 sts)

**Round 10:** [dec] x 6. (6 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a 10 in tail for sewing.

## Assembly

Do steps 1-3 **before** you reach Round 11 of the Head & Body, while the head is still open.

1. **Muzzle** — centre it on the front of the head over Rounds 7-9. Pin it, then whip stitch all the
   way around, adding a small pinch of stuffing just before you close the last few stitches.
2. **Nose and mouth** — with black yarn, embroider a small triangular nose across the top of the
   muzzle, then a short vertical line down from it and a shallow V for the mouth.
3. **Eyes** — with black yarn, embroider or French-knot the eyes on Round 8, **7 stitches apart**,
   sitting just above and either side of the muzzle.
4. **Ears** — flatten each ear and sew the open edge to the head along Rounds 4-5, about
   9 stitches apart across the top of the head. Curve them slightly forward.
5. **Arms** — sew to the sides of the body at Round 15, just below the neck, 12 stitches apart.
6. **Legs** — sew to the bottom front of the body across Rounds 19-20, about 6 stitches apart so
   they do not overhang the body.
7. Weave in all remaining ends.

## Optional: better-proportioned variant

The pattern above keeps the original design, in which the head and body are the same width (both
30 sts). For a more classic teddy silhouette — head about 1.2x the body — replace the Head & Body
section with the following. This variant also validates with 0 errors.

```
Round 1:      Magic ring with 6 sc.        (6 sts)
Round 2:      [inc] around.                (12 sts)
Round 3:      [sc 1, inc] x 6.             (18 sts)
Round 4:      [sc 2, inc] x 6.             (24 sts)
Round 5:      [sc 3, inc] x 6.             (30 sts)
Round 6:      [sc 4, inc] x 6.             (36 sts)
Rounds 7-12:  sc in each st around.        (36 sts)   <- attach face here
Round 13:     [sc 4, dec] x 6.             (30 sts)
Round 14:     [sc 3, dec] x 6.             (24 sts)
Round 15:     [sc 2, dec] x 6.             (18 sts)   <- neck; STUFF THE HEAD NOW
Round 16:     [sc 2, inc] x 6.             (24 sts)
Round 17:     [sc 3, inc] x 6.             (30 sts)
Rounds 18-22: sc in each st around.        (30 sts)
Round 23:     [sc 3, dec] x 6.             (24 sts)
Round 24:     [sc 2, dec] x 6.             (18 sts)   <- stuff body firmly
Round 25:     [sc 1, dec] x 6.             (12 sts)
Round 26:     [dec] around.                (6 sts)
```

With this variant, also consider dropping the legs to 12-15 sts around and shortening the arms to
Rounds 3-6, so the limbs suit the body.
