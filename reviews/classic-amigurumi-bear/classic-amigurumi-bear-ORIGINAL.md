# Classic Amigurumi Bear

**Skill Level:** Easy / Beginner

**Gauge:** Not important

## Materials

- 1 skein worsted weight yarn (Brown)
- 4.0 mm crochet hook
- Stuffing
- Tapestry needle

## Terms & Abbreviations

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = increase (2 sc in same stitch)
- `dec` = decrease (invisible decrease)
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)

## Pattern Instructions

### Head & Body (Worked in one piece)

**Round 1:** Magic ring with 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6–10:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 12:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 13:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 14–18:** sc around. (30 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 20:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 22:** [dec] around. (6 sts)

Fasten off and weave in ends.

### Ears (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.

**Round 2:** 6 sc into ring. (6 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc, inc] 3 times. (9 sts)

**Round 4:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

### Muzzle

**Round 1:** Magic ring, sc 6. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 3, inc] 3 times. (15 sts)

Fasten off.

### Arms (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 5 sc. (5 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (10 sts)

**Round 3–8:** sc in each st around. (10 sts)

**Round 9:** [dec] 3 times, sc 4. (6 sts)

Fasten off.

### Legs (Make 2)

**Round 1:** Magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4–8:** sc around. (18 sts)

**Round 9:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 10:** [dec] 6 times. (6 sts)

Fasten off.

## Assembly

Sew ears to head. Sew muzzle to front of head. Sew arms and legs to body. Embroider eyes with black yarn.
