> Corrected edition. Validates with 0 defects (`verify_astral.py --fixed`).
> See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and the reasoning behind it.
>
> **Only 11 lines changed.** Sections 1 (except Row 13c) and 2 are reproduced essentially as

**Classification:** Super-Extreme Nightmare Class

**Gauge:** 26 sts × 28 rnds = 4 in (10 cm) in sc with a 2.5 mm hook

**Finished Dimensions:** Approximately **9 in (23 cm)** long including tentacles, **4 in (10 cm)**
wingspan.

> The original claimed 18 in / 12 in. At the stated fingering-weight gauge the pieces give 9.3 in
> and 3.7 in. **To hit the original 18 in, work the identical pattern in worsted weight on a 5.5 mm
> hook** — that is a 1.94× linear scale-up and every stitch count stays the same.

## Materials

- Fingering weight yarn:
  - **Color A — Midnight Blue:** approx. 70 g / 300 yds

  - **Color C — Void Black:** approx. 20 g / 85 yds

- Polyfill stuffing

- **2 × 8 mm safety eyes** — the Cranial Vault builds sockets for them; the original never listed any

## Glossary & Non-Standard Operations

- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `tr` = treble crochet
- `FLO` / `BLO` = Front Loop Only / Back Loop Only
- `fpdc` = Front Post Double Crochet
- `fptr` = Front Post Treble Crochet
- `3-st-inc` = **3 sc** in the same st (2-st net gain)
- `3-tr-inc` = **3 tr** in the same st (2-st net gain)

> `bpdc`, `ch-sp`, `3-st-dec` and `split-sc` were defined in the original and never used once —
> removed. If you want the Waistcoat Stitch texture that `split-sc` described, Rounds 10–12 of the
> Cranial Vault are the natural place for it.

## Notes — read before starting

1. **The Cranial Vault, Tentacle Hub and Torso are worked in continuous spirals** unless a round
   explicitly says to join. Mark the first stitch of every round.
2. The short rows (13a–13c) and the Wings are worked in turned rows.
3. **Two FLO/BLO splits run two fabrics off one round** — Cranial R9/R14 and Torso R10/R13. Do not
   work into both loops of those rounds.
4. **Every join is stated as three numbers — worked / skipped / held.** Verify each before moving on
   (see the conservation box at Torso Round 17).

> The original read "dc 2 in next 2 sts", which could equally mean one dc in each. Only the stated

## SECTION 2: The Trifurcating Void-Tentacle Hub

*Reproduced unchanged — the bridge conservation in this section is exact.*

### Root Tube (make 1)

**Round 1:** With Color B, ch 18, sl st to first ch to form an **open ring**. (18 sts)

**Rounds 2–8:** sc in each st around. (18 sts)

### Trifurcation Split (Round 9)

18 base sts split into three branches of 6, using two ch-3 bridges (12 bridge-side positions).

| | base | bridge | total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branch 1 | 6 | 3 (Bridge 1 top) | **9** |
| Branch 2 | 6 | 3 (Bridge 2 top) + 3 (Bridge 1 underside) | **12** |
| Branch 3 | 6 | 3 (Bridge 2 underside) | **9** |

> 18 base + 12 bridge sides = 30 = 9 + 12 + 9 ✔ All four bridge sides are consumed. No slit.

**Branch 1 (Lateral Left)**

**Round 9a:** sc 6, ch 3, skip 12 sts, join to first sc of this round. (9 sts)

**Rounds 10a–15a:** sc 6 around base, sc 3 across bridge. (9 sts)

**Round 16a:** [sc 1, dec] 3 times. (6 sts)

> Stuff the branch lightly before closing.

**Round 17a:** [dec] 3 times. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

**Branch 2 (Central Main)**

**Round 9b:** Attach yarn to the 1st skipped st. sc 6, ch 3, skip 6 sts, sc 3 across the **underside
of Branch 1's bridge**. (12 sts)

**Rounds 10b–18b:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

**Round 19b:** [sc 2, dec] 3 times. (9 sts)

**Round 20b:** [sc 1, dec] 3 times. (6 sts)

> Stuff the branch lightly.

**Round 21b:** [dec] 3 times. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

**Branch 3 (Lateral Right)**

**Round 9c:** Attach yarn to the remaining 6 skipped sts. sc 6 across base, sc 3 across the
**underside of Branch 2's bridge**. (9 sts)

**Rounds 10c–15c:** sc in each st around. (9 sts)

**Round 16c:** [sc 1, dec] 3 times. (6 sts)

> Stuff the branch lightly.

**Round 17c:** [dec] 3 times. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

**Result:** three sealed branch tips, with the Root Base (18 sts) **completely open** for torso
insertion.

## SECTION 3: The Multi-Socket Torso & Hyperbolic Mantle

### Open Neck Start

**Round 1:** With Color A, ch 24, sl st to first ch to form an **open ring**. (24 sts)

**Rounds 2–6:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

### Wing Socket Creation

**Round 7:** sc 4 Body, **ch 6, skip 6 Body sts** (Left Wing Socket), sc 8 Body,
**ch 6, skip 6 Body sts** (Right Wing Socket). (24 sts)

> Body: 4 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 24 ✔ Made: 4 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 24 ✔

**Round 8:** sc 4, sc 6 across the left ch-bridge, sc 8, sc 6 across the right ch-bridge. (24 sts)

**Round 9:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

### Hyperbolic Mantle

**Round 10:** **FLO** [inc] 30 times. (60 sts)

> Round 9's 30 **back loops** stay free for Round 13.

**Round 11:** [sc 1, inc] 30 times. (90 sts)

**Round 12 (Controlled Edging Density):** With Color C, [sl st 1, hdc 1, dc 1, hdc 1, sl st 1] in
every 3rd st around. (150 sts of edging, 30 fans)

> Fullness: 150 / 90 = **1.67×** — an undulating frill that will not self-entangle.
> The 2 sts between fans are deliberately skipped; that spacing is what makes it scallop.

Fasten off the mantle.

### Torso Continuation Behind the Mantle

**Round 13:** Attach Color C to the **30 free back loops of Round 9**: [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 14:** [sc 8, inc] 4 times. (40 sts)

**Round 15:** **[sc 4, 3-st-inc] 8 times. (56 sts)**

> Consumes 8 × 5 = 40 ✔ Produces 8 × 7 = 56 ✔
> The original's `[sc 3, 3-st-inc] × 8` ate only 32 of the 40 and made 48.

**Round 16:** sc in each st around. (56 sts)

### Tentacle Hub Integration

**Round 17:** **sc 20 Body**, sc 12 across the **OUTER** edge of the Tentacle Hub Root *(holding 6
inner sts for the gusset)*, **skip 6 Body sts**, **sc 30 Body**. **(62 sts)**

> **Conservation — every term labelled with the piece it comes from:**
>
> | check | sum | |
> |---|---|---|
> | body worked + body skipped | 20 + 6 + 30 = **56** | = Round 16 ✔ |
> | hub worked + hub held | 12 + 6 = **18** | = hub root ✔ |
> | hub held ↔ body skipped | 6 ↔ 6 | ✔ |
> | round total | 20 + 12 + 30 = **62** | ✔ |
>
> The original's note read `14 + 6 + 24 + 12 = 56` — it reached 56 only by counting the **12
> tentacle stitches as body stitches**. Body alone came to 44 of 56, dropping 12.

> **Close the gusset now:** sew the hub's 6 held inner sts to the 6 skipped body sts.

**Round 18:** sc in each st around. (62 sts)

**Round 19:** **sc 20, [dec] 6 times across the tentacle join, sc 30. (56 sts)**

**Round 20:** [sc 5, dec] 8 times. (48 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 4, dec] 8 times. (40 sts)

**Round 22:** [sc 3, dec] 8 times. (32 sts)

**Round 23:** [sc 2, dec] 8 times. (24 sts)

> **Stuff the torso firmly.**

**Round 24:** [sc 1, dec] 8 times. (16 sts)

**Round 25:** [dec] 8 times. (8 sts)

Fasten off and cinch shut.

## SECTION 4: Dual Hyperbolic Pectoral Wings (make 2)

### Socket Insertion Tab

**Row 1:** With Color B, ch 7, sc in 2nd ch from hook and across. (6 sts)

**Rows 2–4:** ch 1, turn, sc across. (6 sts)

> The tab is **6 sts wide × 4 rows** (Rows 1–4). At gauge that is 0.92 in — exactly the width of the
> 6-st socket. The original's note said "3 rows" while Assembly said "Row 1–4".

### Wing Expansion

**Row 5:** ch 1, turn, [inc] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Row 6:** ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (12 sts)

**Row 7:** ch 1, turn, FLO [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Row 8 (Asymmetric Sweep):** ch 1, turn, sc 4, hdc 4, dc 4, **tr 5**, **3-tr-inc in the last st**.
**(20 sts)**

> Consumes 4+4+4+5+1 = 18 ✔ Produces 4+4+4+5+3 = 20 ✔
> The original's `tr 4` left one stitch unworked and made 19. It also called the final flourish
> `3-st-inc`, which the glossary defines as 3 **sc**.

**Row 9 (Post-Stitch Ridge):** ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across, working **[fptr 1 around the post
of the corresponding Row 8 st]** in every 4th st across the dc/tr section. (20 sts)

> Anchored into **Row 8**, which is directly below and has 20 sts against Row 9's 20 — so the posts
> stack 1:1. Row 8's dc and tr are real posts. The original anchored into Row 6, three rows down,
> and tried to map 5 posts from a 20-st row onto a 12-st row.

Fasten off, leaving a 10-inch tail for socket assembly.

## SECTION 5: Assembly

```
+-----------------------+----------------------+------------------------+
| Source Piece & Seam   | Target Piece & Seam  | Seam St Alignment      |
+-----------------------+----------------------+------------------------+
| Head Base (R19)       | Torso Neck Top (R1)  | 24 sts <---> 24 sts    |
| Left Wing Tab (Row 1) | Torso Socket Left    |  6 sts <--->  6 sts    |
| Right Wing Tab (Row 1)| Torso Socket Right   |  6 sts <--->  6 sts    |
| Hub Inner Gusset      | Torso Skipped R17    |  6 sts <--->  6 sts    |
+-----------------------+----------------------+------------------------+
```
*All four verified — this matrix was already correct in the original.*

1. **Internal Eye-Shelf Sealing — do this FIRST, before stuffing the head.** Working through the
   open Round 19, tack the free loops of the brow ridge (Row 13c) to the internal eye shelf
   (Round 9) to anchor the hollow eye sockets. Then stuff the Cranial Vault.
2. **Head-to-Torso Grafting.** Align the 24 open sts of Cranial Vault Round 19 with the 24 open sts
   of Torso Round 1. Whipstitch stitch-for-stitch (24 ↔ 24), adding stuffing before you close.
3. **Wing Socket Insertion.** Feed the Row 1–4 tab of each wing through the 6-st socket at Torso
   Round 7 from the outside in. Whipstitch the 6 sts of Row 1 to the 6 skipped body sts (6 ↔ 6).
   Tab and slot are both 0.92 in — they fit without forcing.
4. **Tentacle Gusset Closure.** If not already done at Round 17, sew the hub's 6 held inner sts to
   the 6 skipped body sts of Torso Round 17 (6 ↔ 6).
5. **Finishing.** Shake the mantle out and let the frill find its own undulation; check all four
   seams are invisible; weave in all ends.
