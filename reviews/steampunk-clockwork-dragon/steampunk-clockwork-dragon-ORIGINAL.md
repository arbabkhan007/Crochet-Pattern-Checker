# Steampunk Clockwork Dragon

**Skill Level:** Master / Expert

**Gauge:** 22 sts x 24 rnds = 4 inches (10 cm) in sc with 3.25 mm hook

## Materials

- Worsted Weight Yarn (Color A: Bronze, Color B: Copper, Color C: Steel Gray)
- 3.25 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing, stitch markers, tapestry needle, 10 mm safety eyes

## Abbreviations & Special Stitches

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `fpdc` = front post double crochet
- `pc` = popcorn stitch (5 dc in same st, join 1st and 5th st)
- `picot` = ch 3, sl st in 1st ch
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## 1. Head & Curved Jaw Assembly

*(Worked snout back to nape, utilizing short rows for jaw angle)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6–8:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

### Tapestry Scale Ridge (Mid-Round Color Switch)

*(Note: Switch to Color B on highlighted sts, carry Color A behind)*

**Round 9:** With Color A sc 10; with Color B [pc 1, Color A sc 1] 5 times; with Color A sc 10. (30 sts)

**Round 10:** sc in each st around with Color A. (30 sts)

### Short-Row Jaw Shaping

**Row 11a (Partial):** sc 12, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 11b (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 11c (Partial):** Ch 1, sc 10, do not turn. Continue working in round across raw edge.

**Round 12 (Re-joining Round):** sc 10 across short row edge, sc 18 across remaining Round 10 sts. (28 sts)

**Round 13:** [sc 2, dec] 7 times. (21 sts)

**Round 14:** [sc 1, dec] 7 times. (14 sts)

*Stuff head firmly.*

**Round 15:** Switch to Color B, [sc 5, inc] 2 times. (16 sts)

**Round 16–19:** sc in each st around. (16 sts)

Fasten off, leave tail for assembly.

## 2. Clockwork Thigh & Leg (Make 2)

### Thigh Gear Flange

**Round 1:** With Color C, magic ring 8 sc, join with sl st to first st. (8 sts)

**Round 2:** Ch 1, [inc] 8 times, join. (16 sts)

**Round 3:** Ch 1, [sc 1, picot] 16 times, join. (16 sts + 16 picots)

**Round 4:** Working in BLO behind picots, [sc 1, inc] 8 times. (24 sts)

### Lower Leg Continuation

**Round 5–8:** Switch to Color A, sc in each st around. (24 sts)

**Round 9:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 10:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Round 11–14:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

**Round 15:** [dec] 3 times, sc 6. (9 sts)

Fasten off. Leave 3 unworked sts marked for body gusset.

## 3. Torso & Integrated Gear Chassis

*(Worked bottom to top with dual lateral attachment sites)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Round 8–14:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Leg-Gusset Attachment Integration

**Round 15:** sc 12 across Torso, sc 6 across outer edge of Leg 1, skip 4 Torso sts, sc 14 across Torso, sc 6 across outer edge of Leg 2, skip 4 Torso sts, sc 6 across Torso. (44 sts)

**Round 16–18:** sc in each st around. (44 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (38 sts)

**Round 20–21:** sc in each st around. (38 sts)

### Wing-Strut Socket Insertion

**Round 22:** sc 10, ch 3 (skip 3 sts for Wing Socket 1), sc 12, ch 3 (skip 3 sts for Wing Socket 2), sc 10. (38 sts)

**Round 23:** sc 10, sc 3 into ch-3 space, sc 12, sc 3 into ch-3 space, sc 10. (38 sts)

**Round 24:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (32 sts)

**Round 25:** [sc 2, dec] 8 times. (24 sts)

**Round 26:** [sc 1, dec] 8 times. (16 sts)

Fasten off and stuff torso.

## 4. Mechanical Wing Panels (Make 2)

*(Worked in flat rows with Post Stitches and Picots)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 18, sc in 2nd ch from hook and next 16 chs, turn. (17 sts)

**Row 2:** Ch 1, FLO sc 15, dec, turn. (16 sts)

**Row 3:** Ch 1, BLO dec, sc 12, dec, turn. (14 sts)

**Row 4:** Ch 1, FLO sc 12, inc, turn. (15 sts)

**Row 5:** Ch 2, [dc 1, fpdc 1] 7 times, dc 1, turn. (15 sts)

### Wing Tip Spoke

**Row 6:** Ch 1, sc 5, [picot, sc 1] 5 times, sc 5, turn. (15 sts + 5 picots)

**Row 7:** [sc 3, dec] 3 times. (12 sts)

Fasten off leaving a 12-inch tail. Insert lower wing tab into Socket 1/2 of Torso.

## 5. Segmented Clockwork Tail

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Round 2–4:** sc around. (4 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Round 6–8:** sc around. (6 sts)

**Round 9:** Switch to Color B, [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Round 10–12:** sc around. (8 sts)

**Round 13:** Switch to Color A, [sc 3, inc] 2 times. (10 sts)

**Round 14–16:** sc around. (10 sts)

**Round 17:** Switch to Color C, [sc 4, inc] 2 times. (12 sts)

**Round 18–20:** sc around. (12 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 5, inc] 2 times. (14 sts)

**Round 22–24:** sc around. (16 sts)

Fasten off.

## 6. Assembly & Interlocking Directives

1. Sew remaining 3 unworked sts of each Leg to the 4 skipped sts on Torso Round 15 to seal inner crotch.
2. Insert Wing base tabs through Round 22 Torso sockets and anchor internally with yarn tail.
3. Attach Neck opening (Round 19 of Head & Neck, 16 sts) to top opening of Torso (Round 26 of Torso, 16 sts).
4. Attach Tail Round 24 to base of Torso (Rounds 2–6).
