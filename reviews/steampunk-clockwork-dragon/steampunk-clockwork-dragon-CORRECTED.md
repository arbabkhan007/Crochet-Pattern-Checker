# Steampunk Clockwork Dragon — Corrected

> Corrected edition. Validates with 0 defects (`verify_dragon.py --fixed`).
> See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and the reasoning behind it.

**Skill Level:** Master / Expert

**Finished Size:** Approx. 10 in / 25 cm tall, with a 7 in / 18 cm wingspan

**Gauge:** 22 sts x 24 rnds = 4 in (10 cm) in sc with a 3.25 mm hook

## Materials

- Worsted weight yarn:
  - **Color A — Bronze:** approx. 150 g / 300 yds (head, torso, lower legs, tail)
  - **Color B — Copper:** approx. 40 g / 80 yds (scale ridge, neck, tail segment)
  - **Color C — Steel Gray:** approx. 60 g / 120 yds (gear flanges, wings)
- 3.25 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing
- Stitch markers
- Tapestry needle
- **2 x 10 mm safety eyes**

## Abbreviations & Special Stitches

- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `fpdc` = front post double crochet
- `pc` = popcorn stitch (5 dc in same st, join 1st and 5th st)
- `picot` = ch 3, sl st in 1st ch
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## Notes — read before starting

1. **Head, Torso and Tail are worked in continuous spirals.** Do not join, do not turn. Mark the
   first stitch of each round.
2. **The Thigh Gear Flange (Leg Rounds 1-4) is worked in joined rounds** — ch 1 and sl st to join as
   written. **From Round 5 the leg switches to a continuous spiral**; stop joining at that point.
3. The Wings are worked in flat, turned rows.
4. Turning chains (`ch 1` before an sc row, `ch 2` before the dc row) **never count as a stitch**.
5. Stuffing prompts are placed at the last point each piece is still open enough to reach.

## 1. Head & Curved Jaw Assembly

*(Worked from the snout forward edge back to the nape, using short rows to angle the jaw)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Rounds 6-8:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

> **Insert the 10 mm safety eyes between Rounds 6 and 7, 6 sts apart**, centred on the face and
> above the jaw line. Secure the backs before you stuff.

### Tapestry Scale Ridge (mid-round colour switch)

*Carry Color A loosely behind the Color B stitches; do not cut it.*

**Round 9:** With Color A sc 10; with Color B [pc 1, with Color A sc 1] 5 times; with Color A
sc 10. (30 sts)

> The popcorns stand roughly three times the height of the surrounding sc and open a small gap at
> the base of each. When you stuff, keep the fibrefill packed away from the ridge so it does not
> show through.

**Round 10:** With Color A, sc in each st around. (30 sts)

### Short-Row Jaw Shaping

*These three rows work over only 12 of the 30 stitches. The other 18 are held and picked up in
Round 12.*

**Row 11a (partial):** sc 12, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 11b (partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 11c (partial):** Ch 1, sc 10. Do not turn.

**Round 12 (re-joining round):** sc 10 across the live sts of Row 11c, work **3 sc evenly down the
first raw row-end edge**, sc 18 across the remaining Round 10 sts, work **3 sc evenly up the second
raw row-end edge**. (34 sts)

> Those two sets of 3 sc are what close the jaw hinge. Skipping them leaves a gap at each corner of
> the jaw.

**Round 13:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times, sc 4. (28 sts)

**Round 14:** [sc 2, dec] 7 times. (21 sts)

**Round 15:** [sc 1, dec] 7 times. (14 sts)

> **Stuff the head firmly**, shaping the snout and keeping stuffing clear of the scale ridge.

**Round 16:** Switch to Color B, [sc 6, inc] 2 times. (16 sts)

**Rounds 17-20:** sc in each st around. (16 sts)

> **Stuff the neck firmly** as you go.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for assembly.

## 2. Clockwork Thigh & Leg (make 2)

*This piece was already correct in the original; only stuffing notes have been added.*

### Thigh Gear Flange — worked in joined rounds

**Round 1:** With Color C, magic ring 8 sc, join with sl st to first st. (8 sts)

**Round 2:** Ch 1, [inc] 8 times, join. (16 sts)

**Round 3:** Ch 1, [sc 1, picot] 16 times, join. (16 sts + 16 picots)

> For more mechanical, tooth-like gear teeth, work `[sc 1, picot, sc 1] 8 times` instead — same
> 16 sts, but 8 spaced picots rather than a continuous frill.

**Round 4:** Working in BLO behind the picots, [sc 1, inc] 8 times. (24 sts)

### Lower Leg — switch to continuous spiral from here

**Rounds 5-8:** Switch to Color A. sc in each st around. (24 sts)

**Round 9:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 10:** [sc 1, dec] 6 times. (12 sts)

**Rounds 11-14:** sc in each st around. (12 sts)

> **Stuff the leg firmly now.** Once it is joined to the torso in Round 15 you cannot reach inside.

**Round 15:** [dec] 3 times, sc 6. (9 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail. **Mark the 3 sts that will not be worked into the torso** — they
become the crotch gusset.

## 3. Torso & Integrated Gear Chassis

*(Worked bottom to top)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 7:** [sc 5, inc] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Rounds 8-14:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Leg-Gusset Attachment Integration

Hold each stuffed leg against the torso with its 9 open sts facing up. Six are worked into the
round; the marked 3 sit against the body and are sewn to the 3 skipped torso sts afterwards.

**Round 15:** sc 18 across Torso, sc 6 across the outer edge of Leg 1, **skip 3 Torso sts**,
sc 18 across Torso, sc 6 across the outer edge of Leg 2, **skip 3 Torso sts**. (48 sts)

> Torso sts: 18 + 3 skipped + 18 + 3 skipped = 42 ✔  Sts made: 18 + 6 + 18 + 6 = 48 ✔
>
> **Close the crotch now.** Using each leg's tail, sew that leg's 3 marked sts to the 3 skipped
> Torso sts beside it, before Round 16 makes the gap hard to reach.

**Rounds 16-18:** sc in each st around. (48 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 6, dec] 6 times. (42 sts)

**Rounds 20-21:** sc in each st around. (42 sts)

### Wing-Strut Socket Insertion

The ch-6 spans and the 6 skipped sts beneath them form two horizontal slots. Round 23 works over the
chains; the slot itself stays open.

**Round 22:** sc 12, **ch 6 (skip 6 sts for Wing Socket 1)**, sc 6, **ch 6 (skip 6 sts for Wing
Socket 2)**, sc 12. (42 sts)

**Round 23:** sc 12, sc 6 into the ch-6 space, sc 6, sc 6 into the ch-6 space, sc 12. (42 sts)

**Round 24:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Round 25:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

> **Stuff the torso firmly**, packing extra into the shoulders around the sockets.

**Round 26:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 27:** [sc 1, dec] 8 times. (16 sts)

Fasten off. Top up the stuffing before attaching the head.

## 4. Mechanical Wing Panels (make 2)

*(Flat rows. Enlarged with a shaped base tab so it actually fits the socket.)*

**Row 1:** With Color C, ch 18, sc in 2nd ch from hook and next 16 chs, turn. (17 sts)

**Row 2:** Ch 1, FLO sc 15, dec, turn. (16 sts)

**Row 3:** Ch 1, BLO dec, sc 12, dec, turn. (14 sts)

**Row 4:** Ch 1, hdc 13, 2 hdc in last st, turn. (15 sts)

> Worked in hdc through **both loops** — this is what gives Row 5 a post tall enough to work a
> front post dc around. The original worked this row in FLO sc, which has no usable post.

**Row 5:** Ch 2 (does **not** count as a st), [dc 1, fpdc 1] 7 times, dc 1, turn. (15 sts)

### Wing Tip Spoke

**Row 6:** Ch 1, sc 5, [picot, sc 1] 5 times, sc 5, turn. (15 sts + 5 picots)

**Row 7:** Ch 1, [sc 3, dec] 3 times, turn. (12 sts)

### Wing Base Tab

*This is the part that goes through the torso socket. It finishes 6 sts wide, matching the 6-st slot.*

**Row 8:** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 9:** Ch 1, dec, sc 6, dec, turn. (8 sts)

**Row 10:** Ch 1, dec, sc 4, dec, turn. (6 sts)

**Rows 11-13:** Ch 1, sc 6, turn. (6 sts)

Fasten off leaving a 12 in tail.

## 5. Segmented Clockwork Tail

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 4 sc. (4 sts)

**Rounds 2-4:** sc around. (4 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 1, inc] 2 times. (6 sts)

**Rounds 6-8:** sc around. (6 sts)

**Round 9:** Switch to Color B, [sc 2, inc] 2 times. (8 sts)

**Rounds 10-12:** sc around. (8 sts)

> **Start stuffing the tail now** and keep topping it up every few rounds. Leave the last inch of
> the tip unstuffed so it can curl.

**Round 13:** Switch to Color A, [sc 3, inc] 2 times. (10 sts)

**Rounds 14-16:** sc around. (10 sts)

**Round 17:** Switch to Color C, [sc 4, inc] 2 times. (12 sts)

**Rounds 18-20:** sc around. (12 sts)

**Round 21:** [sc 5, inc] 2 times. (14 sts)

**Round 22:** [sc 6, inc] 2 times. (16 sts)

**Rounds 23-25:** sc around. (16 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## 6. Assembly & Interlocking Directives

1. **Crotch gusset.** If not already done at Round 15, sew each Leg's 3 marked sts to the 3 skipped
   Torso sts beside it. Both edges are 3 sts, so they match stitch for stitch.
2. **Wings.** Thread each wing's 6-st base tab (Rows 11-13) through the matching 6-st socket at
   Torso Round 22 from the outside in. Both measure 1.09 in, so it passes cleanly. Fan the tab open
   inside the torso and anchor it with the 12 in tail, stitching through to the torso wall in at
   least six places so the wing cannot pull back out.
3. **Head & Neck.** Attach the neck opening (Round 20 of Head & Neck, 16 sts) to the top opening of
   the Torso (Round 27 of Torso, 16 sts). The counts match exactly. Line them up, pin at four
   points, and whip stitch around, adding stuffing before closing the last few sts.
4. **Tail.** Attach the open Round 25 edge (16 sts) to the back of the Torso centred on Round 4,
   angling the tail down and back. Round 4 is 24 sts around, so a 16-st opening sits comfortably.
5. **Finishing.** Check the crotch seams are invisible; confirm both wing tabs are anchored and the
   sockets have not stretched; with Color C, embroider a small nostril each side of the snout at
   Round 2; weave in all remaining ends.
