# Interchangeable Christmas Wreath

> **US + UK equivalent  ·  Beginner  ·  tube grows by length**  —  Design Code NS 15

A flexible stuffed tube joined into a loop for supported flat display, dressed for the season with three REMOVABLE decorations: poinsettia, lace snowflake and a gift-bow. Swap them as the holidays change. Mini ornament and two supported-display sizes; unsupported vertical door-wreath use is not validated.

**UNVERIFIED SIZE TARGET — measure a sample**  Mini ornament: 22 cm (8.5 in) tube; centreline ring diameter about 7 cm.  Standard supported loop: 92 cm (36 in) tube; centreline diameter about 29 cm.  Large supported loop: 125 cm (49 in) tube; centreline diameter about 40 cm.  The finished OUTSIDE diameter is one tube thickness larger, so measure your sample. Poinsettia approx. 3.5 in; snowflake approx. 4 in; bow approx. 3.5 in wide.

## Structural limitation — read before choosing a size

The 12-stitch tube is NOT validated as a self-supporting circular door wreath. Standard and large versions in this edition are supported tabletop/display loops, not unsupported vertical door wreaths. Do not rely on extra stuffing, an external wrap or an improvised wire to hold a circle. A vertical form-mounted wreath requires a separate fit and attachment design around a suitable manufactured form; that construction is not supplied here. Mini hanging use also requires a sample and attachment assessment.

## Safety — read this first

This design is intended as home decor, not a toy. A stuffed wreath is heavier than it looks: use a securely installed, load-rated hook and a load-rated woven cord or ribbon around the whole tube; do not rely on a suction cup or on a decoration tie.

## Materials

- Wreath base yarn: green worsted/aran (#4), with a 3.5–4 mm starting hook for the standard/large tube; swatch for a dense, bendable fabric (the mini uses DK and a 3 mm hook). Paper estimates at roughly 1.6 g per 100 stitches - weigh your first tube. Mini tube (22 cm) about 12 g including attachments. Standard tube (92 cm) about 40 g. Large tube (125 cm) about 55 g.
- Stuffing (fibre fill, paper estimates): mini about 2 g, standard about 8 g, large about 12 g.

- Decorations: small amounts of red, white, yellow and green yarn; optional metallic thread held with the snowflake yarn; 3.5-4 mm hook.

- For an assessed miniature hanging version, use a suitable woven cord or ribbon. Standard/large unsupported door-hanging construction is not supplied. Do not add loose or glued embellishments anywhere children can reach.

- Notions: stitch marker, scissors, tapestry needle.

## Gauge & size

Gauge affects tube thickness, flexibility and yarn use. The standard/large tube is a constant 12 stitches (the mini uses 8) and wreath size comes from tube LENGTH. Measure the tube, not the round count. The ring's centreline diameter is approximately tube length divided by 3.14; add one tube thickness for the outside diameter.

## Construction & techniques

Wreath base: continuous spiral rounds (no joins - use a marker). Decorations: joined rounds for flowers and snowflake, short worked rows for the bow.

1. **The constant stitch tube** - Every standard/large tube round is exactly 12 sc; the mini uses 8 sc - no increases, no decreases. Tube length sets wreath size: measure against the door/table and stop after a complete round.

2. **Joining the tube** - Keep both openings ROUND and make sure the tube is not twisted. Align each stitch of the final 12-stitch round with one foundation chain at the beginning; whip-stitch the 12 pairs around the circumference. Add the last stuffing before closing the final 3 pairs. Flattening the ends would seal them into bars instead of making a smooth end-to-end tube join.

3. **Snowflake arm (shared with NS 13)** - Into each ch-5 space: (sl st, ch 3, 3 tr, ch 3, sl st). First sl st loose, last sl st snug so the arm lies flat and stands upright.

4. **Removable ties** - Cut a 55 cm strand in the decoration colour and fold it in half (30 cm is enough for the mini wreath). Draw the folded loop under at least TWO sturdy stitches on the decoration back, pass both cut ends through that loop and snug it: this lark's-head anchors the tie without asking one stitch to carry the load. Take the two tails in opposite directions around the wreath tube and tie a secure bow or reef knot at the back. Untie the tails to swap decorations; unlike a short closed loop, this method does not require you to force the whole flower, snowflake or bow through its own attachment.

### 4. Fabric-loop bow — removable

Make the loop strip separately in red: ch 50. Row 1: sc in second chain and across (49), ch 1, turn. Row 2: sc across (49), FO with a sewing tail. Fold both short ends inward to the centre to create two fabric loops; sew the ends securely to the centre and pinch the middle.

Make two tails separately: ch 15, sc in second chain and across (14), FO with sewing tails. Sew both top ends to the pinched centre. Make the band: ch 6; Row 1 sc in second chain and across (5); Rows 2–4 ch 1, turn, sc across (5 each). FO with a sewing tail. Wrap around the pinched centre and sew short ends; tack through the enclosed bases so no component can slide out. If the band does not fit without crushing the loops, remake to measured length, preserving five stitches per row. Attach the separate folded 55 cm removable tie through at least two strong band-back stitches. All finished dimensions are unverified.

## Finishing & assembly

- Stuff the tube as you go - after every 5-8 rounds is far easier than stuffing a finished tube.

- Join the tube LAST: keep the openings round, check for twists, whip-stitch all 12 matched pairs (8 for the mini) around and add the final stuffing before the last 3 pairs.

- Every decoration has a folded 55 cm tie lark's-headed under at least two back stitches; wrap its two tails around the tube and tie them at the back. Untie to swap - nothing is sewn permanently to the wreath.

## Troubleshooting

- **Tube is stiff like a rope.** Over-stuffed - pull a pinch of fill per handful back out; the wreath must flex into a circle.

- **Wreath will not hold a circle.** Under-stuffed at the join - open enough of the seam to access the filling, redistribute it without stretching the fabric, then resew every matched pair. Do not force fill through a closed seam or use an external wrap to conceal a weak join; a large flexible ring may still sag and needs sample testing.

- **Poinsettia petals cluster on one side.** Each petal goes into its OWN centre stitch, moving stitch by stitch around the six - not all six into one stitch.
