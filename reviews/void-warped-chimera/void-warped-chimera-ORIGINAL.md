# Void-Warped Chimera

**Skill Level:** Beyond Grandmaster / Experimental

**Gauge:** 22 sts × 24 rnds = 4 inches (10 cm) in sc with 3.5 mm hook

## Materials

- Worsted Weight Yarn (Color A: Void Black, Color B: Crimson, Color C: Eldritch Gold)
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing, tapestry needle, 12 mm safety eyes (x4)

## Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `tr` = treble crochet
- `fptr` = front post treble crochet
- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## 1. Asymmetric Cranial Core & Dual Eyestalk Cavity

*(Worked in continuous spiral with double-short-row saddle curve)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)
**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)
**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)
**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)
**Round 7-11:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

### Dorsal Brow Wedge (Short Rows)

**Row 12a (Partial):** sc 12, turn. (12 sts)
**Row 12b (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)
**Row 12c (Partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 6, dec, turn. (8 sts)
**Round 13 (Rejoining Round):** sc 8 across Row 12c, sc 2 down raw row ends, sc 24 across unworked Round 11 sts, sc 2 up raw row ends. (36 sts)

### Ventral Jaw Expansion (Irregular Modulo Drift)

**Round 14:** sc 10, [inc] 8 times, sc 18. (44 sts)
**Round 15:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times. (38 sts)

### Post-Stitch Eyestalk Ridges

**Round 16:** Switch to Color B, [sc 2, fptr 1 into Round 14] 12 times, sc 2. (38 sts)
**Round 17:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (32 sts)
**Round 18:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (26 sts)

Fasten off.

## 2. Internal Partition (Dual-Chamber Wall)

*(Worked in flat circular disc to be grafted inside the Cranial Core)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)
**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)
**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)
**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

Fasten off. Leave 12-inch tail for grafting.

## 3. Trifurcating Tentacle Core (Make 2)

*(Splits into 3 distinct branches from a single root)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 9 sc. (9 sts)
**Round 2-10:** sc around. (9 sts)

### The Three-Way Fork

**Round 11 (Trifurcation Round):**

- Branch 1: sc 3, ch 2, skip 6 sts. Continue into 1st st of R11. (5 sts)

  **Rounds 12a-18a (Branch 1):** sc 3, sc 2 in ch-2 bridge. (5 sts)

  **Round 19a:** dec 2, sc 1. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

- Branch 2: Attach Color A to next 3 skipped sts of R11. sc 3, ch 2, skip remaining 3 sts. (5 sts)

  **Rounds 12b-18b (Branch 2):** sc 3, sc 2 in bridge. (5 sts)

  **Round 19b:** dec 2, sc 1. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

- Branch 3: Attach Color A to final 3 skipped sts of R11.

  **Rounds 12c-18c (Branch 3):** sc 3, sc 2 across underside of bridge. (5 sts)

  **Round 19c:** dec 2, sc 1. Fasten off and cinch shut. (3 sts)

## 4. Hyperbolic Mantle & Multi-Limb Socket Torso

*(Worked bottom to top with 4 limb sockets and exponential frill)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, ch 24, join with sl st to form a ring. (24 sts)
**Round 2-6:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

### Integrated Limb Socket Round

**Round 7:**

- sc 2 across Body
- sc 3 across inner edge of Trifurcating Tentacle 1 (leaving 6 sts outer)
- sc 6 across Body
- sc 3 across inner edge of Trifurcating Tentacle 2 (leaving 6 sts outer)
- sc 13 across Body. (27 sts)

**Round 8:** sc in all 27 sts around. (27 sts)
**Round 9:** [sc 2, inc] 9 times. (36 sts)

### Exponential Hyperbolic Frill Section

**Round 10:** Working in FLO, [inc] in each st around. (72 sts)
**Round 11:** [sc 1, inc] around. (108 sts)
**Round 12:** Working with Color C, *[sl st, hdc, dc, tr, dc, hdc, sl st]* in each st around. (756 sts)

Fasten off frill.

### Main Torso Recovery (Behind Frill)

**Round 13:** Attach Color A to unworked BLO loops of Round 9, sc in all 36 sts. (36 sts)
**Round 14:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)
**Round 15:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)
**Round 16:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off and stuff.

## 5. Assembly & Structural Directives

1. Graft the Internal Partition (Round 5, 30 sts) into Round 15 of Cranial Core to divide upper and lower head chambers.
2. Sew Cranial Core base (Round 18, 26 sts) to Torso top (Round 16, 18 sts).
3. Cinch the 6 outer held stitches of Trifurcating Tentacles to the 6 skipped body stitches under each socket.
4. Mount 2 safety eyes on the Cranial Vault, and 2 safety eyes on the Hyperbolic Frill
