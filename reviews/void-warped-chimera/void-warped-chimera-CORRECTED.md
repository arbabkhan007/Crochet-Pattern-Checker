# Void-Warped Chimera — Corrected

> Corrected edition. Validates with 0 defects (`verify_chimera.py --fixed`).
> See `PATTERN_REVIEW.md` for every change and the reasoning behind it.

**Skill Level:** Beyond Grandmaster / Experimental

**Finished Size:** Approx. 7 in / 18 cm tall, plus 3 in / 8 cm tentacles and a 6 in / 15 cm frill spread

**Gauge:** 22 sts × 24 rnds = 4 in (10 cm) in sc with a 3.5 mm hook

## Materials

- Worsted weight yarn:
  - **Color A — Void Black:** approx. 110 g / 220 yds
  - **Color B — Crimson:** approx. 15 g / 30 yds
  - **Color C — Eldritch Gold:** approx. 30 g / 60 yds *(the frill alone takes ~17 g)*
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Polyfill stuffing
- Stitch markers
- Tapestry needle
- **4 × 12 mm safety eyes**

## Glossary

- `sc` = single crochet
- `inc` = 2 sc in same st
- `dec` = invisible decrease
- `hdc` = half double crochet
- `dc` = double crochet
- `tr` = treble crochet
- `fptr` = front post treble crochet
- `ch` = chain
- `sl st` = slip stitch
- `st(s)` = stitch(es)
- `BLO` = back loop only
- `FLO` = front loop only

## Notes — read before starting

1. **Every piece is worked in a continuous spiral.** Do not join, do not turn, except in the short
   rows, which are explicitly labelled and turned. The `sl st` in Tentacle Round 1 and Torso Round 1
   closes the foundation ring only — do not keep joining after that.
2. Mark the first stitch of every round.
3. **Three joins in this pattern are stated as three numbers — worked / skipped / held.** Before you
   continue past any of them, check that *worked + skipped = the previous round* and
   *held = skipped*.
4. **The Internal Partition must go in as you reach Cranial Round 13.** The head closes over it; you
   cannot add it later.

## 1. Asymmetric Cranial Core

*(Worked in a continuous spiral, with a short-row saddle curve for the brow)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

**Rounds 7-11:** sc in each st around. (36 sts)

### Dorsal Brow Wedge (short rows)

*These three rows work over only 12 of the 36 sts. The other 24 are held for Round 13.*

**Row 12a (partial):** sc 12, turn. (12 sts)

**Row 12b (partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 8, dec, turn. (10 sts)

**Row 12c (partial):** Ch 1, dec, sc 6, dec, turn. (8 sts)

**Round 13 (rejoining round):** sc 8 across Row 12c; **dec over the first 2 raw row-ends, sc 1 in
the third**; sc 24 across the unworked Round 11 sts; **sc 1 in the first raw row-end, dec over the
last 2**. (36 sts)

> Perimeter check: 8 live + 24 held + (3 × 2) raw row-ends = **38** positions, all worked ✔, and the
> two decreases bring it to 36. The original worked only 2 sc into each 3-row-end edge, which left a
> hole at each corner of the wedge.
>
> **Graft the Internal Partition in now** — see Assembly 1. Its 36-st edge matches this round
> exactly, and this is the last moment you can reach inside.

### Ventral Jaw Expansion

**Round 14:** sc 10, [inc] 8 times, sc 18. (44 sts)

> Consumes all 36 ✔, makes 44 ✔. Unchanged — an asymmetric expansion round, correct as written.

**Round 15:** [sc 5, dec] 6 times, **sc 2**. (38 sts)

> Consumes 42 + 2 = 44 ✔, makes 36 + 2 = 38 ✔. The trailing `sc 2` is what picks up the 2 stitches
> a bare `[sc 5, dec] × 6` leaves behind on a 44-st round.

### Post-Stitch Eyestalk Ridges

**Round 16:** hdc in each st around. (38 sts)

> This round is hdc, not sc, on purpose — it is what gives Round 17's treble post stitches a post
> tall enough to wrap. The original anchored them into Round 14, which is sc and two rounds down.

**Round 17:** Switch to Color B. [sc 2, **fptr 1 around the post of the corresponding Round 16 st**]
12 times, sc 2. (38 sts)

> Anchoring into Round 16 — same stitch count, one round down — is what lets the ridge stack
> vertically. Posts spaced every 3 sts in a 38-st round cannot align to a 44-st round.

**Round 18:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times, **sc 2**. (32 sts)

**Round 19:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times, **sc 2**. (26 sts)

> **Stuff the lower chamber firmly** before the next round closes it. If you grafted the partition
> at Round 13, the upper chamber must have been stuffed before that — check it is still full.

**Round 20:** [sc 1, dec] 8 times, **sc 2**. (18 sts)

> Consumes 24 + 2 = 26 ✔, makes 16 + 2 = 18 ✔ — which is exactly the torso's top opening.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for assembly.

## 2. Internal Partition (dual-chamber wall)

*(A flat disc, grafted inside the Cranial Core at Round 13)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, magic ring 6 sc. (6 sts)

**Round 2:** [inc] around. (12 sts)

**Round 3:** [sc 1, inc] 6 times. (18 sts)

**Round 4:** [sc 2, inc] 6 times. (24 sts)

**Round 5:** [sc 3, inc] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 6:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

> Round 6 is the addition. At 30 sts the disc was 6 stitches too small for anything in the cranial
> core; at 36 it matches Round 13 exactly.

Fasten off, leaving a 12 in tail for grafting.

## 3. Trifurcating Tentacle Core (make 2)

*(One root splitting into three branches, via two sequential forks)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, **ch 9, join with sl st to form a ring**, careful not to twist.
**Leave this end open — it is the body socket.** (9 sts)

**Rounds 2-10:** sc in each st around. (9 sts)

### Fork 1 — Branch 1 separates from the rest

**Round 11:** sc 3, **ch 2 (Bridge 1)**, skip 6 sts. Continue into the 1st st of Round 11 to close
the Branch 1 loop. (5 sts)

> Consumes all 9 ✔ — 3 worked, 6 skipped and held.

**Rounds 12a-18a (Branch 1):** sc 3, sc 2 across the **top** of Bridge 1. (5 sts)

**Round 19a:** dec 2, sc 1. (3 sts) Fasten off and cinch shut.

### Fork 2 — Branch 2 separates from Branch 3

Attach Color A to the 6 skipped sts of Round 11. **The ring you are now working is 8 sts: the 6
skipped base sts plus the 2 stitches on the UNDERSIDE of Bridge 1.**

> This is the correction. The original never worked into Bridge 1's underside, which left a 2-chain
> slit between Branch 1 and the other two branches.

**Round 11b:** sc 4, **ch 2 (Bridge 2)**, skip 4 sts. Continue into the 1st st to close the Branch 2
loop. (6 sts)

**Rounds 12b-18b (Branch 2):** sc 4, sc 2 across the **top** of Bridge 2. (6 sts)

**Round 19b:** [dec] 3 times. (3 sts) Fasten off and cinch shut.

**Rounds 12c-18c (Branch 3):** Attach Color A to the 4 skipped sts of Round 11b. sc 4, sc 2 across
the **underside** of Bridge 2. (6 sts)

**Round 19c:** [dec] 3 times. (3 sts) Fasten off and cinch shut.

> Bridge accounting: Bridge 1 top → Branch 1, Bridge 1 underside → the lower group, Bridge 2 top →
> Branch 2, Bridge 2 underside → Branch 3. All four sides worked, nothing orphaned.
>
> **Stuff the trunk and all three branches lightly** before attaching to the torso. Once Round 7 of
> the torso closes over the socket there is no way back in.

## 4. Hyperbolic Mantle & Multi-Limb Socket Torso

*(Worked bottom to top, with 2 limb sockets and a hyperbolic frill)*

**Round 1:** With Color A, ch 24, join with sl st to form a ring. (24 sts)

**Rounds 2-6:** sc in each st around. (24 sts)

### Integrated Limb Socket Round

Hold each tentacle against the body with its open 9-st ring facing up. You work across its **outer**
6 stitches; the 3 **inner** stitches are held and sewn to the 3 body stitches you skip beneath it.

**Round 7:**

- sc 3 across Body
- sc 6 across the **outer** edge of Trifurcating Tentacle 1 *(3 inner sts held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 9 across Body
- sc 6 across the **outer** edge of Trifurcating Tentacle 2 *(3 held)*, **skip 3 Body sts**
- sc 6 across Body. **(30 sts)**

> **The three-number check.**
> Body worked 18 + Body skipped 6 = **24** = Round 6 ✔
> Limb sts held: 2 × 3 = **6** = Body skipped **6** ✔
> Made: 18 + (2 × 6) = **30** ✔
>
> **Close the sockets now** — sew each tentacle's 3 held sts to the 3 skipped Body sts beneath it.

**Round 8:** sc in each st around. (30 sts)

**Round 9:** [sc 4, inc] 6 times. (36 sts)

> **Stuff the lower torso firmly now.** The frill is about to cover this section.

### Hyperbolic Frill Section

**Round 10:** Working in **FLO** of Round 9, [inc] in each st around. (72 sts)

**Round 11:** [sc 1, inc] around. (108 sts)

**Round 12:** With Color C: \*[hdc, dc, hdc] in next st, sl st in next st\*; repeat from \* around.
**(216 sts — 54 fans)**

> Consumes all 108 ✔. This is the big change. The original put a 7-stitch treble fan into *every one*
> of the 108 stitches — 756 sts, **11½ feet of edge** hanging off a 6.5 in torso, 21× its
> circumference, with no anchoring stitch. It packs into a ball instead of ruffling.
>
> At 216 sts the frill is 39 in of edge — still a dramatic **6×** frill, at 2.0 sts per base stitch,
> which is standard shell-edging density. The `sl st` between fans is the anchor. Do not omit it.

Fasten off the frill.

### Main Torso Recovery (behind the frill)

**Round 13:** Attach Color A to the unworked **BLO** loops of Round 9. sc in all 36 sts. (36 sts)

> Round 10 used only the front loops, so Round 9's 36 back loops are still free. This is the
> cleverest idea in the pattern and it was correct as written — two fabrics running off one round.

**Round 14:** [sc 4, dec] 6 times. (30 sts)

**Round 15:** [sc 3, dec] 6 times. (24 sts)

> **Finish stuffing the torso**, packing firmly up into the shoulders.

**Round 16:** [sc 2, dec] 6 times. (18 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail.

## 5. Assembly & Structural Directives

1. **Internal Partition.** Graft the Partition (Round 6, **36 sts**) into Cranial Core **Round 13**
   (**36 sts**) — the counts match stitch for stitch. Do this *as you reach Round 13*, not at the
   end: stuff the upper chamber first, then whip stitch the disc's outer edge to the round all the
   way around, then carry on with Round 14.
2. **Head to torso.** Sew the Cranial Core opening (**Round 20, 18 sts**) to the Torso top
   (**Round 16, 18 sts**). Equal counts — pin at four points and whip stitch around, topping up the
   stuffing before you close the last few stitches.
3. **Tentacle sockets.** If not already done at Round 7, sew each tentacle's **3 held stitches** to
   the **3 skipped body stitches** beneath it. Two sockets, 3 to 3 each.
4. **Eyes.** Mount **all four 12 mm safety eyes on the Cranial Vault** — two forward-facing between
   Rounds 8 and 9, 7 sts apart, and two set higher and off-centre between Rounds 6 and 7, 5 sts
   apart, for the asymmetry the piece is named for. Secure every washer before stuffing.
   **Do not attempt to mount safety eyes on the frill**: a safety eye needs flat fabric and rear
   access for its washer, and a free-hanging treble edge offers neither. If you want eyes out on the
   frill, embroider them or glue on flat-backed cabochons.
5. **Finishing.** Check both socket seams and the partition graft are invisible; shake the frill out
   and let it find its own curl; weave in all remaining ends.
