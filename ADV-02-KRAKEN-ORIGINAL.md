1. Product Specification

Create a seated clockwork kraken with a spherical head, a tapered mantle, twelve articulated tentacles, eight embroidered suction-cup rows per tentacle, six brass-look gears, and a removable mechanical heart.

Target specifications

- Finished height: exactly 28 cm, including the tallest tentacle.
- Mantle circumference: exactly 42 cm.
- Head circumference: 38 cm.
- Finished weight: no more than 300 g.
- Construction: completely seamless, with no sewn components.
- Assembly: all twelve tentacles must be permanently sewn to the mantle.
- Articulation: every tentacle must bend independently through 180 degrees.
- Gauge: 22 sc × 24 rounds = 10 × 10 cm using a 3.0 mm hook.
- Alternative gauge: 20 sc × 22 rounds = 10 × 10 cm using the same yarn and hook.
- Intended audience: children under 36 months.
- All mechanical components must be removable for washing, but no component may detach during play.

2. Materials

- 120 g worsted-weight cotton yarn, Color A: Deep Ocean.
- 90 g worsted-weight cotton yarn, Color B: Copper.
- 60 g worsted-weight cotton yarn, Color C: Antique Gold.
- 40 g black embroidery thread.
- 18 mm safety eyes: 2.
- 12 plastic articulated joints.
- 8 miniature plastic gears.
- 1 removable plastic heart, approximately 5 cm wide.
- 1 m flexible steel craft wire.
- 1 tube of hot glue.
- Polyester stuffing: 250 g.
- 3.0 mm crochet hook.
- 4.0 mm crochet hook, referenced in the finishing section only.
- 2.5 mm crochet hook, referenced in the materials list only.
- Tapestry needle.
- Stitch markers: 12.

**Materials note:** The complete finished kraken must weigh no more than 300 g, regardless of how much stuffing, yarn, wire, or hardware is used.

3. Abbreviations

- MR = magic ring
- sc = single crochet
- inc = 2 sc in the same stitch
- dec = invisible decrease
- hdc = half double crochet
- dc = double crochet
- sl st = slip stitch
- ch = chain
- BLO = back loop only
- FLO = front loop only
- FPdc = front post double crochet
- BPdc = back post double crochet
- st(s) = stitch(es)
- rnd(s) = round(s)

All rounds are worked continuously in a spiral unless otherwise stated. Join every round with a slip stitch and turn the work after each round.

6. Suction Cups

Make eight rows of suction cups on each tentacle.

For every row:

- Work 12 suction cups.
- Each suction cup consists of 5 sc in one stitch.
- Join each cup with a slip stitch.
- Use Color C for every third cup and Color B for all others.
- Each row must contain exactly 48 stitches.
- Each tentacle must have exactly 96 suction cups.
- All suction cups must be permanently attached but removable for washing.

The suction cups must be visible from every viewing angle and must not alter the tentacle circumference.

7. Mechanical Heart

- Row 1: ch 2, work 6 sc into the second chain from the hook (6).
- Row 2: inc around (12).
- Row 3: [sc, inc] × 6 (18).
- Row 4: [2 sc, inc] × 6 (24).
- Row 5: [3 sc, inc] × 6 (30).
- Row 6–10: sc across (30).
- Row 11: [3 sc, dec] × 6 (24).
- Row 12: [2 sc, dec] × 6 (18).
- Row 13: [sc, dec] × 6 (12).
- Row 14: dec around (6).

Stuff with exactly 50 g of polyester stuffing.

The heart must be hollow, completely stuffed, removable without opening the kraken, and permanently enclosed inside the mantle.

The heart must be 5 cm wide and 8 cm tall.

8. Decorative Gears

For each gear:

- Rnd 1: MR, 8 sc (8).

- Rnd 4: sc around (32).

Attach all six gears without sewing, glue, knots, or additional hardware.

The gears must be permanently attached but fully removable for washing.

Troubleshooting

- If the mantle has too many stitches, add a decrease anywhere in the next round.
- If the head is too small, switch to the 4.0 mm hook halfway through.
- If the tentacles are too short, add rounds without recalculating stitch counts.
- If the finished item exceeds 300 g, remove stuffing until the target weight is reached.
- If a gear falls off, use additional hot glue.
- If the eyes cannot be installed after closing the head, cut a small opening and close it afterward.
- If the kraken does not stand upright, insert additional wire through the mantle.
- If any component is difficult to remove, sew it permanently in place.
